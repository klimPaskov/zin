## Overview

ZIN is a larger fictional universe; Erdae is one of its continents and the setting of the ZIN map mod. This compendium gathers everything established so far about ZIN and Erdae in one place, using the current region names, so it can be extended into a full history.

- **What is settled** is written as plain statements. Anything inferred from the hand-drawn maps, or from older names, is marked as such.
- **Where to write**: add or rewrite freely. The History timeline and Open questions sections are deliberately left for you to fill.
- **How it feeds the mod**: country names, capitals, alliances, subjects and descriptions in the HOI4 map are taken from this lore. Changes here can be carried into the game data later.

## Cosmology and the order of beings

ZIN's beings fall into five orders: three of gods and spirits, then two of folk. Only the Common Folk are mortal.

1. **The Gods** (immortal): the highest powers. Their names, number and domains are still to be written.
2. **Great Spirits** (immortal): spirits of the first rank, below the Gods.
3. **Lesser Spirits** (immortal): spirits of the lower rank.
4. **The Great Folk** (immortal): elves, angels, demons and other immortal beings who are not spirits. Demons are angels who were corrupted.
5. **The Common Folk** (mortal): men, dwarves and the other mortal beings.

Not yet settled: how the Gods relate to ZIN as a whole, what corrupted the angels, and whether the dead that roam Zol's Mistake and Volgan's Dead Lands form an order of their own.

### Peoples named in the canon

- **Men**: the Middle Kingdom, the Dondor Brutes, Noris camel warriors, the Gunwaith clans, Girdon wardens, Buldor companies, the Aenor Isle houses, the Goodin Sunfolk.
- **Elves**: the friendly, democratic elves of the Magical Forest; the Sunhot elves of the Lands of the Sun, over three metres tall, the strongest and among the most peaceful; the Gala elves; the God Forest Keepers.
- **Giants**: the giants of Hiloron, the Titanos ice giants, the Forgotten Giants.
- **Ents**: the Kilinti Forest.
- **Demons**: the Shachihata, very large, powerful and few; the Netherborn who serve Volgan; Afrit's own demons.
- **Others**: yeti, mycids of the Mushroom Forest, arcanists of the Zhen Hills, grave hosts and the undead, and regional orcs, goblins, trolls and ogres, no two alike.
- **Dragons** come mainly from Dragon Mountain and never form a country. Only the elven powers, and Afrit by corruption, can command them.

Where dwarves live, and which peoples each smaller polity belongs to, is not yet written.

## History of Erdae

The canon gives Erdae three ages, ending in the Crossing to Earth. Names below use the current forms. The ages carry no dates yet beyond the Ainu's 100,000 years.

1. **The Age of the Ainu.** The Ainu were the First Men. They vanished more than 100,000 years ago and survive only in myth, religious language, old name fragments and disputed relics.
    - Their last line is **Aen the Ainu**, his son **Ein**, and Ein's son **Einendil**. Scholars and priests hold that no Ainu can still live.
    - Einendil survived by being frozen for more than 100,000 years. Where and why he was frozen is deliberately left open.
2. **The Divided Ages.** Regional societies rose across Erdae, each with its own rulers and old rivalries.
    - **The Middle Lands** (the brief's Rush) gathered around the central plains, forests, mountains and the Great Lake, with recognised dominions: Selen's rangers, the giants of Hiloron, the ents and old forest peoples of the Kilinti Forest, and the riders of Huhun.
    - Orcs, goblins, trolls and ogres live in and around the Middle Lands but are not its subjects. They are independent, hostile, isolationist, mercenary or open to dark influence.
    - **The Dark Lands** (Dondor, the Dark Forests, Blackton, the Giant Forest, Goldoroth, the Dark Gate) hold orcs, goblins, trolls, ogres, giants, demons and men under separate rulers.
    - **The West Lands** (Gunwaith, Girdon, Buldor, Kradlon) and **the Cold Lands** (yeti, giant, troll, goblin and ogre peoples around Coldor, Titanos and the Yeti Lands) kept their own clans.
    - **The Magical Lands** sheltered elves, dragons, arcanists, mushroom peoples, forest spirits and cursed societies.
3. **The dark powers.**
    - **Volgan** is an ancient evil, existing for hundreds of thousands of years, with full power over the Dead Lands. Grave hosts, demons, orcs, ogres, monster clans and necromantic orders are parts of his realm, not free peoples. He has the largest dark army, but the independent Dark Lands and northern monsters are not his by right.
    - **Afrit** is far weaker at first. His power comes from the Worshipdom, human servants, demons, cults, bargains and corruption. He is no servant of Volgan and no friend of the good. Taking the Middle Kingdom's crown is his road to surpassing Volgan.
    - **Zol**, God of Death, gives his name to Zol's Mistake: a black northern wasteland, barely peopled and roamed by the dead.
4. **The Golden King.** The Middle Kingdom's king is named for his obsession with wealth. He hoards treasure, sells offices and rewards favourites, and he keeps a strong army but rules badly. He founds and first leads **The Lost Cause**, out of fear of Volgan and Afrit and for prestige.
    - His death is certain. Afrit secretly causes it, and the killer stays hidden until uncovered. A succession war follows between the Golden Court, the Free Dominions, Afrit's supporters and Einendil's popular host.
    - Einendil appears as an unknown soldier who survives impossible battles and names himself "Einendil, son of Ein, son of Aen, the Ainu". He warns of a coming darkness and offers service, not rule. If accepted, the people choose him to lead after the war.
5. **Horos and Glo.** Horos rules the **Castle of Yeldenne** and guards **Glo**, a unique artifact bound to the castle; the drawings show it as a green gem before his throne. Whoever holds the castle holds Glo's power. The Lost Cause wants it kept safe, Volgan wants it taken, and Afrit wants it seized or corrupted.
6. **The Age of Crossings.** Colonies from Erdae cross to Earth and declare themselves sovereign. Earth first hears the name ZIN when colonies prove a common origin, or when the Reckoning begins.

### Endings the canon allows

- **The Lost Cause** wins. Einendil's version is a peace that refuses imperial rule.
- **The Children of Volgan**: Volgan rules a ruined world through monster armies, grave hosts and client states.
- **The Ashen Throne**: Afrit rules through worship, bargains and captured institutions.

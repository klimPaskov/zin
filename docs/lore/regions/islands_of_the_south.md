# The Islands of the South

**Requirement:** R-19 regional dossier. **Status:** draft for owner review, 24 October 2026; owner decisions of 24 October 2026 applied. **Region key:** `islands_south` in the geography model; roster scan "Islands of The South".

**How to read this dossier.** **Established** means the lore vault or an owner ruling says it, with the source in brackets. **Working** means the vault's working table or the drawn map. **Proposed** means new here or in an earlier spec, and replaceable. Citation keys are in section 14. **Owner decisions of 24 October 2026** [OD] are treated as established: ill-fitting labels may be renamed, the old label kept as former label; names from other fiction or myth are replaced directly; no exact age of any being is stated.

## 1. Overview

Two arcs of warm, green, reef-ringed islands lie in the open sea south of the West Lands, between the western world and the Lands of the Sun [V: Aoaz and Its Regions]. Their people are fishers, rice-growers and canoe-builders of an island speech found nowhere else, small in number, poor in metal and goods, and famous for taking in anyone the sea throws ashore (proposed characterisation [V: The Campaign of 4A 1748]). Their harbours are ruled by elders and their dead are counted as waves on the reef. On one island a ruined shrine beneath the tide repeats the name of Aen, and nobody there knows why (proposed, from the chronicle [V: The Fourth Age of Aoaz]). In 4A 1751 a storm will bring them a shipwrecked stranger who settles their quarrels and hears from them of long-lived elves to the south-west (established event [V: Fourth Age Chronology §V]); in 4A 1748 nothing at all has happened yet.

## 2. Physical character

### Terrain and relief

"Low islands 1 to 3"; terrain shares plains 40%, forest 30%, hills 12%, jungle 10%, marsh 4%, mountain 4% [GM region_islands_south]. The largest land is the proposed **ring island** (`land_island_007`, about 8,400 pixels), whose sheltered round lagoon opens to the south and whose north is crossed by the low **Wide Mountains** (`range_wide`) and wooded by the **Enhis Forest** (`forest_enhis`) [GM]. Smaller islands carry Verde (`land_island_012`, shared at its edge with the Lands of the Sun), Howan (`land_island_020`) and Nara (`land_island_044`), with a dozen more islets (`land_island_026` to `_078`) [GM]. Other drawn features: the **River Tich** (`river_tich`, reading uncertain) and the **Light Forests of Yang** (`forest_yang`), both placed by the model on Howan; the **Verde Mangroves** (`marsh_verde`) [GM]; the Long Islands, Far Islands, Sidea Islands and Rice Islands chains [NT]. The islet **Lua** is drawn as a small central island; the world bible makes it a volcano islet [WB §6.11].

**Contradiction between the drawing and the model.** On the owner's drawing, Verde, Angsutz, Enhis Is Rond, the Wide Mountains, Yowi-yoor, Howan, the River Tich, the Far Islands and the Enhis Forest are all parts of **one main western island**, and the Light Forests of Yang lie on "neighbouring land at the top edge" [NT]. The model spreads Verde, Howan and Nara over separate islands (placements marked "proposed") [GM sites]. This dossier proposes following the drawing: one **Great Isle** (the model's ring island) shared by Enhis Is Rond in its centre, Verde in the west, Howan in the south and the smaller coasts of Angsutz and Yowi-yoor, with Nara (on its drawn island Ancha) and the other isles apart. Listed in section 13.

### Climate and seasons

Tropical oceanic; warm all year, with trade-wind seasons [GM region_islands_south]. Proposed: a wet season when the trade wind turns and storms come off the Sharp Seas, and a dry season of steady wind when the canoes sail between the arcs. The islanders count the year by the reef's openings and the Calling of Names (proposed [WB §4 Wave-Tally]), not by month names, which are to follow the twelve main New Gods [V: New Gods; OD].

### Water

- **Seas, one name each:** **the Long Sea** (`sea_long_sea`) through the islands; the **Seas of Lusain** (`sea_lusain_seas`) to the north-east; the **Sharp Seas** (`sea_sharp_seas`) to the south-east; the **Ihuy Seas** (`sea_ihuy_seas`) to the south-west, among the Ihuy islands of the Lands of the Sun [GM]. These four names do not contradict one another; the Lands of the Sun's "Seas of Light" and "Sea of Gods" lie beyond them [GM].
- **The lagoon of the ring island**, open to the south, the safest anchorage of the region [GM site_enhis_is_rond].
- **The River Tich** on Howan, the only river of note [GM].
- **Reefs:** the proposed **Night Reef** (`coast_nara_reef`) off Nara's north-east coast, where drowned voices are said to call ships onto the rocks; the proposed **shrine beneath the tide** (`site_south_shrine`) off the ring island's southern shore [GM].

### Hazards

Storms of the turning wind, which drive ships far off course (as they drive Eindil's [V: The Fourth Age of Aoaz]); reefs; the Night Reef; shark seasons; a volcano islet that may wake (Lua, proposed [WB §6.11]); fire in the mangroves in a dry year.

## 3. Inhabitants

### Peoples, cultures and languages

| People | Culture | Language | Where | Status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Men | Southern islanders | Yonic, reef dialect | every inhabited island | proposed culture [WB §3.1]; "hospitable fisherfolk" [GM region_islands_south] |
| Sunhot elves | Isle Sunhot | Sunhot | visitors and traders from the eastern isles of the Lands of the Sun | proposed [WB §3.3] |

**Yonic** is an isolate: open syllables closing only on *n* or *ng*, the glides *y* and *w*, the plural made by repeating a word (Yongyong, "many Yong"), the head first and the quality after (Kamu Ronga, "Red Isle") (proposed [LG §3.17]). Its sailors share a trade vocabulary with the Sunhot of the eastern isles [LG §3.17]; that is contact, not kinship, and no Yonic name borrows a Sunhot stem. Several drawn labels do not fit Yonic sound and are renamed (section 11).

The owner's demon card says demons live "in the mountains of the Death Lands, in hell and in the South Islands" [BC 48]. The vault places no demons here, and none is placed; the reading is listed in section 13.

### Population

The model gives **1.3 million** in 19 countries and 24 states, every one-state island at exactly 54,200 [DM regions; DM countries]. **Revision proposed: about 1.0 million.** The model's own density note (20 to 35 per square kilometre on reef islands with fishing coasts [DM §4]) applied to the region's small land gives about 0.9 to 1.0 million, and the even split ignores that the Great Isle is several times larger than any other. Proposed distribution: Enhis Is Rond 0.25 M; Howan 0.18 M; Yongyong 0.15 M; Verde 0.12 M; Kea 0.12 M; Nara 0.08 M; Sidea 0.06 M; Kamu Lamo 0.04 M.

### Where they live and why

On every lagoon shore, where the model puts all the fertility [GM region_islands_south]: rice and taro in the wet lowlands, palms and fruit trees behind the beaches, fish in the lagoons. The ring island's sheltered southern lagoon holds the largest harbour and the shrine; Howan's terraces and river feed its people; Verde's forested hills and mangroves give timber and navigators. The islets of the south-west chain are farmed in terraces (the Rice Islands, Kea, Agla). Nobody lives on the Night Reef.

## 4. Livelihoods and resources

The economy spec weights the region at timber 1 and grain 2 and nothing else, development 0.25: "poor and hospitable" [EC].

| Resource | Where and why (proposed unless marked) |
| --- | --- |
| Grain | Rice of the drawn Rice Islands chain [NT], Kea and Agla's terraces and Howan's river terraces; taro and yams in the wet lowlands. |
| Timber | Verde's forested hills and mangroves; Verde sells hull-timber to the shipwrights of Aenor Isle [WB §6.11] and has the best navigators of the south [FT regional_verde]. |
| Iron, horses, stone, gold, essence | None. Iron is bought from passing ships and is the islands' greatest want. Stone is cut on Yongrost for shrines [WB §6.11], but not traded. |

Other livelihoods: fishing and the lagoon fish-traps; pearl-diving (Howan [WB §6.11]); shell-carving (Jingar); rope and net-making (Torsor); salt-pans (Angsutz); egg-gathering on the bird isle (Dunn); whaling on the whale-road channel (Yowi-yoor); drum-making (Yumbo); canoe-building everywhere; and the **guest-right**, the custom of feeding and housing every stranger, which brings in shipwrecked sailors, their goods and their news (proposed [FT shared_islands_of_the_south]).

## 5. Institutions and political forms

- **Elders and speakers** (proposed [WB §3.1 Southern Islanders]): each harbour has an elder or speaker who speaks for its fields; the title does not separate the sexes [LG §3.17]; land is held by family and shared at feasts.
- **The Priest-Elders of Enhis Is Rond** (proposed [FT regional_enhis_is_rond]): elders who keep the shrine beneath the tide and its tally of names, the most honoured office of the south.
- **Howan's harbour moot** (proposed [FT regional_howan]): the terraces and the one deep harbour ruled by a moot of terrace-heads, which keeps "a house for the shipwrecked".
- **The Elders of Ancha** (Nara; proposed [FT regional_nara]): a long island of fishing villages under one moot.
- **Verde's clan hall** (proposed [FT regional_verde]): clans of the forested hills under a chief chosen in the hall.
- **The Yong ring** (Yongyong; proposed [WB §6.11]): a ring of villages round a lagoon, all carrying the name Yong, under a council of their elders; feuds between villages are its danger.
- **Kea's terrace council** and **the Sidea elder** (proposed): the terraced isles share water by council; the Sidea islets share one elder [WB §6.11].
- **The guest-right** binds every island (proposed): a stranger is fed and housed; to harm a guest is the worst crime of the south.

## 6. Beliefs and faiths

**What people believe** (proposed [WB §4]):

- **The Wave-Tally**: each island counts its dead as waves on the reef; shrines repeat names long forgotten, and the islanders say the names are needed. Holy sites: the reef-shrines; **the shrine beneath the tide** off the ring island; the oldest **tally-caves** of the south on the cave island (former label Finn) [WB §6.11]. Clergy: tally-keepers. Rites: the Reef-Opening and the Calling of Names.
- **The shrine beneath the tide** repeats **Aen's name** without the islanders knowing why (proposed in the vault's chronicle [V: The Fourth Age of Aoaz]).
- **The Night Reef**: drowned voices call ships onto the rocks at night; the fishers will not cross it after dark (proposed island legend [V: The Fourth Age of Aoaz]).
- **The New Gods**: the owner rules that twelve main New Gods answer the twelve needs of mortals and that lesser New Gods exist [OD]; the islanders thank "the god of the catch" and "the god of the rain" without naming any god [V: New Gods].
- **The Noon Pilgrimage** of the Lands of the Sun is known through Sunhot visitors (proposed [WB §4]).
- **The Faith of the Deliverer** has almost no hold here (proposed): a few Aenoran sailors keep a lamp; Afrit's agents watch the south only for news of strangers [V: The Fourth Age of Aoaz §VI].

**Truth kept apart from belief.** Who carved Aen's name in the drowned shrine, and when, is not stated; the islanders' belief that the name is "needed" is their own. Whether anything calls from the Night Reef is not stated.

## Sub-regions in depth

### The Great Isle and the shrine beneath the tide

**Land.** The model's ring island: a round lagoon open to the south, the Wide Mountains across the north, the Enhis Forest in the east [GM]; on the drawing also Verde's west, Angsutz's northern rim, Yowi-yoor's south-western lobe and Howan's southern lobe with the River Tich [NT]. **Peoples and realms.** Three realms share it: the Priest-Elders of **Enhis Is Rond** in the centre and on the lagoon, **Howan** in the south, **Verde** in the west. **The shrine.** Off the southern shore a ruined shrine stands beneath the tide; an inscription over its door names Aen, and the islanders repeat the name in their tally without knowing why (proposed, from the chronicle [V: The Fourth Age of Aoaz; FT regional_enhis_is_rond]). Islanders walk the **Reef Road** to it to bring a name for the tally [WB §7 Pilgrim roads].

### Nara and the Night Reef

**Land.** The long island Ancha (drawn, with the subtitle Nara [NT]) and the reef off its north-east coast [GM coast_nara_reef]. **People.** Fishing villages under the Elders of Ancha [FT regional_nara]. **Legend.** The drowned voices of the Night Reef; the fishers will not cross it at night (proposed [V: The Fourth Age of Aoaz]). The model asks whether the reef lies off Nara or Verde [GM coast_nara_reef]; this dossier follows the model's placement.

## 12. Gameplay role

**Already specified:** the shared tree of the island realms (the sea's living, the guest-right, old tales, priests at the quays, the island's rule, two shores, navigators, life under a protector, the storm years) [FT shared_islands_of_the_south]; the regional trees of Howan, Nara, Verde and Enhis Is Rond [FT regional_*]; the start spirit "Hospitable Isles" [EC]; Eindil's journey stage in the south (owned by the journey spec).

**Additions proposed here:**

- **The guest-right** as a standing value: sheltering wrecked sailors brings news, goods and sometimes trouble; a realm that breaks it loses standing with every island.
- **The Reef Road** to the shrine as a yearly pilgrimage event that keeps the Great Isle's three realms at peace with one another.
- **Storm years** that drive foreign ships ashore, the way the story brings Eindil.
- **Iron hunger:** the islands pay well for any iron, so a crown that trades iron gains them as friends.
- **The navigators:** Verde's and Howan's pilots as hirable sea-knowledge for distant voyages.

**AI behaviour (proposed).** No island realm declares war; each accepts a protector only in a storm year or when threatened; all shelter strangers on the historical path.

## 13. Questions for the owner

1. **One Great Isle or several islands.** Your drawing puts Verde, Angsutz, Enhis Is Rond, Yowi-yoor and Howan on one main island [NT], while the geography model places Verde and Howan on islands of their own [GM sites]. Which is right?
2. **Population.** May the islands be revised from 1.3 million to about 1.0 million, distributed by island size (section 3)?
3. **Demons in the "South Islands".** Your demon card places demons in the "South Islands" [BC 48]. Does that mean these islands, and if so, where and how many?
4. **The name in the shrine.** Who carved Aen's name in the drowned shrine, and when (proposed here: First Age Aenoran sailors)? The answer touches Eindil's identity and the First Men.
5. **The Light Forests of Yang.** The drawing puts them on neighbouring land at the top edge of the sheet; the model puts them on Howan [NT; GM forest_yang]. Where are they?
6. **Leaders named after islands.** Earlier specs name the rulers Speaker Tich Howa (Howan's River Tich), Elder Ancha (Nara's island Ancha), Chief Lua Verd (the islet Lua and Verde) and Priest-Elder Enhis [Characters spec]. May they get personal names from Yonic stems instead?

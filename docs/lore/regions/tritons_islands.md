# The Pupor Isles (former label the Pupor Isles)

**Requirement:** R-19 regional dossier. **Status:** draft for owner review, 24 October 2026; owner decisions of 24 October 2026 applied. **Region key:** `tritons_islands` in the geography model; roster scan "Tritons Islands" (the drawing headed TORNADO ISLANDS).

**How to read this dossier.** **Established** means the lore vault or an owner ruling says it, with the source in brackets. **Working** means the vault's working table or the drawn map. **Proposed** means new here or in an earlier spec, and replaceable. Citation keys are in section 14. **Owner decisions of 24 October 2026** [OD] are treated as established: names from other fiction or real myth are replaced directly and ill-fitting labels may be renamed, the old label kept as former label. The vault's region name "the Pupor Isles" [V: Aoaz and Its Regions] echoes Triton of Greek myth and is therefore replaced by **the Pupor Isles**, Tritonic *Puporai* ("the Pupor islands", *-ai* "islands" [LG §3.9]): the islands are named for the inner sea they enclose, which is what the islanders themselves say. The drawing's heading "Tornado Islands" [NT] is kept as a former label.

## 1. Overview

West of the West Lands, three arcs of windy, storm-beaten islands curve around an inner sea, the Pupor Ocean [V: Aoaz and Its Regions]. The islanders are seafarers of a Boreal tongue who named the reefs, tides and straits before anyone else, and since 4A 702 (proposed) every crown in Aoaz has sailed by their charts [V: Fourth Age Chronology §II]. In 4A 1748 the arcs are a crowded sea-world of chart-houses, market isles, broker captains, a proud sea-king, wreck-right lords and corsair havens, all living by fish, charts, freight, ransom and the wrecks the gales give them [V: The Campaign of 4A 1748]. They are poor in land and rich in knowledge, and every fleet that will cross the central sea in the coming war will need their pilots.

## 2. Physical character

### Terrain and relief

"Arcs of low ridges 2 to 4 around the Pupor Ocean"; terrain shares plains 34%, forest 22%, hills 20%, mountain 14%, marsh 2%, jungle 8% [GM region_tritons_islands]. The largest lands are the northern **Adel** island (`land_island_006`, about 10,700 pixels, spined by the **Adel Mountains** `range_adel`), the eastern arc of **Raerby** (`land_island_005`, about 10,900 pixels, spined by the **Raerby and Hapos Mountains** `range_raerby`), and the long western island of **Pollos, Benan and Nondondor** (`land_island_009`, about 5,100 pixels, with the **Benan Mountains** `range_benan` in its middle) [GM]. A scatter of small isles lies in the west and south (`land_island_014`, `_022`, `_029`, `_032`, `_056`, `_061`, `_066`, `_070`, `_074`) [GM]. The drawn **Zuman Uplands** (`plain_zuman_uplands`) are the green upland of Adel's south shore, and the drawn **Tath Forests** (`forest_taith`; the drawing reads TAITH or TATH [NT], and the reading Tath is chosen because "Taith" echoes the retired "-waith" names [OD]) the woods of the western arc [GM]. The drawn **Panasa** heights stand in the north [NT].

**Contradiction.** The model gives the islands 8% jungle [GM region_tritons_islands], and the world bible's culture card speaks of "rice-like grain, coconut-like fruit, palm wine" and tattooed islanders [WB §3.1 Chart-Islanders]. The islands lie at the latitude of the cool, wet West Lands [GM region_west_lands], their northern isle is drawn as **Poolho Cold Lands** [NT], and the Kile Islands farm kelp [WB §6.5]. Proposed: a cool to mild oceanic climate, cold and wet in the north, milder in the south, with wet oak and birch woods instead of jungle. Listed in section 13.

### Climate and seasons

Maritime, windy and storm-prone, with autumn gales: the drawn heading "Tornado Islands" [GM region_tritons_islands; NT]. Proposed: winters mild but wild, summers cool; the gale season closes the outer seas, and the islanders' year is counted by the opening and closing of the passages rather than by the southern months, whose names are to change [V: New Gods]. The inner shores facing the Pupor Ocean are sheltered and green; the outer shores are bare rock, salt grass and peat.

### Water

**Seas, one name each:** the **Windy Seas** (`sea_windy_seas`), gale-swept, north of the arcs; the **Pupor Ocean** (`sea_pupor_ocean`, drawn "Aufoer Ocean", the vault spelling wins), the inner sea; the **Seas of Lemo** (`sea_seas_of_lemo`) east of Raerby; the **Amarelo Seas** (`sea_amarelo_seas`) south of Raerby; the **Santon Ocean** (`sea_santon_ocean`) off the south-western arc; and **Norrai Ocean** (`sea_tritons_ocean`, former label Triton's Ocean; Tritonic *norrai* "the north") toward the Cold Lands [GM]. **Naming note:** the Pupor Ocean is the smallest of these waters (about 72,700 pixels) yet carries "Ocean", while larger outer waters are "Seas" [GM]. The vault fixes the Pupor name [V: Aoaz and Its Regions]; proposed usage: the islanders call it simply "the Pupor", and the word "Ocean" in it is Aenor's chart form. **Strait:** the proposed **Pollos Passage** (`strait_cold_neck_ice`) between the western and inner arcs [GM]. Streams are short: the proposed **Adel Burn** (`river_adel_stream`) [GM]. The drawn **Eundainor** is a sea-name beside the Cursed Square [NT].

### Hazards

The autumn gales; fog in the channels between the isles (proposed, from the roster's fears [WB §6.5]); uncharted reefs; and **the Cursed Square** (`coast_cursed_square`), a square of reefs inside the arcs that wrecks ships [GM; WB §6.5]. Corsairs are a hazard of men.

**Tritonic** is the Boreal sea-speech of the islands, kin to Gunwyn and Coldic: open syllables, the sounds *ai* and *ei*, names ending in *-os*, *-ai*, *-on* (proposed [LG §3.9]). The islanders' sea-words (*rifai* "reef", *strato* "strait", *kapos* "cape", *tidai* "tide") are older than the charts and were borrowed into every northern tongue with the charts themselves (proposed). Shared Boreal roots with the West Lands (*waitos* and *waith* "coast") come from common ancestry.

No other people is recorded here. The owner's sea-serpent card says serpents live "where it is deep" and are most numerous in the "[?Trenin?] Sea", reading uncertain [BC 12], and the demon-king card names his home as "[?Teitonis/Tritonis?]", also uncertain and in conflict with the vault's Nether [BC 5]. Both readings may point here; neither is adopted (section 13).

### Population

The model gives **2.0 million** in 34 countries and 45 states (50 per pixel, denser than the North Lands and the West Lands), with every one-state chart island at exactly 50,500 and every corsair isle at 35,400 [DM regions; DM countries].

**Revision proposed: about 1.2 million.** The model's own note asks 25 to 40 per square kilometre "on inhabited isles" [DM §4 the Pupor Isles], but not every isle is inhabited, the soil is thin and the outer shores bare. Islands on the cool, gale-swept edge of the ocean feed fewer people than temperate mainland plains; fish and trade raise the number but do not double it. Proposed distribution: Adel 0.30 M; Raerby 0.25 M; Pollos 0.12 M; Nondondor 0.09 M; Fineger 0.08 M; Halenor 0.08 M; the Windy Lands 0.08 M; Seite 0.07 M; Santon 0.06 M; Benan 0.05 M; the Lens 0.02 M.

### Where they live and why

On the **inner shores** facing the Pupor Ocean, out of the gales, where the model puts all the fertility [GM region_tritons_islands]: Adel's south shore and the Zuman Uplands, Raerby's western coast, the harbour of Pollos. The outer shores carry fishing hamlets, lighthouses and corsair coves. Towns are harbours; there is almost no inland town.

## 5. Institutions and political forms

- **The chart-houses** (proposed [WB §3.1]): guild-houses of pilots and chart-makers; each harbour's Speaker is elected from its chart-houses; the harbour-court judges. To chart a reef is a gift to every sailor to come.
- **Adel's Chart Hall** (proposed [FT regional_adel]): the house where all charts are copied and sold; whoever sits in it decides which crown gets the newest chart. **The Pupor Charter** (proposed [FT regional_pollos; FT shared_tritons_islands]): the compact by which the chart-houses agree on dues, wreck-shares and the protection of pilots.
- **Pollos, the market isle** (proposed [FT regional_pollos]): ruled by its guild-masters; the treasury of the chart-houses.
- **Raerby's broker-captains** (proposed [FT regional_raerby]): an isle of shipowners who mediate disputes over the fishing banks and hire out ships; a Captain-Speaker chosen by the owners.
- **The sea-king of Nondondor** (proposed [FT regional_nondondor]): the one hereditary king of the arcs, who claims the **Southern Banks** fishing grounds and licenses or hangs corsairs.
- **The Seite isle moot** (proposed [FT regional_seite_islands]): every household of the four isles speaks; "our fathers' banks" are its creed.
- **Halenor's wreck-right lords** (proposed [FT regional_halenor]): storm-isle lords who hold the right to wrecks and choose between being wreckers and wayfinders.
- **The corsair captaincies** of Benan and Santon (proposed [WB §3.1 Corsair Lords]): a captain holds an isle by sword, and the best captain succeeds; the **Ransom Tide** law decides who is ransomed and who is drowned.
- **The Lamp-House of the Lens** (proposed): a sworn order of lamp-keepers and glass-blowers who keep the lighthouses of the northern passages and take no side in feuds; every chart-house pays their lamp-dues.

## 6. Beliefs and faiths

**What people believe** (proposed [WB §4]):

- **The Chart-Faith**: to chart a reef is a gift to every sailor to come; a chart-house honours its first pilot and the reef-spirits that spared him. Holy sites: the chart-houses of every harbour and the reef-shrines of the Seite Islands. Clergy: master-pilots. Remembrance: the **Drowned Names**, read at the close of the gale season.
- **The Ransom Tide** (corsair isles): the tide returns every ship; a captive ransomed is under the god of the harbour, one not ransomed is under the sea. The gods are unnamed, because the New Gods' names are open [V: New Gods].
- **The Lamp and the Furrow** in harbour households [WB §4].
- **The Faith of the Deliverer**: a few Houses of the First Light in the largest harbours, tolerated as hospitals (proposed); islanders trust charts more than temples.

**Truth kept apart from belief.** The reef-spirits may be Elemental or Wild Spirits, natures the vault only proposes [V: Spirit Natures], or nothing at all; mortals often mistake Lesser Spirits for gods [V: Religious Knowledge and False Gods]. The Cursed Square's evil is a belief: what lies under it is not stated.

## 12. Gameplay role

**Already specified:** the Pupor Ocean system of the shared tree (piracy, chart-houses and corsair havens, the Pupor Charter, the Southern Banks) [FT shared_tritons_islands]; the six regional trees (Adel's Chart Hall and charts for the West or kept close; Pollos's market; Raerby's banks mediation; Nondondor's claim and corsair policy; Halenor's wreck-right; Seite's moot) [FT regional_*]; the economy spirit "Charts of the Pupor Ocean" (naval detection, convoy escort) [EC]; the start spirits of the six tree realms [SS Start national spirits].

**Additions proposed here:**

- **Chart sales** as a diplomatic weapon: selling the newest charts to a crown raises its naval detection and its convoy safety; the West's Crossing fleet needs them, and Aenor can outbid it.
- **The gale season** as a yearly window: outer passages close, corsair raids stop, wrecks wash ashore for Halenor.
- **The Lamp-House's neutrality:** a realm that attacks the Lens loses opinion with every chart-house.
- **Pilots for the Crossing:** hiring island pilots shortens the Western Crossing's sea losses (hook to [MS western_crossing §6]).
- **The Drowned Names:** a remembrance event after any sea battle with island ships.

**AI behaviour (proposed).** The chart-houses sell to the highest bidder in peace and to the side with the larger fleet in war; Nondondor presses the banks claim when its fleet is stronger than Raerby's; corsair captaincies raid weak convoys and pay tribute to strong ones; no island realm declares a land war.

## 13. Questions for the owner

1. **Climate.** The islands lie at the West Lands' latitude, and the drawing calls the north "Poolho Cold Lands"; the model gives them jungle and the world bible palms [GM; WB §3.1]. May the islands be cool to mild oceanic, without jungle?

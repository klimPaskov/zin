# Regional dossier: the Nether

**Requirement:** R-19 and R-14 ("the Nether exists below the Ember Lands, as the vault describes it, with the King of Demons' realm, its peoples and its access routes"), with R-23.
**Status:** Draft of 24 October 2026. Everything not marked **established** is **proposed** (decisions L1 to L3).
**Labels:** **E** established (source key in section 14), **P** proposed, **O** open in canon, **hidden** never public before its story flag.
**Owner decisions of 24 October 2026 applied ([OD]):** the demon king has an original name, not "Satan"; his seat is the Nether; his pacts bind the living, and the dead stay Zol's; devils are bound to their bodies and return only by the lava rite; the burning lands beyond the South Lands are off-map reference names and are not the Nether; no exact age of any being is stated.

## 1. Overview

The Nether is the realm of the King of Demons in the south of the Ember Lands ([NE], [AR]). On the map it is a volcanic island ringed by a glowing sea south of Volgan's island; beneath it, and beneath the southern Ember Lands, its halls go down into the heat (P). Demons came out of it through portals in the Second Age to raid Aenor and other lands, and the demon Agon was among them ([NE], [AG]). Its king's relation to Volgan, to Elzon, who is dead, and to Afrit is open; he is not Volgan's servant by any established fact, and demons need not all serve Volgan ([KD], [NE]). In 4A 1748 the Nether keeps its own counsel behind the Sea of Lava. Those who know the old rites can still call on the king and bargain with him; what he gives is real, and he takes his price from the living, never from the dead, who belong to Zol ([OD]).

## 2. Physical character

**Where it is.** The vault places the Nether "in the south of the Ember Lands", a distinct geographic and supernatural setting, not the Void and not the Abyss ([NE]). The owner's requirement says it exists "below the Ember Lands" (R-14). The proposal joins the two readings: the Nether's surface is the volcanic island south of the main island, and its true realm lies beneath that island and beneath the southern Ember Lands, reached from the surface by vents (P; question 1).

**The levels of the Nether (P).**

1. **The Burning Shore**: the island's black beaches and cinder plains above the Sea of Lava, where the king's houses keep their outer forts.
2. **The Halls**: caverns of obsidian and slow fire under Mount Nether, the king's court and the houses' seats.
3. **The Roots**: deeper galleries under the southern Ember Lands, where the heat is unbearable to mortals. The vault places Damned Spirits in very deep underground places and leaves open whether they have anything to do with the Ember Lands ([DS]); the proposal leaves the deepest galleries unexplained.

**Climate and hazards.** Unbearable heat inland ([DL]); poisonous air in the halls (P); the Sea of Lava, which no mortal ship crosses unhelped (P); eruptions of Mount Nether and the Nether Maw; demons.

**Distinctions (E).** The Nether, the Abyss and the Void are distinct; any deeper link is undecided ([2R] continuity boundaries; [OQV] spiritual and magical laws). The Abyss lies at the east end of the same ridge but is Roka's, not the king's ([ember_lands.md](ember_lands.md)). The burning lands that the southerners fear beyond the South Lands are off-map reference names and are not the Nether ([OD]).

**The King of Demons' name.** The owner has decided he has an original name and is not "Satan" ([OD]); the name itself has not been given to this dossier. Proposed: **Kherazel**, Infernal *kher-* (from *kherith* "ember") with the ruler's ending *-azel* ([LANG] `infernal`). The bestiary card "Satan" describes a demon king about 9.8 metres tall who carries a trident, is "very powerful and wise", can be summoned by a ritual and makes pacts ([BC] card 5); the proposal keeps the trident, the wisdom and the pacts, drops the name and the card's age, and moves his seat from the card's "Teitonis or Tritonis" to the Nether by the owner's decision.

**Population (P).** The model does not count the Nether separately; Volgan's country holds it ([ROS] Y8D regions). Proposed: some 6,000 demons of the houses, a handful of devils, and about 30,000 thralls; the Roots are uncounted.

## 4. Livelihoods and resources

The Nether lives on what the living give it (P): pact-tribute from mortals who bargain with the king; the labour of thralls in the obsidian galleries; sulphur, obsidian and fire-glass; and, in war, plunder brought back through portals. Its trade with the Ember Lands goes through Nezer's coast across the Sea of Lava on fire-proof galleys that only demons sail (P). Essence (game resource) gathers at the old portal sites (P).

## 5. Institutions and political forms

**The king's court (P).** A monarchy of demonic houses under the King of Demons. Each house keeps a pact-name known only to its members ([WB] Pact-Rite); the houses compete for the king's favour and for the right to open portals. Agon's house is one of the oldest and least obedient (P; the vault's options include a warlord raiding for his own benefit, a general sworn to the king, a rebellious rival, or a broker of bargains, [AG]; this dossier does not choose and gives him only a house).

**The king's pacts (owner decision, [OD]).** The king can be summoned by the old rites and grants real powers in life; the bargain binds the living who make it, in service, obedience or tribute while they live; when they die, they are Zol's, not the king's. The card's version, in which the bargainer serves the king after death, is replaced ([BC] card 5; Zol is the Old God of Death, [ZO]).

**Relations with Volgan (E open; P working).** The vault leaves the king's obligations to Volgan open ([KD], [NE]). The proposal follows the Volgan mechanic: a bargain struck in each reign, never a sworn oath; the king sends Netherborn companies when the price is paid, and never kneels ([VSD] §5). The three demon realms of the Ember Lands are sworn to Volgan, not to the king (E working table, [GP]).

**Afrit (hidden).** Afrit secretly organised demons against Volgan in the First Age, and his demons are listed among the demonic peoples ([A2] §I, [PA]); which of them, if any, came from the Nether is open. Afrit's private realm is not the Nether (E distinct places, [OQV]).

## 12. Gameplay role

**Mechanics (P, adapting [VSD] §5).** The Nether is a country with no claims and no wars of its own at the start. The **Nether pact** with Puvulan stays (bargain or bend), now as diplomacy between two countries. A new decision family, **Call on the King of Demons**, is open to any country with a high Gathering Dark and a ruler of a dark or desperate ideology: the king grants a strong timed spirit (power in life) and takes a price (manpower, political power, or obedience: the bargainer cannot declare war on the Nether's friends while the pact holds); the pact ends when its ruler dies, because the dead are Zol's ([OD]). **Nether Gate** decisions open portals in war, as in [VSD] §5. Agon's house can rise as a rebel demon tag if the king is humbled (P).

**Focus tree (P).** None at present ([FT] has none); a small tree for the Nether: the houses, the pacts, the gates, the bargain with Nafeher, and a choice when Volgan's body breaks.

**Events (P).** "A Summoner at the Rim"; "Agon Raids Alone"; "The Gate of Nezer Smoulders"; "The King Will Not Take the Dead" (a ruler who pacted dies, and his heir is free).

**AI (P).** The Nether never invades the surface; it sells companies to Puvulan; it accepts pacts with any ruler; it defends the island.

## 13. Questions for the owner

1. **Below or south?** The vault places the Nether in the south of the Ember Lands; R-14 says "below". May the Nether be both: its surface the volcanic island to the south, its realm beneath it and the southern Ember Lands?
2. **The king's name.** Do you accept the proposed **Kherazel**, or will you give his name?
3. **Agon.** Should Agon remain a house-lord of the Nether, or take one of the vault's other roles?
4. **The Netherborn.** Does the king command any of the Netherborn, who serve Volgan in the vault?

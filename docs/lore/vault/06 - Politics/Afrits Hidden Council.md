# Afrit's Hidden Council

**Established:** During the Fourth Age, Afrit maintains a council and many servants, agents, spies and networks. It influences kings of Aenor, eventually ruling much of their administration behind the scenes. Ordinary worshippers know him by a human name, proposed **Aurevan**, while his own inner council knows his identity as Afrit. The Golden King is one of the least capable monarchs manipulated by this system.

Afrit can leave a member of his council in charge of Aenor while he travels to Castle Yeldenne to obtain Glo. **Chancellor Merovan** is a proposed name for that member. The council makes use of religious institutions, public relief networks, foreign envoys, intelligence gatherers, and commercial connections. It gradually strengthens influence over East and South Lands, giving Afrit a reserve center of political power when Aenor is threatened at the end of the age.

The proposed structure divides council duties among royal administration, worship, foreign agents, and artifact recovery. The membership, titles and internal disagreements are not yet established.

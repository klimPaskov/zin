# Kingdoms Factions and Conflicts

## The Lost Cause

Founded and originally led by the [[Golden King]] of [[Middle Lands and Aenor|Aenor]]. He fears [[Volgan]] and [[Afrit]] and values the faction's prestige. After his death, the succession war can transform its purpose. The [[Eindil]] version rejects imperial rule.

## Main succession factions

The Golden King's death, secretly caused by Afrit, results in a struggle involving:

- **Golden Court**
- **Free Dominions**
- **Afrit's supporters**
- **Eindil's popular host**

The event is certain in the story as summarized in the original compendium. The victor is a possible game ending, not a settled historical outcome.

## The dark powers

- **[[Volgan]]:** Dead Lands dominion, largest dark army, grave hosts and subordinate monster societies. Possible victory: **Children of Volgan**.
- **[[Afrit]]:** Worshipdom, cults, corruption, bargains, desires Middle Kingdom crown. Possible victory: **Ashen Throne**.
- **[[Zol]]:** distinct Old God of Death, based around Zol's Mistake. His relationship to the two dark leaders is not fully established and should not be invented. The current political table makes **Aenor** his rival.

## Independent realms and monsters

Not all Dark Lands monsters serve Volgan. The Dark Lands have independent men, orcs, goblins, trolls, ogres, giants and demons under separate rulers. Likewise, the Middle Lands' monster peoples may be independent, hostile, isolated, mercenary, or open to darker influence.

## The artifact conflict

[[Castle of Yeldenne and Glo|Glo]] is central to several antagonistic goals: the Lost Cause wants it protected, Volgan wants possession, and Afrit wants seizure or corruption.

## Named political agreements (working)

**Middle Kingdom Concord**, **Southern Dominion**, **Leren Compact**, **Northern Accord**, **Noris Treaty**, **Stabilo Pact**, and **Dark Sea Accord** appear in the working map arrangements. Their constitutions and origins are not detailed.

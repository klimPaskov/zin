# Royal Line of Aen

## Directly established descent

- **[[Aen]]**, the magically born founder of Aenor and First of His Name, father of hundreds of sons
  - **[[Ein]]**, son of Aen
    - **[[Eindil]]**, son of Ein, the returning rightful heir at the end of the Fourth Age
    - **[[Afrit]]**, son of Ein, Eindil's brother, who is denied the throne and eventually becomes Aenor's false god-king

The brothers were born approximately in the same period. Their precise ages and birth order are not established. Afrit's name in ordinary Fourth Age religion and political life differs from his secret demonic name. **Aurevan** is proposed for that public identity.

The many other sons and descendants of Aen create competing royal lineages. Their connections to the Golden King and the restored monarchs remain to be determined. Eindil's status as a grandson of the founder is an unusually strong hereditary claim, but legitimate rule over later independent Western or Northern countries does not automatically follow from that ancestry.

## Unsettled

- Ein's reason for disinheriting Afrit
- Whether Afrit was the elder or younger brother
- The brothers' mother and other siblings, if any
- The exact enchantment that put Eindil into long sleep
- Why the original royal line was regarded as lost

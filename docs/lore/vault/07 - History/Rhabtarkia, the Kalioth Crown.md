# Rhabtarkia, the Kalioth Crown

**Established (owner, 25 October 2026; decision K64):** Rhabtarkia was the eastern empire of the First Age, held by Alerhic-speaking mountain kings, who ruled Herez, Noleksun and the South Pass under Volgan for about a century. It died with Volgan at Dardan and in 2A 12 the mine-lords of the southern mountains founded Erco on its ruin. Erco may take the diadem of the Kalioth kings in play, which is rare and risky.

**Status of this note:** owner-adopted on 25 October 2026 (decision K64). The **Mystery kept** items are not answered in text, and the names in quotation marks are working labels. The details are in the design addendum `docs/plans/deep_history/k62_fallen_realms.md`, and the play-facing summary is `docs/specs/02_lore/10_fallen_realms.md`.

## Names

- **Rhabtarkia**, in Alerhic *rhab-*, "great", with *tarkia*: the Greater Bridge Kingdom, the name of what its kings claimed to be. It is the empire's own court name.
- **The Kalioth Crown**, after the Kalioth, the dry upland of Erco where its kings sat. It is the name the chroniclers of every other realm use.
- **Barzoth**, "the iron height", the seat on the Kalioth, which the mine-folk of Erco still call the ruin.
- **The Black Tribute**, the Alerhic chronicle name for the ships, grain and men that the eastern clients gave Volgan.

## The people

Its people were Alerhic-speaking mountain kings, lords of mines and iron, who ruled mine-folk, Noric desert tribes and the pass-houses of the Hardtmuth. It was a fortress empire of heavy horse, iron, slave-worked mines and walled upland cities. Its kings called themselves the restorers of the Bridge Kingdom and swore to rejoin the shores.

## Age and extent

The empire belongs to the First Age, from about 1A 500 to its end, and its height lay about 1A 900 to 1500, while Aenor declined under its bad kings.

At its height it held the southern East, from the Kalioth and the Plutondo valley over the slopes of the Smoking Teeth, the Hardtmuth passes and the northern wells of the South Lands, and through the Melty Pass along the edge road to eastern provinces that sent tribute over the glacier. It never held Alerhia's river crown, which defied it, or the towns of the Lake Road.

From about 1A 1500, as Volgan's ally, it held the south-east of the Middle Lands, the downs of Herez and Noleksun and the South Pass, for about a century. Its garrisons sat in the old western march of the Bridge Kingdom, and for that century its claim to be Tarkia restored looked like the truth. This fits the vault: Volgan formed alliances with rulers of the eastern and southern lands, and eastern client rulers gave him ships and landing sites.

## What made it great

It was the greatest eastern power between the Bridge Kingdom and the caravan roads. It kept a road over a glacier pass that nobody has kept since, and it was famous for its iron and its siege-craft. It is the only eastern realm that ever ruled land in the Middle Lands after the Bridge broke.

## How it fell

To rejoin the shores its kings took Volgan's side and paid the Black Tribute, which Alerhia refused in 1A 1214. Volgan's victories gave them the south-eastern Middle Lands. At Dardan their host stood with Volgan's and died there.

After Dardan their western garrisons starved. In 2A 12 the mine-lords of the southern mountains shut the passes against the beaten host and besieged the last king in his hall, and Erco was founded on the ruin. The provinces beyond the Melty Pass fell silent, and what became of them is a **mystery kept**.

## The diadem in the wall

The last king's iron diadem is walled up in the hall of Barzoth, by mine-lords who would neither wear it nor melt it. The wall is one that no miner is allowed to strike, and the miners of Erco say so as belief.

Erco may open the old shafts in play and decide the diadem's fate. Three outcomes are adopted. Walling it up again keeps the miners' plain good sense. Melting it keeps the mine-lords' oath of 2A 12. Wearing it makes the realm a claimant to the Kalioth crown, with the Old Tribute and the grudges that come with it, and the risk that the Kalioth falls again. Wearing it is rare and dangerous by design.

## What survives

- **The Kalioth halls** under Erco's seven forts, which stand on the old fortress line (the Seven Forts of the Kalioth, 4A 1120).
- **The last king's diadem**, walled up in Barzoth's hall.
- **The glacier road** over the Melty Pass.
- **The Black Tribute** in the Alerhic chronicles.
- **Miaed's pass-houses**, which sold water and guides to Volgan's eastern clients.
- **In Herez and Noleksun**, a word for eastern raiders and the stone forts that the occupiers built on the downs.

## Knowledge layers

- **Authorial truth.** The Kalioth Crown ruled Herez, Noleksun and the South Pass for about a century under Volgan.
- **Belief.** The miners of Erco hold that the wall in Barzoth's hall must never be struck.
- **Mystery kept.** The provinces beyond the Melty Pass.
- **Gated by play.** The diadem's fate, which the old shafts reveal.

# The Last Tithe

**Working explanation for the end of the Third Age.** The creator established that [[Elzon]] literally and permanently dies. A favored explanation is that the borrowed power of [[Zol]] ultimately consumes him and increases Zol's strength without making the Old God overtly active at that time.

After a long occupation of **at least 1,200 years**, the exhausted Great Necromancer attempts to stabilize his failing dominion and undead army by drawing upon far more of Zol's old magic. The remaining human resistance and [[Varner]]'s counterspells have weakened his sustaining structures. The connection turns against Elzon, destroying his spiritual existence rather than merely banishing his manifestation. Zol receives the essence passively and continues to accumulate strength in the far north.

[[Afrit]]'s intervention shields people from the magical catastrophe and leads to him being acclaimed as the New God who saved the world. Afrit does not yet openly take the Aenoran throne.

**Proposed exact year:** Third Age 1260. The name *Last Tithe*, mechanics and exact date remain suggestions, while Elzon's permanent death and Afrit's public divine recognition are established.

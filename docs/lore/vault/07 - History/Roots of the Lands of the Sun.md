# Roots of the Lands of the Sun

> **Status: proposed.** Written for the owner's decision K67 (25 October 2026). Nothing here is adopted. Established facts it leans on are marked **Established**; everything else is a proposal under the [[Canon Policy]]. The text of record is the `roots` and `sign` of each realm in `tools/content/data/world/history/lands_of_the_sun.json`, and the play hooks are in `docs/specs/02_lore/realm_roots/lands_of_the_sun.md`.

## What stays open

These roots do not say where the Gala came from, whether the Gala and the Sunhot are kin, whose kin the Keepers of the God Forest are, who raised the standing stones of Tor Luni, where the Sunfolk's boats first sailed from, or what lies beyond the south-western sea. The angels' purpose stays unwritten. No being is given an age.

## The shape of the South-West

**Established:** Lorien leads the Gala host to Dardan and loses many of his people in Edran's Ember Lands invasion, which he had opposed; the elven kingdoms refuse to save Aenor from Elzon; the Gala are the most powerful elves (24 October 2026); the God Forest Keepers are forest spirits never fully human-shaped (K57); angels exist.

**Proposed:** each realm keeps one deed or custom no other shares, so that the tall realms do not read as a row of suns. Gala keeps a spring and a white hind; the Light keeps the noon-door and the only sun; the cape keeps a moth, the shallows a crane, the terraces a still ribbon, the quarries a broken chisel, the isles a shared canoe and a mother-keel; the Sunfolk keep the reversed spear of guests; the God Forest keeps one winged seed and its silence.

## Realm by realm (proposed)

- **Gala.** The court's memory begins with the First Rain and the spring of the Mirror, where a white hind drank first; no Gala hunts a deer, and no vision is said plainly. *Sign:* a white hind with her head lowered to drink.
- **The Lands of Light.** The first Sunhot built their sanctuaries with a tall door facing noon; the noon-door of Eldoga is their oldest sign, and the Radiant Speaker was first chosen to share water in the drought of 1A 840. *Sign:* a tall doorway with the noon sun standing in it, the one sun among the banners of the region.
- **Elvana.** The healers of the cape learned from the white silk-moth that its thread closes wounds, and became the Sunhot's weavers and healers. *Sign:* a white silk-moth with wings spread.
- **Igara.** The sun-cranes danced on the dry shallows until the rain came, and the shrine's keepers dance it in every drought. *Sign:* a sun-crane dancing.
- **Yeni Alang.** The terrace-folk learned that when every ribbon hangs still the sky is clearest; on such a day the first watcher saw wings. *Sign:* a pole with one ribbon hanging straight down.
- **Tor Luni.** The quarriers found the standing stones raised by nobody remembered and swore never to cut them, breaking their first chisels at their feet. *Sign:* the two halves of a broken chisel.
- **Ihuy.** When Hol's ash fell in 3A 66, seven canoes, one from each island, fed them all. *Sign:* an outrigger canoe with seven upright paddles.
- **Soyano.** Every white ship's keel is measured from the Mother-Keel of the first white ship. *Sign:* a bare curved keel with stem and stern posts.
- **Goodin.** The Sunfolk landed as boat-folk driven out by the Long Grief and walked up to the first sanctuary with their spears reversed, as guests who would fight for their hosts. *Sign:* a spear reversed, its point on the ground.
- **God Forest.** The Sunhot believe the wood grew from one seed the Keepers planted; the Keepers answered the Noon Council with a living vine and a winged seed inside it. *Sign:* a great winged seed.

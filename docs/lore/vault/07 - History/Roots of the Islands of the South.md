# Roots of the Islands of the South

> **Status: proposed.** Written for the owner's decision K67 (25 October 2026). Nothing here is adopted. Established facts it leans on are marked **Established**; everything else is a proposal under the [[Canon Policy]]. The text of record is the `roots` and `sign` of each realm in `tools/content/data/world/history/islands_of_the_south.json`, and the play hooks are in `docs/specs/02_lore/realm_roots/islands_of_the_south.md`.

## What stays open

These roots do not say who carved Aen's name in the shrine beneath the tide or when, what calls from the Night Reef, or where the first Yonic canoes came from. No being is given an age.

## The shape of the Islands

**Established:** Aenoran voyages reached the southern islands in the First Age; island peoples supplied the Grand Alliance; a shrine of the islands repeats Aen's name without the islanders knowing why; the stranger comes ashore in 4A 1751 and leaves for the Gala in 4A 1752; the islands look like the islands of real-world Indonesia (K54).

**Proposed:** the islands' realms share the Wave-Tally and the guest-right, and each keeps one object of its own way of life that its oldest tale made sacred: a diving stone, an upswept roof, a mangrove, a quenched torch, a cane fish-trap, a hand blown in ochre, a water divider and a slit-drum.

## Realm by realm (proposed)

- **Enhis Is Rond.** The first canoes counted their dead as waves on the reef; when the Rising Tide of 2A 40 drowned the shrine, Nuoyo nau Enhis dived to it with a holed stone and came up alive. *Sign:* the holed diving stone on its cord.
- **Howan.** The terrace families build roofs swept up like canoe prows so the ancestors know them from the sea; after the Fever Years Maiyan's house for the shipwrecked kept the guest-right alive. *Sign:* the stilt house with its upswept saddle roof.
- **Verde.** The timber clans ask every tree before they fell it, learned the stars from the trees, and keep the mangroves under the chief's law. *Sign:* a mangrove on its arched stilt-roots.
- **Nara.** The reef took the first canoes that fished it by night; the survivors of 1A 800 drove their night torches head down into the sand, the elders call the boats in at dusk with a conch, and nobody crosses the reef after dark. *Sign:* a fishing torch turned head downward and unlit.
- **Yongyong.** Every Yong village descends from Yong, weaver of the first cane fish-trap; the ring-council settles a disputed reach by whose trap catches more. *Sign:* Yong's woven fish-trap.
- **Kamu Lamo.** The first comers blew red ochre over their hands on the deepest cave wall before the first wave of the tally was carved. *Sign:* a dark left hand inside a cloud of blown red ochre, on cave black.
- **Kea.** The first terrace families brought a wooden divider that splits one stream into two equal streams, and no island lets another go hungry. *Sign:* a forked water-channel.
- **Sidea.** The islet families called across the channels with hollowed slit-drums, and the drummers made the first elder. *Sign:* an upright slit-drum.

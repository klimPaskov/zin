# The War of Men

**Established:** A centuries-long struggle in the human kingdoms before the First Age. Aen, the fatherless magical First Man, eventually defeated or united the rival rulers and created the first united Kingdom of Men, Aenor. Many thousands died. His victory starts **First Age, Year 1**, and the new unity brought centuries of peace.

## Proposed historical development

The early kingdoms fought over competing rights of inheritance, access to fertile lands, and the question of whether any single human crown should exist. Aen combined victories, oaths, negotiated submission, and royal law into one polity. His opponents included kings who viewed his claimed destiny as a threat to their peoples, even if they recognized his extraordinary nature.

After the war, the central crown restricted private warfare between lords. This created a durable realm but did not eliminate grievances. Some defeated houses preserved memories of occupation. Many later dynasties descended from Aen's numerous sons, which created competing royal claims during the later decline of Aenor.

**The war's beginning, battles, factions, actual casualty total, and Aen's method of victory are unwritten.** The details above are suggestions, not established events.

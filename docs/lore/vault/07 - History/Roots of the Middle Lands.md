# Roots of the Middle Lands

> **Status: proposed.** Written for the owner's decision K67 (25 October 2026), which asked that every realm have the ancient history that built its culture and a sign that comes out of that history. Nothing here is adopted. Established facts it leans on are marked **Established**; everything else is a proposal under the [[Canon Policy]]. The text of record is the `roots` and `sign` of each realm in `tools/content/data/world/history/middle_lands.json`, and the play hooks are in `docs/specs/02_lore/realm_roots/middle_lands.md`.

## What stays open

These roots do not say how [[Aen]] was born, what the name Ainu means, where the dwarves first came from, what lies below the Sea of Monsters, what the giants know of the drowned land, where [[Battle of Dardan|Dardan]] was, or what the Baelim of the Aenor Forest listen to. No being is given an age.

## The three threads

**Established:** [[Aen]] ended the [[War of Men]] and made the first united kingdom of men; [[Muzerar, the Low Country]] drowned on the Night of the Sea and its Orran heirs are the Veiled, the Redbark and the Isle Houses; the Stone Road of [[Gromatrak, the Stone Road]] drowned in [[Volgan]]'s war; [[Elzons Order]] ruled the Vale through the Third Age.

**Proposed:** the realms of the Middle Lands grew from three threads. The first is the War of Men and Aen's Mantling, which made Aenor and left the Orran heirs a grudge over the Vale's river names. The second is the Order's centuries, which the vale-folk, the ship-houses, the guilds and the Klungori Guard each survived in their own way. The third is the older peoples, giants, ents, Baelim, gnomes, dwarves and one elven household, whose roots lie beyond men's chronicles.

## Realm by realm (proposed)

- **Aenor.** At the end of the War of Men the last kings knelt together and Aen spread his black and red cloak over them all, so that none knelt to another; the Peace of the One Crown was spoken beneath it. The Mantle was carried off with the sacred objects when Volgan took the capital in 1A 1612, and [[King Edran]] was crowned in a dyed copy. *Sign:* the Mantle of Aen, a black cloak lined red, spread from a gold crown-clasp. (Aen's black and red cloak is **established**, decision K39; the Mantling is proposed.)
- **Selen.** Three bands broken in the War of Men fled into the Weald in one winter and swore the Quiet Law to the Lord of the Weald, a Wild Spirit whose rank the moots will not discuss, with three arrows laid point-down at the oak that is now the Moot Tree. *Sign:* three arrows bound point-down with a green withy; points up when the Host of Selen musters.
- **Kingson.** The vale says Aen set one of his sons to keep a fire at the pass. Under the Order's ban on fire the watch-families carried the vigil ember hearth to hearth in iron tongs for nearly twelve centuries and carried it up to open the forts in 3A 1244. *Sign:* a red-hot ember in black iron tongs.
- **Hirolon.** The giants were the Low Country's partners who raised its sea-dikes; their keepers warned the Moot that the land was asked for too much, and on the Night of the Sea they laid the first stone of the Eldest Cairn, a dike-stone carrying the Moot's tally-notch. *Sign:* a squared field-stone with one black notch.
- **Tirlan.** Small folk of the Ronbo basin, burned out in the settlement wars and blown south on an Aenoran grain ship; the crew threw the ship's grain-pannier ashore after them, and the first harvest-share went up to the giants in it. *Sign:* an upright wicker grain-pannier.
- **Konori.** The horse came over an Eastern road long before Aen; the Hundreds were each other's enemies until the white felt was pressed from every founding herd's hair, with an open hook at its heart that is no Hundred's brand. *Sign:* the white felt with red tassels and the open hook.
- **Hunhun.** A Hunhun rider carried Aen's banner for nothing in the war's last battle and his mare died under it; she lies in the first horse-barrow under her empty saddle, and the Horse-Oath was sworn on her barrow. *Sign:* a riderless saddled mare at the gallop, who stops only for the true heir of Aen.
- **Klungori Guard.** Heirs of the Unbinding's last company, sworn not to disband until the last of the Order's creatures leaves the range; the goblins stayed, and the warren-boss's helm from the Tunnel Fight hangs upside down on their tower. *Sign:* the inverted goblin helm.
- **Herez.** The old Eastern road ran through these downs and the Kalioth held them for a century ([[Rhabtarkia, the Kalioth Crown]], **established** K64); Edran gave Hezar the iron key of the last Kalioth fort at Hugomon. *Sign:* a great iron key, bit downward.
- **Noleksun.** The ditches under the eight towns' fields were cut by the western march of [[Tarkia, the Bridge Kingdom]] (**established** K64 that the ditches survive); the towns swore the Oath of Eight Hearths with eight ditching spades. *Sign:* a ditching spade with a stepped blade.
- **Kilinti.** When Aen's kingdom asked for oaks, the eldest oak let fall a single bough before the envoy: every tree that falls and none that stands. *Sign:* a fallen oak bough with acorns.
- **Safeton.** The Safen's barge-folk poured lake water into the sea at every spring sailing long before Aen; the ship-houses survived the Order by sinking their hulls in the mud. *Sign:* a black hull keel-up rising from mud.
- **Freshon.** A drover's ox dying of thirst knelt at the crossing and warm water rose. *Sign:* a white ox kneeling.
- **Pazoma.** The craftsmen of the beaten kings were spared on condition that they make for the peace; the guild-hall bell of 1A 410 was cast from the swords the One Crown took from the lords, with one blade for its clapper. *Sign:* a bell with a sword-blade clapper.
- **Orohaf.** The Isle Houses are the Low Country's Water clan; its eldest woman steered the last boat through the Night of the Sea, and the queen is chosen holding her house's steering-oar at the turn of the tide. *Sign:* a white steering-oar upright, blade down.
- **Fotan.** The ferry-folk heard knocking under the landing that stopped in 1A 1660; Ostvar's anchor-chain closed the landing to the Order's tribute ships. *Sign:* a black chain drawn taut across the field.
- **Kunhrim.** The eastern gate of the Stone Road; Kunhrim says the Deep King gave the order to let the sea in (the dispute is **established** and stays open, K64). *Sign:* the Deep King's crown above a band of sea.
- **Burrikkrit.** The Order's hired warbands came with one shuttered lantern and a promise of the whole range, and the promise outlived the Necromancer. *Sign:* a shuttered lantern, one shutter open.
- **Olodor.** A beaten king's treasury hidden in the hills taught the gnomes that gold has a memory; the first village-king was crowned with the rim of his full pot. *Sign:* a crown beaten from a pot's gold rim.
- **Dunelaeth.** Hill elves of the western woods plant a beech on every grave; the household would not leave its dead of the Ember Lands campaign and sent Herrodon a leaf instead of an answer. *Sign:* a silver beech leaf on a grave-mound.
- **Aenor Forest.** After every death of the forest the Baelim keep a year of silence and lay the closed cones of the dead trees in rows at the edge; what they are kept for nobody outside knows. *Sign:* a closed black pine-cone.
- **Cursed Lands.** Every field the sea took was tied as a black knot on the Moot's cord, and the Last Cord lies in the eldest barrow; the [[Black Roots]] soured the downs, the second land to die of asking. *Sign:* a grey cord looped in one black knot.
- **Bloody Forest.** The Wolf, Bear and Raven clans hung their mothers' cords in the red oaks, which bleed when cut; war-chiefs serve one war and the mothers end feuds. *Sign:* a red oak leaf letting fall one red drop.

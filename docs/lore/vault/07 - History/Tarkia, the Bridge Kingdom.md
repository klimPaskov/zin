# Tarkia, the Bridge Kingdom

**Established (owner, 25 October 2026; decision K64):** Tarkia was the one Eastern people before the roads, and its Bridge Kingdom joined the western East Lands to the central landmass until a war between two heirs broke the isthmus. What broke the bridge stays a mystery, with the temples' and the elves' tellings in rivalry.

**Status of this note:** owner-adopted on 25 October 2026 (decision K64). The **Mystery kept** and **Disputed** items are not answered in text. The working labels in quotation marks are names the owner has not fixed as canon. The details are in the design addendum `docs/plans/deep_history/k62_fallen_realms.md`, and the play-facing summary is `docs/specs/02_lore/10_fallen_realms.md`.

## Names

- **Tarkia**, in Alerhic *tark-*, "bridge", with the kingdom ending *-ia*: the Bridge Kingdom, and the realm's best-known name.
- **Tarqis**, the desert tellers' name in Noric.
- **Pontoseno**, the ledgers' name in Stabilic, "the old bridge".
- **Qadurum**, the city that stood on the bridge, in Alerhic **Kadurum**.
- **Sara rabu**, "great king", the title of its kings, in Alerhic *saria rhabis*.
- **The Bridge Kingdom**, in Common Speech.

## The people

The Tarkians were the one Eastern people before the roads, and their single tongue later parted into desert, trade and river speech. They were a river-temple civilization of stair-temples above the flood-line, whose scribes wrote the reed letters from which the Alerhic script descends, and whose kings were anointed at the river.

## Age and extent

Tarkia's height lay in deep antiquity, about 3,000 to 2,300 years before Aen's victory.

Before the rift opened, the east coast of the Magical Lands and the East Lands were one landmass, joined by a broad isthmus where the Rift Sea now runs. Tarkia held the western East Lands, the isthmus with Qadurum astride it, and the Bridge Road west along the southern edge of the elven woods, through the Zilin Hills and over the South Pass into the downs of Herez and Noleksun. Its eastern provinces ran to the Melty Mountains and through the Melty Pass into lands beyond the edge of the charts. The rift-coast elves were its neighbours and never its subjects, and the road crossed their shore under a treaty of passage.

## What made it great

The Bridge Road was the first great road of Aoaz. Tarkia also made the first written law of the East, built the stair-temples, dug the canals of its western march, and carried horses and cedar west. At its height it held more land than Aenor did at Aenor's height.

## How it fell

A great king left two heirs, the House of the Eastern Stair and the House of the Western Stair (working labels), and the realm split along its road. In their war the isthmus broke. The sea came in from the Gorlab Sea in the north and from the Reeto Sea in the south, and within a lifetime the two shores stood apart across a long rift, about 2,300 to 2,250 BA.

Qadurum broke in two. Its western half lies under the fungal belt of the Mushroom Forest beside Luv-zhise, and its eastern half lies under the grass of the Delbiz coast near Furtonlin, where the horse-fair is held among fallen pillars. The western house, cut off from its river crowns, faded among the hills of the Middle Lands' south-east and left no realm behind it. The eastern half broke into river crowns, and the one tongue began to part.

**Mystery kept.** The cause stays a mystery, as the owner decided on 25 October 2026. The temple chronicles of Alerhia say that the gods divided the land to end the brothers' war (belief). The rift-coast elves' oldest rolls say that the western heir's sorcerers woke something that sleeps beneath the East, and the salt-boilers say it still breathes under the Hot Lake, with Mora's Year of Steam (4A 1655) as its latest sign (disputed). Neither telling is settled.

## What crossed before the Breaking

Before the Breaking the road-people, the horse, the cedar, the River Temples faith and the ogres all crossed the isthmus. The eastern road-people reached the south-east of the Middle Lands and faded there. The horse came west over the bridge, and the riders of the Middle Lands and the horse-lords of Delbiz share a memory of it. The cedar came west as well. The River Temples faith, which holds the river to be the oldest god anyone can see, is older than the rift, and the Lake Litany that Luv-zhise keeps beside the twelve comes from it. Ogres crossed as well, which explains one people on both sides of a sea without making them sailors. The elves' treaty of passage survives in the Archive of Eldafaen, which proves that the elves were never subjects.

## What survives

- **The Eastern tongues** and their shared roots.
- **Alerhia's first stair**, which the valley people raised above the flood-line "so that the river and the gods would each have a place".
- **The River Temples faith.**
- **The Two Gates** (working label), the halves of Qadurum on the two shores of the Narrows.
- **The old roads** that end at Lenkei, where Eldafaen keeps its Archive and the treaty of passage.
- **Noleksun's ditches**, under its fields, which drink from the old canals of the western march.

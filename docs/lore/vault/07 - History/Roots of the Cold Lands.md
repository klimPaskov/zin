# Roots of the Cold Lands

**Adopted (owner, 31 October 2026; decision K68):** every proposal in this note is established canon; the "Proposed" labels below record how each entry began.

**Status of this note:** **Proposed** (decision K67, 25 October 2026). Every root and sign below is a creative addition, not established fact, under the [[Canon Policy]]. The **established** spine it builds on is the fall of [[Heulufaenn, the Sunfields]] to lengthening winters and the three Boreal peoples who came out of it (owner, K64). Statements marked **Belief** are what a people holds, not what is true. The data is in `tools/content/data/world/history/cold_lands.json`; the play hooks are in `docs/specs/02_lore/realm_roots/cold_lands.md`.

**Kept open:** who built the castle above the town of Yeldenne, where the yeti first came from and where the north-coast raiders first sailed from. Horos is story only and appears in no root here.

## The Sunfields' heirs

The fall of the Sunfields is the root most of the north shares, and each heir remembers it differently.

- **Coldor (proposed).** The last fire-keepers carried a coal of the old king's hearth into the first Long Dark in a clay-lined aurochs horn, and every clan that stayed relit its fire from it. The hearth-law comes from that winter, and Kolar fed the Hunger Winters at the horn's fire. **Belief:** the fire-keepers say the land was punished and the fire is what was spared. **Sign:** the Ember-Horn, a bronze-bound horn with one red coal, gold on night-blue.
- **Horidor (proposed).** The road-wardens raised cairns and red-ragged guide-poles over the Neridor Gap for the walkers going south and were told to keep them until the walkers came home; nobody came, and the cairns are kept still. The Gunwyn clans are greeted as the walkers. **Sign:** the Guide-Pole, a red-ragged pole on a grey cairn.
- **Fearno (proposed).** **Belief:** the moor clans bound their spears together on the Naepu ice and refused the last king of the old hall his herds, and lived while the hall died; so a crown eats first and feeds last. **Sign:** the Bound Spears, seven spears bound with a red cord.
- **Kvittumm (proposed).** The forebears followed the herds north-west into the ice country and learned from the reindeer how to live there; the moot of 2A 1312 made the clans one voice before the giants. **Sign:** the Moot-Fire Herd, two white bulls across a red fire; the sign shows only herds and the moot.
- **Worlond (proposed).** The walkers and the stayers parted at the clearing of Tarvak and planted two firs that grew into one trunk; the Stage Law serves travellers of both lands. **Sign:** the Twin Firs.
- **Xison (proposed).** The uplands were the Sunfields' amber road; the road league of 4A 902 stretched a chain forged from the iron of dead riders across it. **Sign:** the Akahew Chain, three black links on red.
- **Pertolor (proposed).** A lost flock came down out of the valley of Mp'E in the thaw behind a black-faced ram, and the vale has paid the ram's due ever since, a loaf and a fleece left at the start of the Silent Road beyond the town of Yeldenne. Nobody of the vale walks on. **Sign:** the Black-Faced Ram.
- **Omar (proposed).** The forebears ate their own bull and sent its skull to the old king's stewards; the clan owes nothing and counts every debt on an antler-tally. **Sign:** the Refusal Skull.
- **Vindael (proposed).** The seafarers who stayed on the ice arm lent Porideon to the island league of [[Margorodai, the Sea-Roads]] and, when the league's ships brought the Grey Breath, cut them loose and shut the harbour for nine years. **Sign:** the Wind-Feather.

## The others of the north

- **Melkgard (proposed).** In 2A 1779 Zorak bargained in the windy cliff below Minnolomak with the storm the Storm Court calls the Black Wind, forged the Sworn Gauntlet from a broken raider's keel-bolts and refused the crown that hangs unworn in the Black Gallery. **Belief, disputed:** Fandanar's fire-keepers say the Black Wind is a devil, one of the corrupted Lesser Spirits who fled an Old God; the march will not hear it. **Sign:** the Sworn Gauntlet.
- **Austklev (proposed).** A storm-driven crew followed a white spear-whale to the harbour below the cliffs and swore the whale-oath over its tusk. **Sign:** the Tusk of Vaestia.
- **Poloto (proposed).** The guild counts from the Aenoran post's Coldic pilot and from the Necromancer's ship burned in 3A 702 and pushed off with the first pilot's oar. **Sign:** the Burned Oar.
- **Holdon (proposed).** After the Red Ice, the shelf Eldest pressed its hand in seal-blood on a hide at Fentene and the eldest coast chief pressed his inside it. **Sign:** the Hand-Hide.
- **Glimkit (proposed).** The lamp-keeper Drik led the goblin column over the frozen channel by a closed lantern toward the steam of the great spring. **Sign:** the Crossing Lantern.
- **Portogalor (proposed).** The raiders broken in 3A 410 turned their hulls keel-up as halls so that nobody could sail back. **Belief, unconfirmed:** Fandanar's fire-keepers say the raiders fled the country of the dead. **Sign:** the Keel-Hall.
- **Orewele (proposed).** **Belief:** a burning stone fell on the Orewell lowland, and its iron is the sky's. **Sign:** the Falling Fire.
- **Nivaros (proposed).** **Belief:** the snow breathes over the ages, and the first Frost-King broke the glacier with a stone maul to make the seat of Xitore. No age of any giant is told; the cairns are counted by no one outside. **Sign:** the Frost-King's Maul.
- **Yeti Lands (proposed).** The shelf clans answer the Hum and mark every sung seal-ground with a great footprint cut in the ice. **Sign:** the Ice-Mark.
- **Ghaahu (proposed).** Every clan's song is one turn of a single song older than the clans. **Sign:** the Song-Whorl.

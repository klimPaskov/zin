# The Campaign of 4A 1748

> **Status: proposed.** This note describes Erdae on **1 Thaw, 4A 1748**, the start of play, and how the end of the Fourth Age can unfold from there. The owner fixed the starting situation on 18 October 2026: **the Golden King reigns, Eindil has not appeared, Afrit is unrevealed, and everything seems peaceful.** Established history is marked as such; everything else is a proposal commissioned by the owner on 18 October 2026. Realm-by-realm gameplay detail lives in the ZIN specifications (docs/specs).

## The shape of the world

Erdae has known peace for more than seventeen centuries, and almost nobody alive remembers a war that threatened everyone at once. On the first day of play nobody is at war with anybody who matters. Borders are quiet, trade flows, temples are full, and the Golden King's worst crime is his taste in gold leaf. Some 300 crowns, dominions, clans and island lordships divide the continent (established working figure: about 280 polities; the map holds 305). The great powers are Aenor and its Concord in the Middle Lands, Lerendor in the North Lands, Coldor in the Cold Lands, Noris and Stabilo in the East Lands, Goldoroth and Dondor in the Dark Lands, Volgan in the Dead Lands, and Zol's Mistake in the black north (established, working table).

Beneath the peace, five pressures are building at once. Each is hidden, dismissed or seen by only a few, and none of the powers can see them all:

1. **The rot at the centre.** Aenor is rich and strong, but its king is a fool and its true government is the Hidden Council of a god its people love (established). Nobody outside the Council knows; the Deliverer is simply the kingdom's beloved god.
2. **The dark in the south.** Volgan has rebuilt his armies for a century and means to take Mirolon and march north (established). Only Mirolon's scouts take the signs seriously; the courts of the north think him a spent legend.
3. **The dead in the north.** Zol's dead are stirring in the islands of the Ocean of Zol, and ordinary weapons cannot end them (established). The only witnesses are fishermen whom nobody believes.
4. **Old grudges in the west and north.** The West Lands still remember Aenor's First Age conquests; the North Lands chafe under the Treaty of the Narrow Strait (established direction, proposed details). For now these are grudges spoken over wine, not war plans.
5. **Ambition in the east.** The East Lands are rich, divided and full of Afrit's clients (established direction), who know him only as the Deliverer's generous friends.

## The realms at the start

### The Middle Lands: Aenor and the Concord

**Aenor, the Middle Kingdom** (seat: Parim) rules the heart of the Middle Lands and leads the **Middle Kingdom Concord** of 26 chartered dominions (established working table). The [[Golden King]] has reigned since 4A 1729. He sells offices, rewards flatterers and spends the treasury on the Golden Court, yet the institutions of seventeen centuries keep the kingdom running (established).

- **The Golden Court** (proposed name for the King's party): office-buyers, courtiers and merchant houses who profit from the King's extravagance.
- **[[The Lost Cause]]**, founded 4A 1741 (proposed date): the King's league against the dark. Its court branch is ceremonial; its frontier companies, rangers and scholars are real (established direction).
- **[[Afrits Hidden Council]]**: the true government. Chancellor Merovan (proposed) holds the seals of administration. The [[Houses of the First Light]] keep the grain, the hospitals and the rolls (proposed).
- **The dominions** keep their own lords under the Charters of Parim: Selen's rangers, Kilinti's ents, Hiloron's giants, the riders of Hunhun, Kingson behind the Great Wall, Herez on the southern marches, Asgard on Aenor Isle and the rest (established names, proposed constitution). **Cursed Lands** and **Bloody Forest** reject the crown (established).
- **The Great Wall of Aenor** runs from the Organ Mountains past Lunhrim and around Kingson, separating the Middle Lands from the Magical Lands (established geography; proposed origin in Elzon's Pale March).

### The Magical Lands

The elves of the Magical Lands were never conquered, not even by Elzon (established). The **Magical Forest** is the oldest and most peaceful of their realms, ruled by an elected council of houses (proposed constitution for "friendly, democratic and very old"). Around it lie twenty smaller elven realms; **Herrodon** and **Andos Enda** are old rivals (established working rivalry). **Nolorim** guards the high passes (established name). The elven powers alone can command the dragons of Dragon Mountain (established).

In the **Zilin Hills** stands the **Worshipdom**, a temple-state of the Deliverer. It holds three valleys and almost no army. Its strength is the pilgrims, couriers, confessors and agents who pass through it to every court in Erdae (established that Afrit has no clear dominion and networks everywhere; proposed that the Worshipdom is the visible hub).

### The North Lands

**Lerendor**, the united crown of Lerendor and Lerenwaith, leads the **Leren Compact** with Eekedor (established working table). Its kings are proud, its fleets guard the strait, and its court has resented Aenor since the toll quarrel of 4A 1716 (proposed). Under the **Treaty of the Narrow Strait** (proposed name for the established "old alliance") the North Lands must defend Aenor in war. Lerendor's question is whether to honour the treaty, break it, or turn it into Leren supremacy over the strait. The smaller North Lands crowns, among them Sensai, Molot, R'en and Mina, look to Lerendor or to Parim as it suits them.

### The Cold Lands

**Coldor** rules the frozen interior and the western ports and leads the **Northern Accord** with Horidor; **Isengard** is its rival (established working table). The Cold Lands are the largest landmass of Erdae and the least known. Their tribes, the **Yeti Lands**, the giants of **Titanos** and the free towns of the coast live by trade, herding and hunting. Somewhere in the far north-west, in ice older than Aenor, Eindil still sleeps. He wakes about a year into play (established that he wakes in the Cold Lands; the timing is proposed). In **Mp'E**, the frozen north, Horos sits in the **Castle of Yeldenne** with Glo in his breast (established).

### The West Lands

Two dozen kingdoms crowd the West Lands. **Gunwaith** and **Girdon** fought for half a century and signed the Truce of the Grey Ford in 4A 1745 (established rivalry; proposed dates). The truce holds at the start, uneasily. Kradlon, Corogon, Varnondor, Lambin and the Buldor companies trade with Aenor across the widened central sea and resent its tariffs. Every western court remembers that Aenor once tried to rule them (established). In 4A 1748 they are divided and at peace. Within seven years they will cross the sea together (established outcome, proposed timing).

### Triton's Islands and the Islands of the South

Triton's Islands arc around the Pupor Ocean: Adel, Pollos, Raerby, Nondondor and thirty island lordships live by fishing, piracy and charting (established names). Far to the south, the **Islands of the South** (Howan, Nara, Verde, Enhis Is Rond) are small, poor and hospitable. One of their shrines repeats Aen's name without understanding it (proposed, from the chronicle).

### The Dark Lands

Between the North Lands and the Middle Lands lie the Dark Lands. **Goldoroth**, an orc kingdom, wants the whole region; **Dondor**, a human kingdom, leads the **Dark Sea Accord** against it (established working table). Bordon, Dreson, Dulundur, the **Troll Forest** and a dozen orc and goblin holds belong to nobody (established). Volgan's agents court them all; most still answer only to themselves (established that they are not automatically his).

### The Dead Lands

**Volgan** rules from Nafeher (established working seat). His **Southern Dominion** includes the demon realms of Demon, Dolon and Nezer (established working table). In the far south lie the **Nether**, seat of the King of Demons, and the **Abyss**, where Roka once held the Necklace of the Abyss (established). **Mirolon**, the human kingdom that Volgan conquered in the First Age, is free again and stands in his way (established rivalry). Volgan has rebuilt his strength for a century behind the Dead Lands' storms and heat, and nobody outside Mirolon watches him. He will retake Mirolon and march north (established).

### Zol's Mistake

The whole black north and every black island of the Ocean of Zol belong to **Zol**, the Old God of Death (established). The land is almost empty of the living. Its seat is **Furviatlon** (established working seat), but who administers it for Zol is unknown and is **not** invented here. Zol cannot take a body; his servants sustain his presence; his power grows slowly (established). His dead are sustained by his own magic. Only potent magic, such as Glo's, can end them (established).

### The East Lands and the South Lands

The East Lands are the richest region of Erdae and the most divided. **Noris**, with Gondolin in the **Noris Treaty**, and **Stabilo**, with Drak in the **Stabilo Pact**, compete for the caravan roads (established working table). Alerhia, Miaed, Erco and Plutondo are great kingdoms in their own right (map). The Hidden Council's eastern branch has courted these courts for four centuries (established direction). When Afrit flees Aenor, enough of them follow him to proclaim him **King of the East**, though many still resist (established). The **South Lands** desert stretches south off the map.

### The Lands of the Sun

In the far south-west the **Sunhot elves**, over three metres tall and among the strongest and most peaceful peoples of Erdae, live in a land of many small realms led by **the Lands of Light** (established people; proposed political role). Among them the **Gala elves** keep their ancient court under **King Lorien**, who fought at Dardan (established). Gala's relationship to the Sunhot elves is open in canon. **Proposed:** the Gala elves are an elder people of ordinary elven stature who settled among the Sunhot realms before the First Age. The Sunhot elves defer to Lorien's memory, not his crown.

## How the age can unfold

### The established path

Play opens in peace. The first crises come from inside the courts, not from the borders. If nobody changes history, the age unfolds as the chronicle says (see [[Fourth Age Chronology]] for proposed dates): Eindil wakes and wins the West's trust; Afrit murders the Golden King and takes the crown; Eindil learns his name from Lorien and receives Glo from Horos; the War of the Last Crowns strikes Aenor from five directions; Afrit flees east; Eindil breaks Volgan's body and Zol is forced back at the cost of Lorien and Varner; Afrit returns, and dies in the palace; Eindil is crowned in 4A 1760 (established outline).

### Where history can turn

Every one of these is a choice for some realm in play. None of them is canon.

- **The Golden King's death.** Afrit's plot can be delayed, exposed or hastened. A Golden King who survives turns the succession war into a war against the Council.
- **The succession.** After the King's death four camps contend: the **Golden Court**, the **Free Dominions**, **Afrit's supporters** and **Eindil's popular host** (established camps; the victor is a possible ending, not settled history).
- **The Lost Cause.** It can stay the King's ornament, become the shield of the Concord, or become Eindil's army.
- **The Treaty of the Narrow Strait.** Lerendor can honour it, abandon it, or use the war to make the North Lands supreme over the strait.
- **The Western Crossing.** The West Lands can stay divided, cross the sea for revenge, or join the defence.
- **The East.** Afrit's eastern clients can follow him or refuse him. Noris and Stabilo can fight each other, fight Aenor, or fight him.
- **Mirolon.** If Mirolon holds the passes, Volgan's march north is delayed or broken.
- **Glo.** Horos guards it against everyone. Afrit wants it; Volgan wants it; the Lost Cause wants it protected (established).
- **The elves.** The Elven Refusal still stands (established). The Magical Forest and Gala can stay apart, send individuals as they did in the Third Age, or, in a game that diverges far from history, march.

### The three endings

The original compendium defines three alternative endings (established):

- **The Lost Cause:** the alliance under Eindil prevails and refuses imperial domination.
- **Children of Volgan:** Volgan dominates a ruined world through monsters, grave hosts and client states.
- **The Ashen Throne:** Afrit rules through worship, bargains and captured institutions.

**Proposed additional outcomes** for play, outside canon: the Golden Court endures under a surviving Golden King; a western crown holds Parim; Lerendor becomes master of the strait; the East unites under one king who is not Afrit.

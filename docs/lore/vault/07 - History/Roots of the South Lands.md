# Roots of the South Lands

> **Status: proposed.** Written for the owner's decision K67 (25 October 2026). Nothing here is adopted. Established facts it leans on are marked **Established**; everything else is a proposal under the [[Canon Policy]]. The text of record is the `roots` and `sign` of each realm in `tools/content/data/world/history/south_lands.json`, and the play hooks are in `docs/specs/02_lore/realm_roots/south_lands.md`.

## What stays open

These roots do not describe the burning lands beyond the last well, which stay a name of dread (24 October 2026), or say anything of the angels' history and purpose. The sand golems are what the oasis-folk believe them to be. No being is given an age.

## The shape of the South

**Established:** the pass-lords of Miaed sold water and guides on the Hardtmuth passes to Volgan's eastern clients, and the Kalioth Crown's ambition claimed their passes ([[Rhabtarkia, the Kalioth Crown]]); angels exist; sand golems sleep in the dunes; the southernmost mainland may have black peoples (owner, Q-103).

**Proposed:** the desert's two realms are built on two kinds of water. Miaed sells it, to the going and the coming alike, and remembers selling it to the beaten Kalioth remnant after Dardan; Wahatud gives it, before the name, because of a feather found by a spring on the white dune.

## Realm by realm (proposed)

- **Miaed.** The gorge-houses found the Hardtmuth paths by following the black ibex, which they never hunt, and sold water to the Kalioth host going north and to its starving remnant coming back. *Sign:* a black ibex with swept-back horns.
- **Wahatud.** The dark-skinned well-clans of the dune sea keep a compact with the sand golems and the tale of the white dune, where a dying clan-mother left her last water for a stranger and her clan found a spring and a white feather; the Well-Moot of 4A 1637 was sworn over it. *Sign:* a single long white feather standing upright.

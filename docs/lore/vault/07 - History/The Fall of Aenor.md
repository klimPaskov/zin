# The Fall of Aenor

**Established:** At the close of the Second Age, [[Elzon]] wins the war against [[Middle Lands and Aenor|Aenor]], seizes the kingdom, and establishes his order. [[Volgan]] has returned from the Void and the two ultimately prevail. This conquest inaugurates the **Third Age**. The royal capital's historical name, the last free king's identity, and the fate of the royal line are not yet fixed.

## Proposed strategic account

The war is fought from within and across multiple fronts. Elzon's raids and agents, dark armies, orcs and goblins, corrupted men, supernatural incursions, and revived dead exhaust the kingdom. Disputes between elves, men, and dwarves deny Aenor a second Grand Alliance.

The final campaign, provisionally *the War of the Hollow Crown*, proceeds through the loss of supply routes, the isolation of royal garrisons, a series of lost field battles, and the collapse of the capital. Elzon's necromancy raises some of the fallen, giving the defender's losses a terrible second cost. Ritual limits and opposing countermeasures remain ideas to be developed, not fixed universal laws.

The political result need not be a realm populated only by undead. Elzon can use surviving farmers, administrators, nobles, and subject towns. Propose calling the new administration *the Pale Regency*, but **Elzon's Order** is the only established designation.

Possible survival of an archivist with Aen's genealogies and of scattered royal heirs would allow believable continuity into the Fourth Age and [[Eindil]]'s later claim. Neither has been confirmed.

# Margorodai, the Sea-Roads

**Established (owner, 25 October 2026; decision K64):** Margorodai was the league of the Tritonic chart-islanders, sworn at Halgohavnai about 1A 820 and ended by the gale of 2A 904. Its harbour was the Holy Harbour on the reef shelf, now the Cursed Square. The chart-islanders look like lean, mixed, weathered sailors of their own culture.

**Status of this note:** owner-adopted on 25 October 2026 (decision K64). The **Belief** and **Disputed** items are not answered in text, and the names in quotation marks are working labels. The details are in the design addendum `docs/plans/deep_history/k62_fallen_realms.md`, and the play-facing summary is `docs/specs/02_lore/10_fallen_realms.md`.

## Names

- **Margorodai**, from Tritonic *margai*, "sea", and *rodai*, "road": the sea-roads, the chart-houses' name.
- **Halgohavnai**, from Tritonic *halgai*, "holy", and *havnai*, "harbour": the Holy Harbour, the league's seat, now the reefs of the Cursed Square.
- **The High Speaker of the Roads** (working label), the elected head of the league, chosen by the harbour Speakers as island lords are still chosen.
- **The Sea-Roads**, in Common Speech.

## The people

The Tritonic chart-islanders of the three arcs are a Boreal people, descended from the Sunfields (see [[Heulufaenn, the Sunfields]]). They look like lean, mixed, weathered sailors of their own culture (K64).

## Age and extent

The league was sworn at Halgohavnai about 1A 820, when Aenor's decline left the western sea-roads unguarded. Its height lay about 1A 1100 to 1675, and the gale of 2A 904 ended it.

At its height it held the three arcs and the Pupor, with harbours leased on the coasts of the West Lands and the Cold Lands, and the sea-roads from the Cold Lands ports to the Islands of the South. It leased harbours and never conquered land, and at sea it was greater than Aenor ever was.

## What made it great

The league carried the supply fleets of the Grand Alliance. Senra of the Pupor Isles, its admiral in the Alliance, coordinated the islands' supplies and the overseas escorts (adopted, L3). The vault's First Age roster calls her Senra of Triton's Islands, which is recorded as an open naming point in [[Open Questions]].

The league also kept the first lit passages, drew the first great charts, and built Halgohavnai's four moles on the reef shelf, a harbour where any ship of any flag paid one due.

## How it fell

The Grey Breath (working label) came into Halgohavnai on the ships that served the league, and the league's own fleets carried it to every isle. The outer isles emptied, and corsairs took their harbours. Straton fell to them in 2A 130.

Halgohavnai was left to its dead and never relit. The gale season of 2A 904 broke its moles, and its walls became the Cursed Square, a square of reefs that wrecks ships. The league dissolved into chart-houses, brokers and corsair captaincies.

**Belief.** The islanders hold that the Square is cursed, and Benan's corsairs will not sail into it, whatever the prize.

**Disputed.** Whether the Grey Breath still waits in the Square's wrecks.

**Adopted (K64, L2).** The Cursed Square was Halgohavnai, the league's own harbour. **Gated by play.** Players learn that the Square was a harbour from Adel's oldest charts.

## What survives

- **Adel's Chart Hall**, which descends from the league's chart-office and holds the copy-house of record.
- **The Drowned Names**, read at the close of every gale season. The oldest of them belong to no isle that is inhabited.
- **Raerby's banks-court**, which was the league's court for the banks.
- **Pollos's strong-room**, which was the league's treasury.
- **The Lamp-House of the Lens**, the league's lights, relit after the Wreck of the Square about 2A 1507.
- **The Ransom Tide law** of the corsairs, born in the emptied harbours.
- **Nondondor's claim** to rule the banks.

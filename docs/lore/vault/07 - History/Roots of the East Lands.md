# Roots of the East Lands

> **Status: proposed.** Written for the owner's decision K67 (25 October 2026). Nothing here is adopted. Established facts it leans on are marked **Established**; everything else is a proposal under the [[Canon Policy]]. The text of record is the `roots` and `sign` of each realm in `tools/content/data/world/history/east_lands.json`, and the play hooks are in `docs/specs/02_lore/realm_roots/east_lands.md`.

## What stays open

These roots do not say what broke the bridge of [[Tarkia, the Bridge Kingdom]] (the temple and elven tellings stay in rivalry), what breathes under the Hot Lake, what became of the Kalioth Crown's provinces beyond the Melty Pass, which Old God the devils served, or why the black isles of the north are called a mistake. The Deliverer's temples stay Afrit's in secret. No being is given an age.

## The shape of the East

**Established:** one Eastern people held both shores before the rift ([[Tarkia, the Bridge Kingdom]]); [[Rhabtarkia, the Kalioth Crown]] paid Volgan the Black Tribute, held Herez, Noleksun and the South Pass for about a century and died with his host at Dardan, and Erco was founded on its ruin in 2A 12; Alerhia refused the Black Tribute in 1A 1214; devils are corrupted Lesser Spirits bound to their bodies; gunpowder is common in some realms (24 October 2026).

**Proposed:** the East's realms are built on two memories. The older is the Bridge Kingdom, kept by Alerhia's stair, Delbiz's pillars, Stabilo's bronze Measure, Ostoroth's cedar, Mora's hot lake and the flood that sent Orato's families onto their heights. The younger is the Kalioth Crown, which almost every realm of the south and the desert remembers from underneath: Noris's locked wells, Drak's broken collars, Plutondo's glass ploughshares, Mostok's Knife-Ground, Azano's unpaid tally, Obmi's fever road, Pornivior's devils' road and Erco's buried diadem. The north coast remembers Volgan's ship-tax, which made Naamis a crown of harbour-mothers and Woqood a people of refusers.

## Realm by realm (proposed)

- **Noris.** The desert tribes were the Bridge Kingdom's drovers and the Kalioth's water-bearers; after Dardan they broke the iron lids of the wells and swore that water would never belong to one man. *Sign:* an upright lance with a water-gourd beneath the blade, the gourd Qasud ben Melar offered to every tribe in the War of the Eighth Well.
- **Stabilo.** The bronze towns kept the King's Measure of the Bridge Road, a bronze ox, and sent the Kalioth kings a false ox of lead. *Sign:* a crouching bronze ox with a lifting ring.
- **Alerhia.** After the Breaking the valley raised a stair above the flood and crowned the man who carried the first stone; its scribes keep the empty line of the old title. *Sign:* a white stair of five steps with a river-king's crown on the top step.
- **Naamis.** The harbours gave Volgan ships and none came back; the harbour-mothers rebuilt and swore that no fleet would sail for a foreign lord on a man's word. *Sign:* the Returning Ship, double-prowed and mastless.
- **Drak.** The forge-houses descend from Kalioth slave-smiths who broke their collars after Dardan and swore never to wear a master's iron. *Sign:* an open iron collar broken at its hinge.
- **Rhachia.** Mothers who lived through the ash of the Rhachia Fires in the caves fired the first clay figures of their dead and founded the kilns. *Sign:* a seated clay mother-figure, carried at the rising of 4A 1719.
- **Erco.** The mine-lords of 2A 12 walled up the last Kalioth king's diadem, neither wearing nor melting it. *Sign:* the diadem upside down, its seven points downward.
- **Plutondo.** The valley ploughed the vents' ash, was the Kalioth's granary, chipped its own ploughshares from vent-glass and turned its barges to the besiegers in 2A 12. *Sign:* a black glass ploughshare, point downward.
- **Ostoroth.** The cedar-folk cut the cedar the bridge carried west; the lake-readers' floating lantern made the king in 3A 515. *Sign:* a closed cedar lantern.
- **Mora.** The lake-folk stayed when the lake turned hot in the Breaking and lived on the steam-eels; they sing low so as not to wake the lake, and bought their freedom with three harvests. *Sign:* a white steam-eel rising.
- **Delbiz.** The horse-lords keep the eastern half of Qadurum and the Gate-Horses said to descend from the last horses over the bridge. *Sign:* a broken column with a horse-head capital.
- **Kersian.** Aenoran exiles poured the Vale's water into Osrale's cistern and swore to carry it home. *Sign:* a sealed two-handled jar.
- **Mostok.** The junction was the Kalioth's Knife-Ground; the weighers tied every blade into its sheath at the gate. *Sign:* a sheathed sword with its hilt bound by a white cord.
- **Azano.** The Azani were Kalioth tally-lords; the last king died owing them. *Sign:* the two halves of a split tally-stick.
- **Obmi.** The jungle edge was the Kalioth fever road; the hornbill led the town upslope in the fever of 4A 1319. *Sign:* a black hornbill with a great casque.
- **Pornivior.** Nerhuus took up, without knowing it, the payment the Kalioth kings made to the devils of Mount Ergo. *Sign:* a balance-scale with its beam snapped, the caravan of 4A 744.
- **Woqood.** The boat clans refused Volgan their boats and fled onto the ice, where the Grandmother Under the Ice showed them the seals. *Sign:* the moot's single toggle-headed harpoon.
- **Orato.** The families fled onto the isles' heights in the flood of the Breaking and blew horns to call their boats home. *Sign:* the great horn of the northern height.

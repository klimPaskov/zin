# Roots of Triton's Islands

> **Status: proposed.** Written for the owner's decision K67 (25 October 2026). Nothing here is adopted. Established facts it leans on are marked **Established**; everything else is a proposal under the [[Canon Policy]]. The text of record is the `roots` and `sign` of each realm in `tools/content/data/world/history/tritons_islands.json`, and the play hooks are in `docs/specs/02_lore/realm_roots/tritons_islands.md`.

## What stays open

These roots do not say whether the Grey Breath still waits in the Cursed Square's wrecks (disputed), what the reef-spirits are (a belief of the islanders), or what lies beneath the Square. No being is given an age.

## The shape of the Arcs

**Established:** the chart-islanders are a Boreal people whose sea-words are older than any chart, descendants in part of [[Heulufaenn, the Sunfields]]; [[Margorodai, the Sea-Roads]] was sworn at Halgohavnai, carried the Grand Alliance's supply fleets under Senra of the Pupor Isles (K66) and died in the Grey Breath; the gale of 2A 904 broke Halgohavnai's moles into the Cursed Square; the chart-islanders look like lean, mixed, weathered sailors (K64).

**Proposed:** every realm of the arcs keeps one piece of the dead league or of the first crossings: Adel the lead that proves a reef, Raerby the cut fish of the banks-court, Pollos the three keys of the strong-room, the Lens the league's light, Halenor the shore-fire that guides or wrecks, Santon the black sail of the plague ships, Benan the dead harbour it will not enter, Nondondor a crown twisted from rope; and the first crossings gave Fineger its ram on the ice, the Windy Lands their kite and Seite its seals.

## Realm by realm (proposed)

- **Adel.** No reef is named until it is proved by the lead; the Andaios chart-house keeps a lead it calls Senra's. *Sign:* a long tapered sounding lead on its line.
- **Raerby.** The first fish of a disputed shoal is cut in two before both crews; the banks-court judged on the water. *Sign:* a banks-fish cut through by a thin white line.
- **Pollos.** The last living key-holder of the league's strong-room carried all three keys home in the Grey Breath. *Sign:* three keys on one ring.
- **Nondondor.** Kusion of the Kilai, having no gold, crowned himself with his ship's anchor cable. *Sign:* a crown twisted from rope.
- **Fineger.** The families crossed the frozen channel behind a white ram; a pilot is the ram of his ship. *Sign:* a white ram's head.
- **Halenor.** The first shore-fires guided kin home and strangers onto the rocks; Lusai set an empty fire-basket at Sonrale. *Sign:* an empty iron fire-basket on a pole.
- **Windy Lands.** The first winter on the capes was lived through by flying kites to catch seabirds. *Sign:* a diamond fishing kite with a streaming tail.
- **Seite Islands.** The grey seals bore the first wrecked pilots ashore, and no household kills one. *Sign:* a grey seal rising.
- **Santon.** Corsairs took the plague-emptied harbours under the black sails of ships of the dead. *Sign:* a black sail without ship or mast.
- **Benan.** The corsairs hold the Cursed Square in name and never enter it; a corsair is a crab in a borrowed shell. *Sign:* a hermit crab in a spiral shell.
- **The Lens.** The glass-blowers swore to keep the passages lit after the Wreck of the Square; the first Keeper blew the great lamp-glass in one breath. *Sign:* a glass-blower's pipe with a glowing gather.

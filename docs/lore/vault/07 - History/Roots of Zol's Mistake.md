# Roots of Zol's Mistake

**Status of this note:** proposed (decision K67, 25 October 2026). Everything below is a **Proposed** creative addition under the [[Canon Policy]] unless it is marked **Established**, and nothing here answers an item of [[Open Questions]]. The play hooks are in `docs/specs/02_lore/realm_roots/zols_mistake.md` and the data in `tools/content/data/world/history/zols_mistake.json`.

**Established background:** [[Zol]], the Old God of Death and the only Old God in Aoaz, came to the black north long before Aen and Volgan, and the land was already desolate. Nobody knows why it is called a Mistake. Who administers Furviatlon is unknown. The dead wander the land without an established allegiance. [[Kusven]], Zol's most loyal servant, dwells on Šodolôrō and is known from the start as a legend of the winter tales (owner, 25 October 2026, K55). The souls of the dead remain Zol's (owner, 24 October 2026).

## Told from the sea

**Proposed.** The Mistake has no founder, no first king, no oath and no tongue of its own, so its roots can only be told as the living of the north know them. The oldest sea-words of the north-west, older than any chart, have a word for the black coast that means only the shore from which nothing comes back.

## The open rings

**Proposed.** On the outer isles someone laid the dead inside rings of black stones and left every ring open on its northern side. Nobody knows whose hand that was. The Leren fishers who began to winter on the isles in 4A 230 found the rings older than any record and took up the custom, because the dead belong to the Old God of Death and a closed ring would be a theft from him.

The first fish left on the black shore, the silence on still nights, the name never spoken at sea and the winter tale of Kusven the grave-wind all read as branches of that one belief. In 4A 1450 a priest of the Strait Litany persuaded a family of Erinlor to close the ring of their dead in the name of the twelve; the fishers say their boat was lost before the next still night, the neighbours opened the ring again, and no priest has asked it of the isles since.

## The sign

**Established device:** the broken concentric rings of the Will of Zol, the emblem in whose name the realm is held (heraldry spec 1.5).

**Proposed story:** the sign first appears on the Aenoran chart of 1A 470, where a pilot of the northern voyages drew rings on flat water beside the name, broken where his pen lifted, as the mark of a sea that lay still. The fishers found the same figure laid in stones on the outer isles in 4A 230. To the living it means that what dies is owed; over the hosts of the black north it threatens that every grave in their path is open.

## Kept mysteries

Why the land is a Mistake, who keeps Furviatlon, who first laid the open rings, what Kusven truly is, and everything of the wider world stay unanswered. The flag shows only the rings, never Kusven, his isle or the Beasts.

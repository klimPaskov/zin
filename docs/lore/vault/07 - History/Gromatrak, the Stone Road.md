# Gromatrak, the Stone Road

**Established (owner, 25 October 2026; decision K64):** Gromatrak was the dwarves' realm under the seas, a union of the holds of every great range joined by roads through the deep rock. In the First Age the dwarves let the sea into their own roads to drown the Damned Spirits that Volgan released, and Tromahrim, the Great Hall of the Deep King, lies under the Sea of Monsters. A Sea-Door can be opened in play at great risk.

**Status of this note:** owner-adopted on 25 October 2026 (decision K64). The **Disputed** and **Mystery kept** items are not answered in text, and the names in quotation marks are working labels. The details are in the design addendum `docs/plans/deep_history/k62_fallen_realms.md`, and the play-facing summary is `docs/specs/02_lore/10_fallen_realms.md`.

## Names

- **Gromatrak**, in Dwarvish *grom*, "stone", and *trak*, "road": the Stone Road, the name every hold uses.
- **Tromahrim**, in Dwarvish *trom*, "great", and *hrim*, "hall": the Great Hall, seat of the Deep King.
- **The Deep King**, from Dwarvish *drav*, "king".
- **The Stone Roads**, in Common Speech, kept in the epithet of Brunek of the Stone Roads, the dwarven engineer of the vault's First Age roster.

## The people

The realm was the dwarves of every great range, as one realm. The owner's decision K22 places the dwarves in the great ranges of the West and North Lands and the Great Wall range. The one tongue that every hold still speaks is the realm's legacy, so that the names of a western hold and of a northern one are built from the same words. The realm says nothing about where the dwarves first came from, which stays open (V11).

## Age and extent

The first roads were cut in the Age of Kingdoms, beneath the low country while it was still dry land, about 2,000 BA. The realm outlived the drowning above it, because its roads ran in deep rock far below the plain, and it was at its height in the First Age until its fall at the end of that age.

At its height it held the western ranges, now the holds of Brundahrim and Gromkerm, the Great Wall range, where Kunhrim stands, and the High Mountains of the North Lands, where Hrakbrund stands. These were joined by roads under the floor of the Crown Sea and under the Narrow Strait. Tromahrim lay deep beneath the low country's northern hills, which are now the floor of the Sea of Monsters.

## What made it great

It is the only realm that ever spanned three landmasses, and a dwarf could walk from the West Lands to the North Lands without seeing the sky. It sold its iron to Aenor through the Western Gate, which opened about 1A 300, and it sent its engineers to Dardan.

## How it fell

In Volgan's war the Damned Spirits rose from deep beneath the mountains and the underground world, and they came into the Stone Roads. The Deep King ordered the roads under the sea broken and the sea let in, and Tromahrim drowned with the spirits inside it, while Kunhrim's delvers collapsed the tunnels at Dardan.

The four holds were cut off from one another and have had no Deep King since. Each keeps a sealed **Sea-Door** (working label) at the head of its old road.

**Disputed.** Who gave the order is not settled. Kunhrim says that the Deep King ordered the Letting-In of the Sea. The western holds say that Kunhrim's lords urged it to save their own gate, a grudge older than any charter.

**Mystery kept.** What remains below the sea.

## What survives

- **One tongue** in the four holds.
- **The four Sea-Doors**, one at the head of each old road.
- **The Halls Below the Halls**, a focus of the holds' shared tree.
- **The Deep King's lamp**, which passes with the Deep King's seat if the holds choose one again.
- **The grudge** between the western holds and Kunhrim.

Brunek of the Stone Roads, the dwarven engineer on the vault's First Age roster, is named for these roads. The roster keeps him without a homeland, and this note does not place him.

## Knowledge layers

- **Belief.** The holds hold that the sea is the seal, and that while the sea lies on the hall, what is in it stays.
- **Disputed.** Whether Kunhrim or the western holds bear the blame for the Letting-In.
- **Mystery kept.** What remains below the sea.
- **Gated by play.** The names of the last delvers who sealed each door, which are read at the door.

# Possible Endings

The imported original compendium defines three principal outcomes. These are **alternative endings**, not three events that all historically occur.

## The Lost Cause

The Lost Cause prevails. Under [[Eindil]], it becomes a settlement seeking peace that refuses imperial domination. This differs from the [[Golden King]]'s original prestige-driven faction.

## Children of Volgan

[[Volgan]] dominates a ruined world through armies of monsters, grave hosts, and client states. His rise is compatible with his status as a **Great Spirit**, not a New or Old God.

## The Ashen Throne

[[Afrit]] achieves power through worship, bargains, corruption, and capture of worldly institutions.

**Open:** the precise military, political, or supernatural mechanics leading to each ending. The supporting 18-document spec package was identified in the original snapshot as proposed, not automatically canonical.

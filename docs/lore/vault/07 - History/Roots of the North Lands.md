# Roots of the North Lands

> **Status: proposed.** Written for the owner's decision K67 (25 October 2026). Nothing here is adopted. Established facts it leans on are marked **Established**; everything else is a proposal under the [[Canon Policy]]. The text of record is the `roots` and `sign` of each realm in `tools/content/data/world/history/north_lands.json`, and the play hooks are in `docs/specs/02_lore/realm_roots/north_lands.md`.

## What stays open

These roots do not say where the dwarves or the dwarf goblins first came from, what the smooth square workings in Mina's ore hills are, or what lies beyond the Hrakbrund Sea-Door. No being is given an age.

## The shape of the North

**Established:** the North Lands were allies of Aenor and never its subjects ([[The First Age of Aoaz]]); the dwarves hold the great ranges of the North (K22) and Hrakbrund's road under the strait was part of [[Gromatrak, the Stone Road]]; the dwarf goblins are a people of their own near Lerendor (24 October 2026).

**Proposed:** the North's crowns are almost all Leren, and each of them is given the one deed or custom no other crown shares, so that the region does not read as a row of alike ships and crowns.

## Realm by realm (proposed)

- **Lerendor.** The first Leren captains learned the shoaled strait by following the strait-whale, and under the Ship-Bond a Sonarvik captain led the Aenoran hulls through behind a sounding whale. *Sign:* the two flukes of a sounding whale, the strait kingdom and Lerendor's North as one tail.
- **Eekedor.** In 2A 1205 the grain-families of Viktara weighed a raider chief's sword on the harbour steelyard against grain, silver and a siege's lives, and the host took the silver. *Sign:* a black steelyard with its hanging weight.
- **R'en.** The cape-clans took one fish from every boat under their rocks; Torik of the Farnur, the Cormorant, refused the strait kings their payment and made himself prince. *Sign:* a cormorant on a rock with a fish in its beak.
- **Molot.** Helmund of the Ostvar's men moved the marsh's path-stakes by night and drowned a raider host. *Sign:* a crooked white path-stake in black marsh water.
- **Keredor-Hellekton.** The Arbitration Court married the two coast crowns in 4A 1344, and the smiths forged one double crown of Keredor's silver and Hellekton's gold, worn twice at every crowning. *Sign:* a double crown of a silver and a gold circlet.
- **Tolvael.** When the coast crowns proposed to share the vale out, every envoy's axe broke on a Kerhil pine log, and Brann of the Agtos was crowned seated on it. *Sign:* an unsplit pine log with a broken axe-head.
- **Tolhon.** The Onp fishers kept the Herring Peace long before the princes, and Ulvek of the Ishor swore it as the coast's first law at the bridge of Sirtum. *Sign:* a silver herring leaping over a stone arch.
- **Heodwa Lands.** The three hill crowns and the dwarves swore the Hill Pact of 3A 806 on one great horn, which the dwarves bound with three gold rings. *Sign:* a great black horn with three gold rings.
- **Hrakbrund.** After the Hill Pact the White Hall cut a stair from the High Gate down to the valley floor, wide enough for a column; the dwarves came down it in 3A 1022. *Sign:* a white stair descending.
- **Mina.** Miners walled up the smooth square workings no man cut; in the gallery fall of 4A 1567 the king died at the rescue with his crown hung on his pick. *Sign:* an upright pick with a gold circlet on its haft.
- **Dolpar.** In the Raiding Years the four walled towns judged their quarrels on four boats lashed together in the lake's middle. *Sign:* a white four-oared boat seen from above.
- **Mustnom.** The first packmen came home from the long road's end with a sack of salt, and the boss poured a pinch to thank the road. *Sign:* a pedlar's pack-frame with a white salt-bag.

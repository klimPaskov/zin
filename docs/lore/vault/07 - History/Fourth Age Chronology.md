# Fourth Age Chronology

> **Status: proposed.** The owner asked on 18 October 2026 for a deep Fourth Age history, the campaign's start year and how the age plays out. Every date below is a proposal built on the established outline in [[The Fourth Age of Erdae]] and the [[Fourth Age Canon Register]]. Established events keep their established order. The dates assume the proposed **1,760-year** Fourth Age, so Eindil's coronation falls in **4A 1760**.

## How years are counted

Years are reckoned from the death of [[Elzon]] and the crowning of the first restored king, written **4A** (Fourth Age). 4A 1 is the first year of the restored monarchy. The age ends with Eindil's coronation.

**Proposed calendar.** Erdae keeps twelve months whose lengths match the old reckoning of Aen's astronomers. Their names come from the farming year of the Middle Lands:

| No. | Month | Season |
| --- | --- | --- |

| 2 | Ironfrost | winter |
| 3 | Thaw | spring |
| 4 | Rainwake | spring |
| 5 | Sowing | spring |
| 6 | Highsun | summer |

| 8 | Harvest | summer |
| 9 | Fallowing | autumn |
| 10 | Emberleaf | autumn |
| 11 | Mistfall | autumn |
| 12 | Longnight | winter |

## The campaign begins in 4A 1748

**Start: 1 Thaw, 4A 1748** (the owner fixed the situation on 18 October 2026; the date itself is proposed). The Golden King reigns in his nineteenth year. Eindil has not appeared. Afrit is unrevealed: the world knows only the Deliverer it worships. Erdae seems at peace, as it has been for seventeen centuries. The dangers exist, but only a few people see them: Mirolon's scouts, island fishermen, the Hidden Council itself. Twelve years later, in 4A 1760, the age can end with Eindil's coronation. Eindil's whole journey, the Golden King's murder, Afrit's open kingship and the War of the Last Crowns therefore fit inside one campaign, and the player meets every established event as something that can still go another way.

See [[The Campaign of 4A 1748]] for the world at the start and how the age can unfold.

## I. The Restoration, 4A 1 to 4A 212

| Year | Event |
| --- | --- |
| 4A 1 | [[Harion the Restorer (proposed)|Harion of Selen]] is crowned at Parim after Elzon's death. Afrit is acclaimed publicly as the Deliverer, under the proposed name [[Aurevan the Deliverer|Aurevan]]. |
| 4A 3 | The **Charters of Parim** bind the surviving Middle Lands dominions to the crown as self-governing members of a common defence. They are the origin of the satellites of the Middle Kingdom Concord: Selen, Kilinti, Kingson, Hiloron, Konori, Hunhun and the rest keep their own lords and laws. |
| 4A 4 to 60 | Varner's Unbinding lifts the curse from the old grave-fields. The [[Lantern Chain]] dissolves into village healers and wardens. |
| 4A 11 | The [[Houses of the First Light]] receive royal charters to keep the grain stores, hospitals and birth rolls of the kingdom. |
| 4A 40 to 120 | Roads, canals and the Great Lake ports are rebuilt. The Second Age's medicine and farming return to every province. |
| 4A 96 | **The Wall Accord.** Elzon's Order had raised a fortified line, the Pale March, against the Magical Lands, which it never conquered. Harion's grandson and the elven realms agree to keep it rather than tear it down: the elves wanted their old separation, the kings wanted a customs line and a guard against the dragons of the east. Rebuilt in stone, it becomes the **Great Wall of Aenor**. |
| 4A 150 to 212 | Aenoran merchants return to the West Lands for the first time since the First Age wars. Western lords accept the trade but not the old Aenoran claims. |

## II. The Long Peace, 4A 212 to 4A 1000

| Year | Event |
| --- | --- |
| 4A 212 | **First Concord Council** in Parim. The dominions and the crown agree on a common army in war and free roads in peace. |
| 4A 340 | The universities of Parim and Selen are founded. Physicians of the Houses of the First Light teach in both. |
| 4A 411 | **The Leren crowns unite.** Lerendor and Lerenwaith marry their royal houses and become one kingdom, the strongest power of the North Lands. |
| 4A 512 | **The Treaty of the Narrow Strait.** The North Lands and Aenor promise mutual defence across the strait. This is the "old alliance" the North later abandons. |
| 4A 600 to 780 | The great caravan roads of the East Lands open. **Noris** and **Stabilo** grow rich on them and begin a rivalry that never fully ends. |
| 4A 702 | The Triton islanders chart the Pupor Ocean and sell their charts to every crown. |
| 4A 850 | Coldor's kings bring the frozen interior of the Cold Lands under one crown. Isengard refuses and becomes Coldor's enduring rival. |
| 4A 1000 | **The Thousand-Year Jubilee.** Parim celebrates a millennium of peace. Aurevan's temples bless the crown in front of envoys from every region. Later historians date the end of the high peace to this year. |

## III. The Kingdom Behind the Throne, 4A 1000 to 4A 1729

| Year | Event |
| --- | --- |
| c. 4A 1100 | [[Afrits Hidden Council]] takes its lasting form: four seats for royal administration, worship, foreign agents and the recovery of artifacts. |
| 4A 1140 to 1400 | Priests educate princes, the Houses keep the kingdom's records, and royal councillors rise by Aurevan's favour. Successive kings reign while the Council governs. |
| c. 4A 1300 | The eastern branch of the Council begins its long courtship of East Lands and South Lands courts as a reserve seat of power. |
| c. 4A 1450 | Orc warlords unite the inner Dark Lands as **Goldoroth**. Its wars with the human kingdom of **Dondor** begin. |
| c. 4A 1520 | The Worshipdom grows in the Zilin Hills of the Magical Lands, around a hermitage of the Deliverer. It holds little land; its value lies in the pilgrims, couriers and agents it shelters. |
| 4A 1611 | **The theft of the Necklace of the Abyss.** The master thief, proposed [[Sevrin the Unending (proposed)|Sevrin]], returns from Roka's domain. Afrit grants him unending life. |
| 4A 1611 to 1700 | Afrit gathers lesser artifacts: the proposed Ring of Doors, Crown of Witnesses and Bracelet of the First Seal. |
| c. 4A 1650 | Volgan's strength returns. Demon, Dolon and Nezer swear to him and form the **Southern Dominion**. Mirolon fortifies its northern passes. |
| 4A 1702 | The Gunwaith–Girdon wars divide the West Lands. Each side courts Aenoran gold and both resent it. |
| 4A 1716 | Lerendor's envoys are humiliated in Parim over a disputed toll. The Leren court begins to doubt the Treaty of the Narrow Strait. |

## IV. The Golden King, 4A 1729 to 4A 1748

| Year | Event |
| --- | --- |
| 4A 1729 | The [[Golden King]] is crowned. He sells offices within the year. |
| 4A 1733 | The Golden Court is completed in Parim: a palace district of gilded halls where offices, titles and charters are bought. |
| 4A 1736 | A Mirolonese envoy warns Parim that the Dead Lands are arming. The court laughs: Volgan has been quiet for longer than Aenor has had kings. |
| 4A 1741 | **The founding of [[The Lost Cause]].** The Golden King proclaims a league of the Concord and willing foreign crowns against "the powers of the dark", names himself its first lord, and spends its first treasury on a triumph. Most of Erdae treats it as royal vanity against old legends. Its frontier companies and scholars take it seriously. |
| 4A 1744 | Island fishermen tell of black ships among the islands of the Ocean of Zol. Nobody in a royal court believes them. |
| 4A 1745 | Gunwaith and Girdon sign the **Truce of the Grey Ford** after half a century of border war. The West Lands are quiet for the first time in a generation. |
| 4A 1746 | In secret, Afrit's Council concludes that the Golden King is a liability: the dominions grumble at his taxes and the Lost Cause could become a rival centre of power. Preparations for his death begin, known only to the Council. |
| **4A 1748** | **Campaign start.** Erdae seems at peace. |

## V. The end of the age, 4A 1748 to 4A 1760

The established events below are the **historical path**. In play they are what happens if nobody changes them. The dates are proposals.

| Year | Event |
| --- | --- |
| 4A 1748 | The peaceful year. Nothing visible happens beyond the ordinary quarrels of courts. |
| 4A 1749 | **Eindil wakes** in the ice of the far Cold Lands, early in the year. Tribes of the north-west find him and nurse him back to strength. Late in the year he travels south with West Lands traders. |
| 4A 1750 | The Bridge of the Three Oaths, Girdon's Winter Ford and the Gunwaith trial (proposed episodes). When the Truce of the Grey Ford breaks, a western confederation makes Eindil its temporary war-king; he makes peace and lays the title down. **In Mistfall, the Golden King dies.** The Council and the temples proclaim Aurevan King of Aenor, First of His Name. |
| 4A 1751 | Eindil sails for Aenor; storms carry him to the Islands of the South. |
| 4A 1752 | Eindil reaches the Gala elves. Lorien names him. The Mirror of First Rain (proposed). |
| 4A 1753 | The voyage north. Afrit leaves Chancellor Merovan (proposed) in Parim. The ambush; the hall of Horos. Horos gives Glo and dies; Afrit is wounded and flees. |
| 4A 1754 | Eindil warns Aenor and is denounced as an impostor. Alleria of Selen protects him. |
| 4A 1755 | **The War of the Last Crowns begins.** The Western Crossing, the Leren withdrawal from the Treaty of the Narrow Strait, the eastern invasion of the Magical Lands, Volgan's reconquest of Mirolon and the rising of Zol's dead. |
| 4A 1756 | Afrit abandons Aenor and is proclaimed King of the East. Western armies take Parim. |
| 4A 1757 | Eindil returns on a dragon with Varner and Golbran. Varner dies. The New Gods restore Eindil. Volgan's manifested body is destroyed. Zol manifests and is forced back; Lorien dies. |
| 4A 1758 | Afrit returns with an eastern host, casts Eindil from the cliff and claims kingship over all Erdae. |
| 4A 1759 | The battle in the palace. Afrit dies. Golbran dies saving Eindil. |
| 4A 1760 | **Eindil is crowned King of Aenor.** The Fourth Age ends. |

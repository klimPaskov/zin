# Roots of the West Lands

> **Status: proposed.** Written for the owner's decision K67 (25 October 2026). Nothing here is adopted. Established facts it leans on are marked **Established**; everything else is a proposal under the [[Canon Policy]]. The text of record is the `roots` and `sign` of each realm in `tools/content/data/world/history/west_lands.json`, and the play hooks are in `docs/specs/02_lore/realm_roots/west_lands.md`.

## What stays open

These roots do not say where the dwarves first came from, what lies below the sea, or what the Sunfields were beyond what [[Heulufaenn, the Sunfields]] already holds. No being is given an age, and Corogon's sealed letter is never read in text.

## The shape of the West

**Established:** the West Lands had peoples and kingdoms before Aenor's ships came, and the settler wars are remembered as theft ([[The First Age of Aoaz]]); the Gunwyn clans descend in part from the people of the Sunfields, who went south over the neck when the winters lengthened (K64); the western dwarf holds were part of [[Gromatrak, the Stone Road]].

**Proposed:** the West shares one grievance and differs in everything else. The downs clans walked; the Erg clans sailed; the settler towns broke with Aenor each in its own way; the dwarves lost the deep roads; the plague made Buldor; and the small folk fed their neighbours.

## Realm by realm (proposed)

- **Gunwyn.** The clan song says the rooks went before the clans over the neck, carrying ears from their last barley-carts, and the clan that walked behind them became the Rookwyn; land belongs to whoever sows it. *Sign:* a black rook with a gold barley-ear in its beak.
- **Girdon.** In 1A 640 each ford-warden carried a stone from his own ford to Arligon and laid it in the river, making one line of stepping-stones; a stone was pulled from the line at the Grey Ford in 4A 1702. *Sign:* five stepping-stones across a river band.
- **Werlendor.** The Erg clans came down the coast by boat while the downs clans walked; Dyfan, they say, broke his king-torc for his two sons, and Emric carried his half to Caer-Erg. *Sign:* half of a broken gold torc.
- **Lambin.** A banked field above Faennon was ploughed in common long before Aen under the law that no blood is shed on sown ground; Branorn's granaries stand on its staddle-stones. *Sign:* a granary raised on four staddle-stones.
- **Thenon.** The lowland's first folk gave the hungry neck-clans olives and married them; the Mother Olive of Tummin is the eldest graft, regrafted from the small folk's orchards after the blight. *Sign:* an olive tree with one grafted lighter bough.
- **Dobbum.** In the famine of 3A 899 the small folk carried the Famine Bread over the passes, each loaf stamped with the baking household's thumb. *Sign:* a round loaf stamped with a thumb-print.
- **Brundahrim.** When the western Sea-Door was sealed the fire-hold carried one fire out of the drowning road in an iron fire-pot, the Road-Fire, which lit the West's first powder. *Sign:* an iron fire-pot on a chain with a red fire.
- **Gromkerm.** The oldest western door held open for the others' delvers before it was sealed last; Saopon's hired companies tore the iron fist-knocker from its Outer Gate in 2A 1712. *Sign:* an iron knocker shaped as a clenched fist.
- **Saopon.** The Kulolo boatmen swore loads on their weight, and the league of 1A 930 was sworn with a dwarf ingot on the guild book. *Sign:* a closed book weighted with an iron ingot.
- **Buldor.** The first banners of the companies were sewn from the shrouds of the plague dead, and the first contract-book was written on the backs of the plague-rolls. *Sign:* a white swallow-tailed pennon with a black seam.
- **Kradlon.** Bolbin recorded Parim's dues on split hazel tallies and broke that year's tally across in the Unpaid Year of 1A 912. *Sign:* a hazel tally broken across.
- **Corogon.** Oslodon's first settlers landed Aenor's letter of claim to the West; in 1A 940 the merchant houses swore never to break its seal and gave each of the two consuls one key to its chest. *Sign:* a rolled letter under an unbroken black seal.
- **Varnondor.** The cape clans kept fires above their barrows to watch the sea; Oslin put the fires behind glass in iron lanterns in 2A 455. *Sign:* a black iron lantern with a white light.
- **Wennarith.** The bend's ferry-families never cut a ferry-rope, because a cut rope drowns whoever is mid-river, and the wardens carry no army. *Sign:* an empty white ferry-barge.

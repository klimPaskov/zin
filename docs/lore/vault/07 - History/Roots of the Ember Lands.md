# Roots of the Ember Lands

> **Status: proposed.** Written for the owner's decision K67 (25 October 2026). Nothing here is adopted. Established facts it leans on are marked **Established**; everything else is a proposal under the [[Canon Policy]]. The text of record is the `roots` and `sign` of each realm in `tools/content/data/world/history/ember_lands.json`, and the play hooks are in `docs/specs/02_lore/realm_roots/ember_lands.md`.

## What stays open

These roots do not say which New God Volgan served and overcame, or when; how he came to hold the plateau; whether his essence survives the breaking of his body; the King of Demons' own name; which Old God the devils served; or anything of Roka and the Abyss beyond the vault. No being is given an age.

## The shape of the Ember Lands

**Established:** [[Volgan]] is a Great Spirit who once served a New God and overcame him; he took Mirolon in the First Age and lost it after Dardan; Elzon governed the Ember Lands in his name and brought his presence back from the Void; demons are corrupted angels, immortal Great Folk, and devils are corrupted Lesser Spirits bound to their bodies; the [[King of Demons]] has his seat in the [[Nether]], and his pacts bind the living while the dead remain Zol's; Nazerun is the old name of Nezer.

**Proposed:** the island's realms are the power's own (Puvulan, whose sign is Volgan's gate as the Eye was Sauron's), three demon houses who each carried one craft up out of the Halls (beasts, pacts, galleys), the court that seals every pact, and the peninsula's men, whose signs all come from how they lived beside the Dominion: watching it, salting their dead against it, fleeing it through the storm, or sheltering what escapes it.

## Realm by realm (proposed)

- **Puvulan.** The orders teach that the Gate of Nafeher was the first thing raised on the plateau; every servant is cut into the ring of names round it, and a traitor is cut out. *Sign:* the Gate of Nafeher within the plain ring of names.
- **Mirolon.** The lake-kingdom's survivors hid five centuries in the Great Swamp and read Volgan's patrols by the herons; Hadric hung the crown in the beacon tower. *Sign:* a grey heron standing watchful.
- **Khorvrath.** A house of the Halls' beast-breeders, refused a portal of its own, rode up out of the Nether Maw and broke a horn on its rim. *Sign:* a great curved horn broken at its tip.
- **Dolon.** Queen Khirara drowned the salt coast's headman in his brine-pan and crowned herself with its salt crystals; Dolon's oaths are written in salt. *Sign:* a crown of jagged salt crystals.
- **Nezer.** A family of the Nether's ferrymen, whose lava galleys hold their course only when both steering oars pull together, took the Necromancer's empty hills as two crowns. *Sign:* a black galley with two steering oars.
- **Nether.** The houses cut the Halls beneath Mount Nether and took a king whose seal is burned into every pact. *Sign:* the King's seal, the one five-pointed figure of Aoaz, inside a ring of fire.
- **Halia.** The salt-boilers buried their dead in salt, and the freeholders believe the salted dead were spared when the necromancers raised the lake-kingdom's dead. *Sign:* an oar standing in a white cairn of salt.
- **Nan.** The men of the old coast who would not serve the Dominion fled across the storm belt; their wayfinders learned the route by following the storm-petrels. *Sign:* a dark storm-petrel skimming low.
- **Olen.** A breeze out of the storm belt kept the cliff lamps lit for a winter while sea-kestrels hung still in it. *Sign:* a hovering sea-kestrel.

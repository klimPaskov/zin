# Holy Seven

## Established cosmological role

The **Holy Seven** are the seven overwhelmingly powerful cosmic creators of the ZIN universe and the creators of both the [[Old Gods]] and the [[New Gods]]. They exist below [[He Who Was, Is and Will Be]] and above the ordinary divine orders.

They are intentionally unimportant to day-to-day ZIN history because they are too vast and removed from it. Most inhabitants of [[Erdae and Its Regions|Erdae]] do **not** even believe they existed. Trace references may remain in the most distant myths. By contrast, ordinary people generally know of Old and New Gods.

Their individual names, fields of power, number of interventions, and appearances are **not established**. Avoid turning them into active patron gods or major antagonists.

## Proposed names people could use

These were offered as possible mythical or skeptical labels and remain **unselected**:

| Candidate | Possible in-world sense |
| --- | --- |
| **The Seven Before Names** | An expression from traditions claiming they predate naming, language, and history. |
| **The Seven Who Never Were** | A skeptical dismissal of the oldest creation myths. |
| **The Unremembered Seven** | Their deeds and identities have supposedly disappeared from memory. |
| **The First Hands** | An ancient image of the makers of existence. |
| **The Nameless Seven** | Their names are unknown or unexpressible. |
| **The Seven Beyond Time** | Their existence surpasses ordinary time. |
| **The Forgotten Makers** | A rare scholarly or mythic label for primordial creators. |

A previously suggested **working combination**, not fixed lore, was to retain *Holy Seven* as the actual designation, *Seven Before Names* in ancient writings, and *Seven Who Never Were* among skeptics.

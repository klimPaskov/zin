# Old Gods

## Established

The **Old Gods** are the original, almost unimaginably ancient generation of gods. The [[Holy Seven]] created them before the [[New Gods]]. Originally they possessed far more power than the New Gods.

Their strength and ability to exercise power have faded across immense spans of time. They are generally unable to take active physical form and rarely influence the world openly. Yet even diminished Old Gods remain enormously powerful.

**[[Zol]] is an Old God and the only Old God in Erdae.** This does not prove that no other Old Gods exist elsewhere in ZIN.

[[The Fading]] describes the process restricting them. [[Zol]] is an important exception to any simplistic assumption of continued decline: he **gains** power slowly every day, but Erdae's magic makes it difficult for him to channel and manifest that power.

## Ideas explored but not fixed

- Each Old God might be bound to an underlying principle of reality rather than a narrow religious domain. For example, Zol governs **Death** itself, while a New God could govern mourning or funerary ritual.
- Their inability to act might derive from a growing entanglement with the foundations of existence.
- Their physical presence, dreams, visions, and influence on sacred places may have changed between ages.

These are proposed interpretations. The underlying cause of [[The Fading]] is deliberately unresolved.

## Religion

The Old Gods are known in myth and religious tradition, despite being old and often regarded as lost or inactive. [[Religious Knowledge and False Gods|Accounts may conflict]] about whether they died, slept, left, or became hidden.

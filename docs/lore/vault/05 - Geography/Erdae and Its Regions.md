# Erdae and Its Regions

**Erdae** is a continent in the larger fictional ZIN universe and the setting of the ZIN map mod. The map was assembled from **13 hand-drawn regional sheets** and rearranged in October 2026. **Aenor Isle** is treated as a part of the Middle Lands, though it appears separately in the region list below.

The **far west and north** of the Cold Lands, and **far east and south** beyond the East Lands and South Lands desert, extend outside the known map. What lies beyond is not fixed.

| Current region | Drawn sheet or previous designation | Established geography, sites and peoples |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Cold Lands** | Cold Lands | Northwest, reaches off west and north of the map, largest landmass. Coldor, Isengard, Horidor, Yeti Lands, Windwaith arm. |
| **West Lands** | West-Earth, drawn sheet headed North Earth | South of Cold Lands, connected by broad neck. Gunwaith, Girdon, Kradlon, Corogon, Varnondor. Snow only near the Cold Lands neck. |
| **Triton's Islands** | Tritons Islands / Tornado Islands | West of West Lands, arcs around Pupor Ocean. Adel, Pollos, Raerby, Nondondor. |
| **North Lands** | East-Earth, drawn sheet headed West Earth | North of Middle Lands across narrow strait. Lerendor and Lerenwaith (one crown), Eekedor, Molot, R'en. |
| **Dark Lands** | Dark Lands | Between Lerendor and Middle Lands across narrow straits. Bordon, Dreson, Dulundur, Troll Forest, Dondor, Goldoroth, Sea of Darkness. |
| **Middle Lands** | Rush Land | Geographic center. Aenor and its neighbouring regions: Selen, Kilinti, Kingson, Hiloron, Great Lake. See [[Middle Lands and Aenor]]. |
| **Aenor Isle** | Rush Islands | Off the Middle Lands' west coast, **part of Middle Lands**. Includes Asgard. |
| **Magical Lands** | Magical Lands | East of Middle Lands, same landmass. Magical Forest, Mushroom Forest, Magic Mountains, Herrodon, Andos Enda, Nolorim. Long rift sea separates it from East Lands. |
| **Zol / Zol's Mistake** | Ocean of Edvoid | Far northern black wasteland and every black island of the Ocean of Zol, largely deserted and roamed by the dead. Contains Šodolôrō. See [[Zols Mistake]]. |
| **East Lands** | South-Earth | Across the rift in the east, reaches off the eastern map. Noris, Stabilo, Avalon, Ostoroth, Gondolin, Drak, Mora. Mirrored for mod map. |
| **South Lands** | Desert of South-Earth | Great desert of East Lands, extends south off the map. Noris Desert and former Sunhot Desert. |
| **Dead Lands** | Dead Lands | South of Middle Lands. [[Volgan]]'s domain: Mirolon, Nan, Demon, Dolon, Nezer, Nether. See [[Volgans Dead Lands]]. |
| **Islands of the South** | Islands of The South | Two arcs south of West Lands. Howan, Nara, Verde, Enhis Is Rond. |
| **Lands of the Sun** | Sunhot | Far southwest, runs beyond southern map. Elvana, Igara, Hengyang, Sea of Gods, Heaven, Flame, Godland. |

The full **711-label** map transcription is described in the source snapshot but was not separately available when this vault was built. This note includes every regional name and example locality actually reported in that snapshot, not the missing 711-label data.

## Newly confirmed places within the Dead Lands

The [[Nether]], in the **south of the Dead Lands**, is the seat of the [[King of Demons]] and associated with the demon [[Agon]] in the Second Age. The [[Abyss]], also in the Dead Lands, is associated with [[Roka]] and the [[Necklace of the Abyss]], taken and used by Afrit in the Fourth Age. The **Void**, which formerly held Volgan, is a distinct name and should not be equated with either region.

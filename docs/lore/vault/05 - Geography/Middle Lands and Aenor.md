# Middle Lands and Aenor

**Aenor, the Middle Kingdom**, was previously called **Rush, the Rush Kingdom**. It is the central great power of Aoaz. Its current map-design capital is **Parim**.

The **Middle Lands**, previously Rush Land, contain the Middle Kingdom's central plains, forests, mountains, and Great Lake. Associated named regions include **Selen, Kilinti, Kingson, Konori, Hunhun**, and **Herez**, beside the sovereign giant kingdom of **Hirolon** (former label Hiloron), which treats with Aenor (owner, 24 October 2026). **Aenor Isle** (previously Rush Islands) lies off the west coast and contains **Asgard**.

**The Great Wall of Aenor (owner, 24 October 2026):** the Great Wall of Aenor is an enormous **natural mountain range** that separates the Middle Lands from the [[Aoaz and Its Regions|Magical Lands]]. It is not a built wall or fortified line. Its passes, valleys and snowlines decide the routes between the two lands, and fortified passes or watchtowers may stand only where history explains them. **Dwarves** hold the range, as they hold the great ranges of the West and North Lands (owner, 24 October 2026).

**Aenor Forest (owner, 24 October 2026):** the largest forest of the Middle Lands. The bestiary cards call it Rush Forest, after Rush, the old name of Aenor. It is home of the walking tree people of Aenor Forest (former card name Coniferon; new name to be chosen), a people separate from the ents. Ents and the walking tree people never share a forest: the ents live in Kilinti. The forest's exact place in the Middle Lands is not yet fixed.

The older original story recognizes dominions including Selen's rangers, Kilinti's ents and forest peoples, and the riders of **Huhun**. It also counted Hiloron's giants among them, but **Hirolon is a sovereign giant kingdom that treats with Aenor**, not a dominion (owner, 24 October 2026). The revised map's country system treats Aenor's surrounding Middle Lands regions as **satellites**. Whether each original dominion is constitutionally a satellite or an internal division remains an open implementation/lore detail.

The current working table assigns Aenor **26 satellites**, with listed examples **Selen, Kilinti, Kingson, Konori, Hunhun, Asgard, Herez**. The older table also listed Hiloron, but Hirolon is sovereign (owner, 24 October 2026). The **Cursed Lands** and **Bloody Forest** resist its control.

Its ruling [[Golden King]] founded **The Lost Cause**, built a strong army, and misgoverns the realm. [[Afrit]] secretly causes his death and hopes to capture the crown. [[Eindil]] may emerge as a leader in the war that follows.

**Names to keep distinct:** The snapshot uses both **Huhun** in historical discussion and **Hunhun** in the working satellite list. Whether both refer to the same place is not proven in the provided text, so this vault preserves both and flags the ambiguity in [[Open Questions]].

## Aenor in ancient history

**Newly established:** [[Aen]] founded Aenor by unifying human kingdoms after the centuries-long [[War of Men]]. His victory began **Year 1 of the First Age**. The kingdom experienced centuries of relative prosperity, exploration, and the settlement of territories beyond the Middle Lands. Later misrule and revolt left it vulnerable to [[Volgan]], who seized the capital during the great war. The later [[Grand Alliance]] saved the kingdom.

Aenor's first settlement and expansion into the West Lands provoked **wars with pre-existing inhabitants**, creating hostilities still visible in the Fourth Age. The sea between West Lands and Middle Lands must be **significantly wider** than the supplied approximate Fourth Age map depicts, which makes ancient control more difficult.

**Fourth Age lineage:** [[Eindil]], directly descended as Aen's grandson through Ein, awakens in the Cold Lands and is eventually crowned king in Aenor.

## Second Age defeat

After the First Age, Aenor survives under a great king and tries to rebuild. Generations of raids, plagues, demonic portals, division among its former allies, and the Great Spirit [[Elzon]]'s necromancy reverse this recovery. **At the end of the [[The Second Age of Aoaz|Second Age]], Elzon takes Aenor and establishes his order**, inaugurating the Third Age.

The historical name of the capital during this period is **not yet confirmed**, even though the Fourth Age map-design seat is Parim. The later restoration of an Aenoran monarchy and the continuity of Aen's heirs before [[Eindil]]'s coronation remain to be written.

## The Third Age and restored monarchy

[[Elzon]] conquers Aenor in **Second Age year 2133**. Through the Third Age, men resist while he rules in [[Volgan]]'s name. He lacks a natural source of sustaining magic in Aenor and cannot permanently support his undead army without changing the land itself. The elven kingdoms do not send rescue armies to Aenor because of [[King Edran]]'s failed Ember Lands campaign. Elzon's eventual permanent death ends the occupation.

In the Fourth Age a genuinely good human king restores the monarchy, followed by a long peace under successive rulers. [[Afrit]] is praised and worshipped as a New God and secretly grows influential in the kingdom. See [[The Third Age of Aoaz]] and [[The Fourth Age of Aoaz]].

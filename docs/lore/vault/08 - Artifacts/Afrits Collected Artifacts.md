# Afrit's Collected Artifacts

**Established:** [[Afrit]] spends the Fourth Age collecting magical objects across Erdae. These contribute substantially to his strength, spiritual transformation, control over men, and supernatural defenses. The most important known object is Roka's [[Necklace of the Abyss]]. Other artifacts, including a ring forcibly removed by Eindil, also matter to the final defeat.

| Artifact | Status | Proposed function |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Necklace of the Abyss** | **Established** | Major source and amplifier of Afrit's power, linked with teleports and his private dimension in the climax |
| **Ring of Doors** | Proposed identity for an established ring | Makes escape or teleportation easier, lost when Eindil cuts off Afrit's finger |
| **Crown of Witnesses** | Proposed | Draws power from prepared royal oaths and institutions |
| **Bracelet of the First Seal** | Proposed | Maintains part of Afrit's personal magical defense |

A proposed staged palace battle removes each source through coordinated action rather than matching Afrit's full strength directly. Alleria exposes his eye, Eindil removes the ring, allies interfere with the crown and shield, and Golbran removes the necklace. Once these are gone, the once-human Afrit is mortal.

The number, exact names and abilities of his other artifacts remain open.

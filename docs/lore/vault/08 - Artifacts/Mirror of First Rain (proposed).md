# Mirror of First Rain

**Proposed name and mechanism:** An ancient prophetic fountain or basin in the realm of the Gala elves, under the guardianship of [[Lorien]]. During Eindil's many weeks among the Gala, Lorien uses this fountain to reveal the stranger's ancestry and possible future events.

The source idea is established: Lorien reveals the ancient history of Aen, Ein, Elzon and Volgan, and presents a vision of the dangers that Eindil must face. That vision directs Eindil toward Horos and Glo in the Cold Lands. The name *Mirror of First Rain* and the rule that visions show uncertain futures are proposed.

Lorien may be familiar with the First Men from memory or from other surviving records. The fountain need not reveal every secret directly. It may show the truth of bloodlines and images that Lorien interprets through experience.

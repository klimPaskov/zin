# Canon Policy

## Priority

1. The world creator's most recent explicit statement or correction has highest authority.
2. Original ZIN and Chaos Redux lore represented in the imported compendium supplies background unless later corrected.
3. Proposed character names, dialogue, year ranges, battle choreography, divine mechanics, and suggested explanations from the assistant remain proposals until adopted.
4. Gameplay and event specification ideas are separate from the history of ZIN unless explicitly made canon.

## How to read notes

- **Established:** Confirmed by the world creator or unmodified original canon that has not been superseded.
- **Proposed:** A creative addition that has not been accepted as fact.
- **Mixed:** A note containing both established plot points and proposals, labeled within the text.
- **Superseded:** Historical ideas retained only for source tracking.

## Current calendar

- First Age: **1,675 years**.
- Second Age: **2,133 years** and the longest historical age; ends when Elzon captures Aenor.
- Third Age: **at least 1,200 years**, with **1,260** the current proposed precise duration.
- Fourth Age: lasts **more than a thousand years**; **1,760** is a proposed precise duration.
- Eindil's age has **no current numeric limit**. The older 100,000-year freezing and intermediate 5,000-year maximum are no longer active. Afrit and Eindil are brothers born approximately in the same period.

## Metaphysical distinctions

- [[Zol]] remains immensely powerful and very slowly gains power, but the Fading and Erdae's local magic constrain manifestation. His late Fourth Age body is exceptional, temporary and costly.
- Great Spirit, Lesser Spirit, New God, Old God and Great Folk are distinct ranks or orders. Sovereign Spirit is not a separate rank.
- [[Afrit]] is publicly worshipped as a New God and effectively attains godlike power. Whether he belongs to the original divinity created by the Holy Seven remains undetermined.
- [[Elzon]] is a Great Spirit and Great Necromancer. He permanently dies after the Third Age. [[Volgan]] loses his manifested body in the Fourth Age, but the complete destruction of his essence has not yet been established.
- A strong mortal or spirit can be mistaken for a god by worshippers.

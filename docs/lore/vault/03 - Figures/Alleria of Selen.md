# Alleria of Selen

**Established:** Alleria is a celebrated **ranger and archer of Selen**, one of the Middle Lands territories. She helps Eindil in the final fight against Afrit.

At the royal palace she shoots Afrit in the eye, creating the opportunity for Eindil to cut off one of Afrit's fingers and take a ring. She continues to help him disable the enchanted artifacts that maintain Afrit's overwhelming strength.

**Proposed earlier role:** Alleria investigates the rumors about Eindil when most of Aenor denounces him. She leads an underground group loyal to the old royal line and helps recover him from the ravine after Afrit throws him from a cliff. Her training, family, age and eventual fate remain open.

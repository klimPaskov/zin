# Aurevan the Deliverer

**Status: Proposed public name for [[Afrit]].** The world creator specified that men do not know their divine king by the name Afrit, which belongs to his secret demonic identity. Aurevan is the working human-facing name, not yet approved.

Aurevan begins as the name of the celebrated Deliverer credited with ending Elzon's reign. He is worshipped as a New God. In the Fourth Age his sacred institutions gain influence over education, relief, courts and monarchy. He publicly becomes king after the Golden King's murder and proclaims himself First of His Name. The crown, worship, and title are historical realities even while his inner circle knows he is Afrit, son of Ein and brother of Eindil.

Alternative public names worth considering later: **Aurevan**, **Aelorin**, **Arvedan**, **Vaelorian**, **Elarion**. These are naming options, not simultaneous aliases.

# Ein

**Established:** Ein is the son of [[Aen]], first king and unifier of men. Ein is father to **[[Eindil]]** and **[[Afrit]]**, two brothers born approximately in the same period. He refused Afrit the royal succession. After Afrit betrayed his brother, the disappointed prince gradually turned toward corruption and demonic power.

The reason Ein rejected Afrit and the specific means by which Afrit betrayed Eindil remain open. A possible explanation is that Ein recognized a dangerous desire for absolute rule, but this is a proposal. Ein's own reign, the circumstances of his death, his other children, the name of the brothers' mother, and his place within the sequence of Aenor's early kings have not been recorded.

Eindil is **son of Ein, son of Aen**. The founder Aen had many descendants, but the two brothers belong to his particularly direct royal line. Their lifespan is not numerically fixed.

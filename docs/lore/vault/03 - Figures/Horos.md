# Horos

**Established:** Horos is a **Great Spirit**, lord of [[Castle of Yeldenne and Glo|Castle Yeldenne]] in the **Cold Lands**, and guardian of [[Castle of Yeldenne and Glo|Glo]]. He is an enormous knight who appears in yellow-green armor with unusual branching points or spikes on his helm, glowing blue eyes and a blue magical sword. His original three drawings are preserved in [[Horos Visual References]].

The crystal Glo, initially shown as a green jewel before his throne, is in fact **Horos's own heart**. It contains part of the power of **[[Glernaen]]**, a true New God of Power and Wisdom. Horos therefore carries both his Great Spirit strength and an entrusted divine power. He is able to resist the artifact-amplified Afrit far more effectively inside the castle than elsewhere.

In the late Fourth Age, Afrit arrives to seize Glo. Horos challenges him, exposes his origins as a brother of Eindil and warns him to leave. Afrit refuses. During their struggle, Horos seizes him, but Afrit teleports behind him and stabs the back of his neck. Eindil arrives, wounds Afrit and forces him to flee. Dying, Horos entrusts Glo and his blue sword to Eindil, physically removes his own crystalline heart and crumbles to dust. Eindil becomes Glo's bearer and the sword subsequently proves capable of destroying the direct undead servants of Zol.

**The castle's earlier rule that possession conveys control of Glo is superseded in part.** The guardian's willing transfer matters. Once Glo is removed from Horos and the castle, it can be carried by Eindil. The castle no longer guarantees ownership. The fate of Horos's underlying spirit after turning to dust is open, though his bodily death is established.

# The Golden King

**Established:** A famously poor late Fourth Age ruler of [[Middle Lands and Aenor|Aenor]]. He is preoccupied with wealth, sells offices, rewards favorites and governs badly despite inheriting strong institutions and a flourishing kingdom. He founds **the Lost Cause**, a political and military organization ostensibly intended to resist growing dark powers but also used to aggrandize himself.

By his reign, the publicly worshipped Deliverer, secretly [[Afrit]], has spent centuries strengthening control over Aenor's council, clergy, and major institutions. The Golden King is effectively one of his puppets. **Afrit secretly engineers the Golden King's death** and exploits the resulting succession crisis to have himself proclaimed monarch under a human public name, proposed [[Aurevan the Deliverer|Aurevan]]. The conspiracy is not broadly understood by ordinary worshippers.

The Golden King's personal name, exact years, relatives and political dealings with Afrit have yet to be established. The Lost Cause survives in altered form and can support the struggle led by [[Eindil]].

# Proposed characters of the First Age

The following characters were created as candidate additions on 17 October 2026. Their names, biographies, orders, and actions are **not** established lore unless adopted. The owner already established the king who founded the Grand Alliance, King Lorien of the Gala elves, and Elzon's defense, although the new king's name is undecided. Elzon has since been confirmed as a **Great Spirit**, the **Great Necromancer**, and a loyal servant of Volgan. His earlier biography remains partly unknown.

| Candidate | Suggested position | Important story function |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [[King Edran|Edran the Gatherer]] | King of Aenor in exile | Builds the Grand Alliance, wins Dardan, orders the disastrous Dead Lands offensive |
| **Sirael** | Elected war-speaker of elves in the Magical Forest | Keeps the democratic Magical Lands distinct from the hereditary elf kings |
| **Taryn of Gunwaith** | West Lands commander | Demands political guarantees before aiding the kingdom that historically colonized the West |
| **Senra of Triton's Islands** | Island fleet captain | Makes the multinational alliance's sea supply and far-flung troop movements possible |
| **Brunek of the Stone Roads** | Dwarven military engineer | Holds the Dardan crossings beside giants without establishing a dwarven homeland |
| **Rovek of Dondor** | Independent Dark Lands leader | Shows that the Dark Lands were not united behind Volgan |
| **Ithren of the Threshold** | Allied Great Spirit associated with boundaries | Assists Volgan's banishment and becomes bound to the ward at Dardan |
| **Maerul of the Deep Stone** | Allied Great Spirit | Resists the Damned Spirits and helps seal underground ruptures |
| **Vareth the Pale** | Commander of corrupted elves | Represents the corruption and divided loyalties of elf peoples in Volgan's host |

## Story cautions

- Do not turn Sirael into a hereditary queen. The Magical Forest elves were previously described as democratic.
- Do not assert that all of Dondor or the Dark Lands fought for the Grand Alliance.
- Do not assign the dwarves a home region yet, since that question remains open.
- Allied Great Spirits may be bound or shattered, but the established cosmology calls them immortal.
- Do not turn Ithren's proposed ritual into an act of the Old Gods or Holy Seven.

# Damned Spirits

## Established direction

**Damned Spirits** are a proposed or developing kind of spirit associated with **very deep underground places**, far below the ordinary inhabited surface. This detail was expressly introduced by the owner.

They are also called *Blighted Spirits*. beings transformed through curses, corruption, or disaster. Damned and blighted spirits are the same thing.

## Undecided

- Are they all spirits in the formal sense, or do some belong to another order?
- Are there both Great and Lesser Damned Spirits?
- Were they born in the deep world, driven underground, imprisoned, or transformed there?
- Do they have distinct societies, hierarchies, or territories?
- Do they have anything to do with [[Volgans Dead Lands|Volgan]] or the dead of [[Zols Mistake|Zol's Mistake]]?

## Optional motifs for later use

Deep fissures, sunless caverns, sealed ruins, forgotten roots of mountains, and pressure or darkness-driven changes. These are **ideas**, not established locations, origins, or abilities.

## Persistence into the Second Age

Volgan released Damned Spirits from very deep underground during the First Age war. The user's later Second Age account leaves Erdae in a state of disorder, and some of these older threats may remain within subterranean places. Their precise allegiances and participation in [[Elzon]]'s second conquest are **proposed** in [[The Second Age of Erdae]] and should not automatically be assigned to every Damned Spirit.

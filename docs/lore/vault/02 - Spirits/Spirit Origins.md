# Spirit Origins

> **Status: proposed taxonomy.** These names were suggested and retained for possible later adoption. Only Great and Lesser are confirmed formal spirit ranks.

| Proposed origin | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Elder Spirits** | Spirits so ancient that their beginnings precede many races and civilizations. |
| **Worldborn Spirits** | Spirits that formed through the nature of places or forces, such as forests, mountains, oceans, or storms. |
| **Godwrought Spirits** | Spirits that were deliberately made by an Old God or New God for some purpose. |

Origin is separate from **rank** and **nature**. A spirit could theoretically be an Elder Great Spirit and also a [[Spirit Natures|Wild Spirit]], if later canon allows that combination.

[[Volgan]] is confirmed to be ancient, and the phrase **Elder Great Spirit** is a useful working descriptor. His literal place of origin is not established. Do not assume he was Worldborn or Godwrought.

# Spirit Natures

> **Status: developing classification.** Proposed nature labels are preserved here. They are not extra formal ranks.

| Nature | Status | Description |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Wild Spirits** | Proposed | Beings associated with forests, mountains, beasts, wilderness, and places outside civilization. |
| **Veiled Spirits** | Proposed | Spirits associated with dreams, memory, hidden things, visions, or the unseen. |
| **Elemental Spirits** | Proposed | Beings associated with fire, wind, water, stone, ice, lightning, and other forces. |
| **Damned Spirits** | **Accepted direction, details open** | Supernatural beings inhabiting **very deep underground** reaches. See [[Damned Spirits]]. |

The earlier suggestion **Blighted Spirits** described entities changed by corruption, curses, or disaster. The owner instead introduced **Damned Spirits** and associated them with the deep underground. Corruption may be relevant but has **not** been confirmed as a necessary origin for all Damned Spirits.

"Sovereign Spirits" was an earlier assistant proposal and is explicitly **rejected as a nature category**. Great Spirits may hold dominions, such as [[Volgan]].

See [[Spirits and Their Ranks]] for the real two-tier rank distinction, [[Spirit Origins]] for origins, and [[Spirit Manifestation States]] for how spirits appear.

# Spirit Manifestation States

> **Status: proposed descriptive system.** These are possible conditions, not additional ranks of beings.

| State | Working definition |
| --- | --- |
| **Embodied** | Maintains a stable physical body and can interact directly with the material world. |
| **Unbound** | Exists without a permanent physical body and can move between places. |
| **Bound** | Its presence is attached to a specific place, item, person, or other anchor. |
| **Dormant** | Exists but is inactive or rarely able to act. |
| **Shattered** | Exists in dispersed fragments after losing its prior unity or identity. |

These names have **not** been made canonical. In particular, do not use them to imply that [[Zol]] is weak or inactive merely because he has no active body. Old Gods are subject to their own limitations under [[The Fading]].

# Change Log

## 17 October 2026: Cosmology and First Age

- Established the Supreme Creator, Holy Seven, Old and New Gods, Great and Lesser Spirits, and Aoaz's peoples.
- Zol is an Old God limited by the Fading and the local magic. Volgan is a Great Spirit; Damned Spirits live deep underground.
- Replaced an older thematic age scheme with the numbered Ages. First Age fixed at 1,675 years, beginning when Aen wins the War of Men.
- Expanded Aenor's founding, western expansion, Volgan's invasion, Dardan, Edran's Grand Alliance, and Elzon's defense of the Dead Lands.

## 17 October 2026: Second and Third Ages

- Fixed the Second Age at 2,133 years. Recorded disease, famines, progress in warfare, medicine and farming, Edran's death of illness, and elves' refusal to aid Aenor against Elzon.
- Confirmed Elzon as Great Spirit and Great Necromancer, creator of undead hosts, conqueror of Aenor, and later permanently destroyed after long occupation.
- Recorded Volgan's return from the Void and inability to leave Dead Lands during the Third Age.
- Established Varner as Lesser Spirit, Golbran as small purple companion, and Magical Lands remaining unconquered.
- Afrit becomes the publicly worshipped New God credited with saving Aenor after Elzon's death, while his true divine classification remains unconfirmed.

## 17 October 2026: Fourth Age and chronology revision

- Third Age revised to last **at least 1,200 years**, with **1,260** a working precise count. Former **128-year** model superseded.
- Eindil's former **5,000-year limit** revoked as a rough estimate. An even older 100,000-year estimate also stays superseded.
- Afrit and Eindil confirmed as **brothers**, both sons of Ein and grandsons of Aen, approximately the same age.
- The Fourth Age now contains over a millennium of prosperity, Afrit's concealed control through religion and royal councils, the theft of Roka's necklace, the Golden King's murder and Afrit's open kingship.
- Eindil's journey from Cold Lands through West Lands, Islands of the South, Lorien's Gala elves and Horos's Castle Yeldenne has been chronicled. Horos's original three drawings preserved.
- The final wars pit Aenor against Western, Northern, Eastern, Volgan and Zol-linked forces. Volgan's embodied form is destroyed, Zol retreats after Lorien's sacrifice, and Varner dies.
- Afrit attacks his brother, retakes Aenor, and is later killed after Eindil, Alleria and Golbran strip his artifacts away. Golbran sacrifices his last strength to save Eindil, who returns with the Necklace of the Abyss and is crowned.

Dates, secondary names and magical mechanisms not directly specified by the creator remain proposed and are listed in the canon registers.

## 18 October 2026: Eindil naming revision

- Renamed **Einendil** to **Eindil** at the creator’s request. This affects his personal note, journey note, all current histories, genealogies, Obsidian links, timelines, and consolidated lore.
- All other character facts are unchanged, including Eindil and Afrit being brothers, both sons of Ein and grandsons of Aen, approximately the same age with no fixed numerical maximum.
- The older original source and superseded Third Age draft are preserved unchanged as historical documents. Their previous spelling is not current canon.

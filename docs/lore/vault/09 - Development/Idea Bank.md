# Idea Bank

> **None of the ideas in this note should be presented as established canon without adoption.** They are preserved from the earlier lore discussion so no potentially useful proposal is lost.

## The Seven's mythical names

The Seven Before Names, The Seven Who Never Were, The Unremembered Seven, The First Hands, The Nameless Seven, The Seven Beyond Time, The Forgotten Makers. See [[Holy Seven]] for interpretations and the suggested division between ancient and skeptical usage.

## Possible god-domain structure

Old Gods may embody fundamental principles of existence, while New Gods govern narrower aspects of those principles. Zol could govern **Death itself**, and some New God might govern mourning or funerals. There is no actual named New God of mourning in canon.

## Possible explanations for the Fading

The Old Gods may have become so bound to the underlying forces of reality that they can no longer freely move through and command the material world. This idea is unconfirmed and must not be turned into the factual cause of [[The Fading]].

Conflicting faiths might believe Old Gods died, slept, withdrew, or were replaced. The actual historical explanation need not be known to worshippers.

## Potential spirit classification language

- **Origins:** Elder, Worldborn, Godwrought. See [[Spirit Origins]].
- **Natures:** Wild, Veiled, Elemental, Damned. See [[Spirit Natures]].
- **Conditions:** Embodied, Unbound, Bound, Dormant, Shattered. See [[Spirit Manifestation States]].
- **Damned Spirits:** sealed cavern civilizations, sunless roots of mountains, ruined underground temples, curses, lost spirits. These are only motifs.

Earlier **Blighted Spirits** meant cursed or corrupted beings, and the idea was redirected toward deep-dwelling **Damned Spirits**. The separate "Sovereign Spirits" classification was explicitly rejected. Do not add it back.

## Possible Zol-related political interpretation

The map-design capital **Furviatlon** might house a hierarchy of priests, New God servants, or spirit administrators acting for Zol. No such office or agent has yet been canonized. His servants' sustenance mechanism is likewise **unexplained**.

## Possible rule for divinity

A spirit that attracts worship is often mistaken for a god, but worship alone may never grant true godhood. This would make the difference between [[Volgan]] and the [[New Gods]] clear while still allowing one to surpass another in effective might. **Ascension rules remain undecided.**

## Possible ongoing story hooks

- Different religions disagree on the nature of Zol's growing power and dependence on servants.
- A god who serves Zol has reasons to conceal that allegiance.
- Remnants of the cult of the unknown New God overpowered by Volgan survive in a forgotten region.
- A Damned Spirit emerges from the deep underground and is worshipped as a New God.

All these hooks are optional and should not be interpreted as events that already happened.

## Second Age optional development (17 October 2026)

- [[Second Age Proposed Timeline]] now reflects the **established 2,133-year length**. Only internal phase dates and sample lifespan calculations remain proposals.
- [[The Second Age of Erdae]] contains possible named events including the *Council of Empty Chairs*, *War of the Black Roads*, *Burning Gate of Nezer*, *War of the Hollow Crown*, and optional plague names.
- Possible mechanisms for the [[The Return of Volgan|return from the Void]] include weakening the Dardan boundary and using magic Elzon drew from [[Zol]]. The mechanism is not established even though the return itself is.
- *Night of a Thousand Oaths* is a proposed explanation for [[Afrits Ascension|Afrit's ascension]].
- *Pale Regency* is a suggested later name for [[Elzons Order|Elzon's Order]].
- An escaped royal archivist could provide genealogical continuity into the Fourth Age, when [[Eindil]] appears as Aen's direct grandson.

## Third Age ideas now developed

The detailed Third Age includes suggested **Black Roots** as Elzon's attempt to convert Aenor into self-sustaining necromantic territory, **Lantern Chain** as Varner and Golbran's decentralized anti-curse network, **War of Unbinding** as the final liberation campaign, **Last Tithe** as Elzon's permanent destruction via a dangerous conduit to Zol, **Harion the Restorer** as the first good restored human king, and **Afrit the Deliverer** as the publicly revered savior. Names, dates, and mechanics remain proposals except where the owner has confirmed the broad outcome.

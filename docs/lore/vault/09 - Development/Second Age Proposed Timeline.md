# Second Age Working Timeline

**Canonical year count:** The Second Age is exactly **2,133 years**, ending with the fall of [[Middle Lands and Aenor|Aenor]] to [[Elzon]] in **2A 2133**. A prior *2,300-year model* was a draft and is **superseded**. The 2,133-year total is now established, but the phase boundaries below are speculative.

| Second Age years | Proposed period | Broad development |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 to 240 | The Long Mourning | Edran's recovery effort, rebuilding, disease and the eventual death of the king |
| 241 to 660 | Wars of the Remnants | Persistent orc and goblin campaigns, rising local autonomy, elven refusal to renew the old alliance |
| 661 to 1,150 | The Hard Centuries | Repeated hunger and plague alongside major improvements in agriculture, irrigation, quarantine, medical treatment, and military defenses |
| 1,151 to 1,550 | The Open Gates | Nether demonic raids, Elzon's networks, Afrit's worship and influence |
| 1,551 to 1,900 | The Return | Elzon restores Volgan's spiritual presence from the Void and uses ancient magic tied to Zol |
| 1,901 to 2,080 | The War from Within | Plagues, subversion, necromancy, and internal invasions weaken Aenor |
| 2,081 to 2,133 | The Fallen Crown | Final conquest, late ascension of Afrit, Elzon occupies Aenor |

Only the last date and full duration have been established. The exact year of Edran's death, Volgan's return and Afrit's Second Age ascension are open. The long age includes considerable cultural, agricultural, medical, and military development, not just uninterrupted suffering.

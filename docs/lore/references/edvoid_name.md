# A new name for Edvoid

**Status:** Proposal for the owner's approval (Q-10). **Date:** 24 October 2026.
**Companions:** [pantheon.md](pantheon.md), [calendar.md](calendar.md), [knowledge_layers.md](knowledge_layers.md) (C24, C25). **Data:** `edvoidName` in [tools/content/data/world/pantheon.json](../../../tools/content/data/world/pantheon.json).
**Sources read:** the Edvoid drawing and card (index 62 and 63) and the Beast card (index 55) in `docs/lore/sources/bestiary_cards_part2.md`; the vault's `05 - Geography/Zols Mistake.md`, `Aoaz and Its Regions.md`, `03 - Figures/Zol.md`, `07 - History/War of the Last Crowns.md`, `The Fourth Age of Aoaz.md`, `Fourth Age Chronology.md`, `09 - Development/Names and Retcons.md`; `docs/lore/names_traceability.md` section 3; the regional files `docs/lore/regions/magical_lands.md` and `cold_lands.md`; the world bible's culture entries and section 5; `docs/specs/02_lore/06_languages.md` and `tools/content/data/world/languages.json`; `tools/content/data/world/reserved_names.json`.

This is a design document. The servant's identity is sealed (C25): nothing in this file, including the name, may be copied into player text.

## 1. What is fixed

- Edvoid is an evil spirit made by Zol, "a former minion made by Zol", who lives in Šodolôrō (written *Šodõlõrõõl* on the card), feeds on the souls of others, grows stronger by it and wants the whole of Aoaz; "everyone fears him" (card 63, translated). His ordinary form is a black shrouded bulk with pale, bony, clawed limbs and a gleaming slit, about two and a half times a man's height (cards 62 and 63).
- Šodolôrō lies within **Zol's Mistake**, the black north: a wasteland and every black island of the **Ocean of Zol** (E, vault).
- The being who appears in the War of the Last Crowns is **Zol's most loyal servant, a Great Spirit**; the owner has identified it as Edvoid, and **Edvoid is to receive a new name** (E, owner, 24 October 2026; Q-10).
- The servant's identity is sealed in every layer of player text, before and after it appears (C25); after its appearance text may call it only "a Great Spirit of the black north" or similar (C24).
- The drawing's sea label "Ocean of Edvoid" and the region label "Edvoid's Mistake" are already superseded by **Ocean of Zol** and **Zol's Mistake** (E, vault, 24 October 2026).
- The card's age for Edvoid is not used: no being's exact age is stated anywhere (E, owner rule).

## 2. Who fears him most

Zol's Mistake has no language of its own (`06_languages.md` section 2), so the name must come from a neighbour. Four peoples face the black north.

| Candidate | Language | What ties them to the black north | Weight |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **The Leren of the North Lands**: the Leren of the Strait (Lerendor, Eekedor, R'en, the Low-ness Islands and three more) and the twenty-one Free Crowns of the North | Leren | The North Lands lie closest: Zol's forces come "from the black northern wasteland, through islands and straits toward the settled kingdoms" (vault). The island fishermen who saw the black ships in 4A 1744 fish among the black islands, most likely from the North Lands' coasts and isles (inference from the map: the old strategic region 262 joins the Sea of Ren, off R'en, to the Ocean of Edvoid). Lerendor "fears ... from the black north, whatever the fishermen report" (world bible). In the last war the North Lands withdraw from Aenor's alliance. The sea that separates them from the black islands is the old map's "Sea of Ren", merged with the Ocean of Edvoid in one strategic region. | Strongest |
| The Sylvan elves of Herrodon and Andos Enda | Sylvan | The brown highland of Herrodon "against the Ocean of Zol"; the still sea of the north coast "which sailors fear more than storms" (`magical_lands.md`, P) | Strong, but a mountain coast with few sailors, and the elves are Great Folk who fear little by name |
| Woqood of the Noris desert | Noric | "A north-coast desert kingdom whose wells face the Ocean of Zol" (world bible) | One small kingdom; "rumours from the sea" only |
| The Cold Lands | Coldic | No shared coast on today's map (`cold_lands.md`) | Weak |

**The Leren fear him most.** They are the most numerous people on the black sea's shore (28 countries), the only ones who sail it for a living, the source of every witness the vault names, and the realm whose politics the fear changes in the last war. Their faith already has the habit the name needs: the Strait Litany names the ships lost each year and "the storm that took them, which they do not call a god" (world bible). A people who refuse to give a terror its own name call it by what it does.

## 3. The proposal: Kusven

**Kusven** (said *KOOS-ven*, stress on the first syllable as in all Leren words): "the grave-wind".

| Step | Form | Note |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Elements | Leren *kus* "grave" + Leren *ven* "wind" | Both are regular Leren words in `languages.json` (*kus* from Old Ainic *kuth*, *ven* from Old Ainic *wen*). |
| Compound | *kus-ven* → **Kusven** | Leren builds names modifier before head and closes the compound (`06_languages.md` 3.3): "the wind of graves". |
| Old Ainic source | **\*Kuthwen** | Never attested: no Ainic text names the servant. The Leren form is its regular reflex: *th > s* gives *kuswen*, *w > v* gives *kusven*; the remaining Leren laws (*ae > ee*, loss of a final vowel, final *b d g > p t k*) do not apply. |
| Fit to Leren | closed syllables *kus.ven*, no doubled letters needed, ends in a consonant | Leren allows any single consonant at the end of a word and forbids a final open vowel; the name obeys both. |
| Common Speech calque | *Kutven* (Aenoran *kut* grave + *ven* wind) | What a southern scholar would write if he ever heard the word. |

**Why "the grave-wind".** The card's Edvoid feeds on souls; the Ainic dictionary has no word for breath or soul, and this proposal takes wind, *wen*, to serve for both, as many tongues do (P): a Leren saying would be that the dying on the north coast give their breath to the wind. A wind that takes breath into the grave is how a seafaring people would name a soul-eater they will not name: by the cold that comes off the black north and the quiet after it. The name also fits the drawing, a black shape in a storm of smudged strokes.
**How the word lives (P).** In Leren speech *kusven* is first a common word: the cold, breathless wind that comes off the black sea in still weather, and the reason a Leren house shuts its shutters on the side facing north when someone is dying. That the grave-wind is a being, a Great Spirit made by Zol, and that its name is Kusven, is sealed authorial truth (C25). Whether the common word may appear in player text at all, as Leren sailors' dread with no link to the servant, is an owner question (section 7): the default is that it does not, because a word shown before the servant appears would hint at it (knowledge-layer rule 6).

## 4. Two alternates

| Name | Derivation | Gloss | For and against |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Kusferl** (*KOOS-ferl*) | Leren *kus* "grave" + Leren *ferl* "lord" (the irregular Leren word for lord, as in the royal name Ferlin) | "the grave-lord" | Harder and more royal; it reads as a title rather than a terror the Leren avoid naming, and it echoes the common royal stem *Ferl-* of the Leren kings. |
| **Kourven** (*KOOR-ven*) | Leren *kour* "black" + *ven* "wind", from Old Ainic *\*kaurwen* (*au > ou*, *w > v*) | "the black wind" | Simple and dark, but "black" says less than "grave" about a soul-eater, and it sits close to *kaur*, "the black", an epithet Leren personal names already use. |

## 5. The sea

The drawing's "Ocean of Edvoid" has already been renamed **Ocean of Zol** in the vault (E, 24 October 2026), and every regional file and spec now uses that name. Naming the sea after the servant would put a sealed name on the map, which every player reads from the first day, and would break C25.

**Proposal.** The chart name stays **Ocean of Zol**. The Leren have their own name for it: **Kusmeel** (*KOOS-meel*), "the grave-sea", from Leren *kus* "grave" + *meel* "sea" (Old Ainic *\*kuthmael*, *th > s*, *ae > ee*). It shares the element *kus* with the being's name only as two words for graves share it, and says nothing about the servant. It can appear in Leren event text as their sailors' name for the sea (P), since it reveals nothing.
**If the owner prefers the sea named after the being**, the chart name would be **Ocean of Kusven**, and C25 would have to change: the servant's name would become public from the first day, while its nature stayed sealed.

## 6. Collision check

The three names and the sea name were compared, with letters only and case, accents and hyphens ignored, against `reserved_names.json` (established and superseded names), every sample and established name of the 24 languages in `languages.json`, every capitalised word in `docs/lore/`, `docs/specs/02_lore/`, `docs/generation/name_assignments.json` and `docs/design/countries.csv` (8,363 forms), counting an exact match or a match within one edit. **Kusven, Kusferl, Kourven, Kusmeel and Kutven are all clear.** None is known to the author from another work of fiction, a game or a real myth or religion, and none contains "-waith".

## 7. Follow-ups once a name is chosen

These are for the owners of the files named; this document changes none of them.

1. `docs/lore/references/knowledge_layers.md`: C25 records the new name; rule `kl_sealed_021` gains the name as a term rule (case-insensitive, whole word) beside `bw_superseded_names_002` for "Edvoid".
2. `tools/content/data/world/reserved_names.json`: the chosen name and both alternates go to `doNotGenerate` or a sealed list, so that the generator never produces them or a name within one edit; "Edvoid" stays in `superseded` with `current` changed from "Zol" to the new name for the being (the region and sea keep "Zol's Mistake" and "Ocean of Zol").
3. `docs/lore/names_traceability.md` section 3 lists the renames still to do on the map rebuild: strategic regions 256 and 262 of the old map, the AI theatre `zin_theater_ocean_of_edvoid` (to `zin_theater_ocean_of_zol`), the state labels "Edvoids Mistake" and "Edvoids Mistake Teste", and the bestiary card (index 63), which takes the new name in its English text.
4. The vault: `09 - Development/Names and Retcons.md` and Open Questions Q-03 and Q-10 record the decision.

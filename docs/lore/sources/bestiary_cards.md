## What the cards change or add

Consistent concepts are kept and enter the bestiary (R-40) as established where the card is the owner's statement and no vault fact contradicts it, and as needing the owner where they conflict. The conflicts and missing foundations are owner questions Q-13 to Q-24 in `docs/zin-handoff/OWNER-QUESTIONS.md`: card ages against the Ages (millions of years for Satan, Roka, Edvoid), borrowed names from other works, gunpowder (the Orc card's cannons), Gorbatik and Golbran, the demon king's home and soul pacts, Coniferons and the ents, dwarf and dwarf-goblin homelands, Gektor killing Great Spirits, goblins and the cold, single kings over trolls and goblins, Elzon's home, Horos's underground castle, unplaced card places, and the real-world religious symbols drawn on the Demon card.

# Bestiary cards and drawings, part 1 (photos 0 to 32)

Transcription of the owner's handwritten bestiary cards and figure drawings, photographed on 23 October 2026, indices 0 to 32 in sorted file-name order of `C:/Users/klimp/Documents/ZIN/World/Erdae/Drawings/` (`IMG_20261023_112647.jpg` to `IMG_20261023_114743.jpg`).
The photos are the owner's private originals and are not copied, resized or embedded here; this file holds text transcriptions and factual descriptions only.

Conventions:

- Card text is transcribed line by line as written, keeping the owner's spelling. The owner's cursive `s` often looks like `r` or `v`; where the intended letter is clear from Estonian it is transcribed as `s`, and the shape is noted only when the reading is in doubt.
- `[?word?]` marks an uncertain reading, `[illegible]` an unreadable part.
- The main setting was called Erdae (written **Arda** on the cards) and is now named **Aoaz**. English prose here says Aoaz; transcriptions and literal translations keep the original word with a note.
- **Vez'nan** (any spelling) is an old name of **Volgan** and is translated "Volgan (written Vez'nan)".
- **Edvoid** keeps its written form with the note "(name to be replaced, owner question Q-10)".
- Canon-check labels: `consistent`, `new (no conflict)`, `conflict: ...`, `needs owner`. Card years and life spans are known to be unreliable; each is compared with the Ages chronology (`docs/lore/vault/07 - History/Ages of Aoaz.md`: First Age 1,675 years, Second Age 2,133, Third Age at least 1,200 (1,260 proposed), Fourth Age over 1,000 (1,760 proposed), so roughly 6,800 years of reckoned history, with men living "thousands of years" before Aen).
- Owner question ids refer to `docs/zin-handoff/OWNER-QUESTIONS.md`. Map-label checks use `docs/name_table.md` (transcription of the drawn map). Vault paths use the file names current on 24 October 2026 (the vault files were renamed from "Erdae" to "Aoaz" that day).

## Questions for the owner

Consequential conflicts and open choices raised by cards 0 to 32. None is decided here.

1. **Satan's name and seat** (card 5, drawing 17; extends Q-07). The card names the demon king "Satan" (a real-world religious name) and seats him in "Teitonis/Tritonis", while the vault seats the King of Demons in the Nether of the Dead Lands. What is the king's name, and where does he live? Can he bind mortals by pacts that give powers in life for service after death, given that Zol is the God of Death?
2. **Creature ages beyond the chronology** (cards 5, 8, 13, 25; also 12). The cards give Satan 11,357,440 years, Roka 9,405,007, Horos 185,700, Smash 48,500 and sea serpents up to 10,000, against roughly 6,800 reckoned years of the four ages plus some thousands before Aen. Should these ages be dropped, scaled into the chronology, or kept as in-world legend (and if so, how old is the world)?
3. **Horos's castle** (card 25). The card says Horos lives in the underground castle of "Tol Hithi"; the vault establishes Castle Yeldenne in the Cold Lands. Is Tol Hithi another name for Yeldenne, part of it (an underground hall), or to be dropped? It is Tolkien-derived.
4. **The rhino-headed people** (cards 8, 18; vault open question). Is "Rhino-Head" the people's name or a placeholder? The card calls them always evil and usually solitary, yet Roka keeps a castle where he is served; and "always evil" conflicts with the vault's rule that monster peoples are diverse, not uniformly aligned. What is their rank (Roka is immortal)?
5. **Kings of whole peoples** (cards 13, 29). Smash is "king of the trolls" and a hobgoblin is "king of the goblins", but the vault says trolls, orcs and goblins are independent, regional and serve no single master (the Troll Forest and the goblin holds belong to nobody). Are these kings of one realm each, or overlords of their whole kind? Where is Smash's "Holos"?
6. **Borrowed names** (cards 12, 15, 25, 27, 32; extends Q-06). Belegaer, Tol Hithi, The Shire, hobbit/kääbik, warg and Olog come from Tolkien. Which are kept, and which get original names?
7. **Gunpowder** (card 20). Orcs use cannons "when they can". Gunpowder appears nowhere in the vault. Does it exist in Aoaz, since which age, and who has it? This affects the HOI4 technology model.
8. **Unplaced places** (cards 12, 13, 29, 30). Where on the map are the Trenin/Irenin Sea, Holos, the caves of Ellenmoor and Salomonanos? Should Rush Forest keep the superseded "Rush" in its name?
9. **Immortal creatures outside the spirit ranks** (cards 7, 8, 30). Sand Golems, Krors and Roka are called immortal. Are they spirits (and of which rank), made beings, or immortal Great Folk?
10. **Unidentified drawings and names** (drawings 0, 1, 4, 26; card 27). Drawing 0 is a classical statue study and drawing 1 a pointed-eared portrait; drawings 4 and 26 are small armed warriors. Do any of them depict ZIN peoples? What is the creature written "wuukid" on the Human card?

# Bestiary cards and drawings, part 2 (photos 33 to 66)

Transcription of the owner's handwritten bestiary cards and figure drawings, photographed on 23 October 2026, indices 33 to 65 in sorted file-name order of `C:/Users/klimp/Documents/ZIN/World/Erdae/Drawings/` (`IMG_20261023_114823.jpg` to `IMG_20261023_120307.jpg`), plus index 66, the owner's world map (`IMG_20261023_121133.jpg`).
The photos are the owner's private originals and are not copied, resized or embedded here; this file holds text transcriptions and factual descriptions only.
Part 1 (`bestiary_cards_part1.md`) covers indices 0 to 32 and uses the same conventions.

Conventions:

- Card text is transcribed line by line as written, keeping the owner's spelling. The owner's cursive `s` often looks like `r` (for example "koobaster" for "koobastes", "kasvavad ... pikkuseks" written like "kavvavad ... pikkureks"); where the intended letter is clear from Estonian it is transcribed as `s`, and the shape is noted only when the reading is in doubt.
- Line breaks follow the physical lines of the card. Decorative initials (the owner draws large ornamented first letters) are transcribed as ordinary letters.
- `[?word?]` marks an uncertain reading, `[illegible]` an unreadable part.
- The main setting was called Erdae (written **Arda** on the cards) and is now named **Aoaz**. English prose here says Aoaz; transcriptions and literal translations keep the original word with a note.
- **Vez'nan** (any spelling) is an old name of **Volgan** and is translated "Volgan (written Vez'nan)".
- **Edvoid** keeps its written form with the note "(name to be replaced, owner question Q-10)".
- Canon-check labels: `consistent`, `new (no conflict)`, `conflict: ...`, `needs owner`. Card years and life spans are known to be unreliable; each is compared with the Ages chronology (`docs/lore/vault/07 - History/Ages of Aoaz.md`: First Age 1,675 years, Second Age 2,133, Third Age at least 1,200 (1,260 proposed), Fourth Age over 1,000 (1,760 proposed), so roughly 6,800 years of reckoned history, with men living "thousands of years" before Aen).
- Vault paths are relative to `docs/lore/vault/`. Owner question ids refer to `docs/zin-handoff/OWNER-QUESTIONS.md`.
- Drawing subjects are identified from the matching card that follows them when the drawing itself carries no title; such identifications are marked "probably".

## 33. Gorbatik, figure on a rock (IMG_20261023_114823)

**Kind**: drawing.
Coloured-pencil scene, landscape format. A small, round-headed, grey-violet humanoid with a smooth hairless dome head, large round yellow eyes with dark pupils and a wide dark mouth or mask-like lower face stands on the summit of a large dark brown-grey rock mound.
He raises both fists at shoulder height; each fist is wrapped in a large swirling cloud of blue magic. Yellow lightning or light bursts radiate from the rock around and below him, and more yellow sparks flare at the lower left.
Behind him a large pale blue and green dome or sphere of light (a shield or aura) frames the whole figure; small blue bubbles float near his hands.
The figure matches the small 1.4 m figure on the Gorbatik card (index 34) and is probably Gorbatik casting a spell; the colouring (grey-violet) also fits the vault's description of Golbran as small and purple.

**Transcription**: none (no text on the front; faint writing from the back of the sheet shows through at upper right and is not legible).

**Translation**: none.

**Claims**: none written. Visual only: a small grey-violet magic user who fights with blue hand magic and a protective dome of light.

**Canon check**:

- Small purple-grey magic-wielding humanoid: `consistent` with Golbran, "the small, purple, humanoid servant and companion of Varner" who uses magic (`03 - Figures/Golbran.md`), if Gorbatik is Golbran (see index 34).

**Kind**: card.

Reading notes: "mägedes" is written "mägeder"; "suremata" (immortal) is the owner's form of standard "surematu"; the card reads "suremata" or "suremotu" and "suremata" is preferred.

**Translation**:

> GORBATIK
> Gorbatik is Varner's helper. He lives in the mountains of Magic
> Mountain. Gorbatik is a wizard and very wise. He knows very many
> spells. Most of all he kills demons. He is immortal and has already

**Claims**:

- Gorbatik is Varner's helper.
- Habitat: the mountains of Magic Mountain (the Magic Mountains).
- He is a wizard and very wise; he knows very many spells.
- He kills demons more than anything else.
- He is immortal.
- Age: 197,000 years.
- Height 1.4 m.

**Canon check**:

- Varner's helper: `consistent` with Golbran, Varner's servant and companion (`03 - Figures/Golbran.md`, `03 - Figures/Varner.md`). `09 - Development/Names and Retcons.md` records that the owner's variant "Gorbat" is the same character as Golbran, "not an additional being". Whether "Gorbatik" is a third spelling of Golbran or a separate helper: `needs owner`.
- Magic Mountains: `consistent`; the Magic Mountains are in the Magical Lands (`05 - Geography/Aoaz and Its Regions.md`), Varner's land.
- Wizard, wise, many spells: `consistent` with Golbran's magic (he strips the Necklace of the Abyss from Afrit by magic).
- Kills demons most of all: `new (no conflict)`.
- Immortal: `conflict: Golbran dies in the final palace battle, transferring his last life and magic to Eindil (03 - Figures/Golbran.md, 07 - History/Timeline.md)`. "Immortal" could mean "does not age" rather than "cannot be killed"; `needs owner` if Gorbatik is Golbran.
- 197,000 years: `needs owner`. It is about 29 times the reckoned ages (about 6,800 years). An ageless spirit could predate the ages, but Golbran's species and origin are unknown in the vault, and the vault already rejected an analogous 100,000-year figure for the Ainu as "not an active chronology fact" (`Names and Retcons.md`). Treat as unreliable.
- 1.4 m: `consistent` with "small".

## 36. Goblin (IMG_20261023_114932)

**Kind**: card.
Title "GOBLIN" in large grey stone-textured letters.
Size panel: a green goblin with a bow against a bar marked **1,6m**, beside a man outline with a bar marked **1,8m**.
Illustrations: a wooden-hafted axe with green bindings (top right) and a wooden bow with green grip wraps and an arrow (lower left).

**Transcription**:

```
GOBLIN
Goblinid on nagu orgid, kuid nad on väiksemad ja nõrgemad,
aga targemad ja tehnoloogiliselt arenenumad. Nad elavad mägede
sees või koobastes. Goblinitele meeldib
väga kuld ning hinnalised ehted või esemed.
Neil on väga halb tervis, kuna nad [?söövad?]
mädanema läinud toitu ja hapnikku nad üldse
ei saa. Nad elavad 40-60 aastat ja kasvavad
1,4-1,8m pikkuseks. Päike teeb nad uimaseks ja
nõrgaks ning et kiiresti liikuda maapinnal, kasutavad nad
warge. Kaklevad nad relvadega (kirves, mõõk, vibu,
nood, oda jne) ning kannavad kaitseid. Nad elavad
seal, kus on mägesid ja koopaid ning kus on soe. Külmas
goblinid ei ela.
[size bars] 1,6m | 1,8m
```

Reading notes: "orgid" is the owner's spelling of "orkid" (orcs). "koobastes" is written "koobaster", "esemed" looks like "eremed". "söövad" is written with a doubled ö and an unclear first letter (it can also be read "ööval"); "söövad" fits the sentence. "hapnikku ... ei saa" (they get no oxygen at all) is as written. "warge" (wargs) is spelled with `w`. "nood" is probably the owner's spelling of "noad" (knives). "kaitseid" (protections, armour) is written "kaitreid".

**Translation**:

> GOBLIN
> Goblins are like orcs, but they are smaller and weaker,
> yet smarter and technologically more advanced. They live inside
> mountains or in caves. Goblins very much like
> gold and precious jewellery or objects.
> They have very bad health, because they eat
> food that has gone rotten, and they get no oxygen
> at all. They live 40-60 years and grow
> 1.4-1.8 m tall. The sun makes them drowsy and
> weak, and to move quickly on the surface they use
> wargs. They fight with weapons (axe, sword, bow,
> knives, spear etc.) and wear armour. They live
> where there are mountains and caves and where it is warm. In the cold
> goblins do not live.
> [size bars] 1.6 m | 1.8 m

**Claims**:

- Goblins are like orcs but smaller and weaker, smarter and technologically more advanced.
- Habitat: inside mountains or in caves; only where there are mountains, caves and warmth; never in the cold.
- They love gold and precious jewellery and objects.
- Very poor health from eating rotten food and lacking oxygen (living underground).
- Lifespan 40 to 60 years.
- Height 1.4 to 1.8 m (drawn example 1.6 m).
- Sunlight makes them drowsy and weak.
- They ride wargs to move quickly on the surface.
- Weapons: axe, sword, bow, knives, spear and others; they wear armour.

**Canon check**:

- Goblins as a people related to orcs: `consistent`; orcs and goblins are listed together and fight in Volgan's and Elzon's forces as well as independently (`04 - Peoples/Peoples of Aoaz.md`, `06 - Politics/Kingdoms Factions and Conflicts.md`).
- Smarter and technologically more advanced than orcs: `new (no conflict)`.
- Mountain and cave dwellers: `new (no conflict)`; compatible with the Second Age "Opening of the Passes", where orc and goblin armies "rise from deep underground" (`07 - History/The Second Age of Aoaz.md`).
- Never live in the cold: `conflict: the original compendium snapshot lists goblin peoples among the clans of the Cold Lands around Coldor, Titanos and the Yeti Lands (Sources/ZIN Lore Compendium - original snapshot.md, line 132)`. `needs owner`.
- Love of gold and treasure: `new (no conflict)`.
- Poor health, rotten food, lack of oxygen: `new (no conflict)`.
- Lifespan 40 to 60 years: `consistent` with the chronology (a short mortal life span).
- Height 1.4 to 1.8 m: `new (no conflict)`.
- Weakened by sunlight: `new (no conflict)`.
- Warg riders: `new (no conflict)`; wargs have their own card in part 1 (index 15) and are not yet in the vault.
- Weapons and armour: `new (no conflict)`.

- Every village has one king who leads and crowns Leprechauns.

- [?Gelbin?] Mekkatorque: `needs owner`. "Gelbin Mekkatorque" is a character name from Blizzard's Warcraft games; the project forbids copying text from other works, so this name cannot be used as written.

## Questions for the owner

Each question below comes from a consequential conflict or gap found in photos 33 to 66. None is decided here.

1. **Gorbatik and Golbran (index 34).** The vault treats "Gorbat" as a variant spelling of Golbran, Varner's small purple servant. Is "Gorbatik", "Varner's helper", also Golbran? If so, the card's "immortal" conflicts with Golbran's death in the final palace battle, and his 197,000 years need an anchor in the chronology.
2. **Goblins and the cold (index 36).** The card says goblins never live in the cold; the original compendium lists goblin peoples among the clans of the Cold Lands. Which holds?
3. **Borrowed names.** Several cards use names from other works, which the project may not copy: "Leprechaun" (Irish folklore, index 38), "Gelbin Mekkatorque" (Warcraft, index 38), "Chiromaw" (The Edge Chronicles, index 53), Blue Mountains, Grey Mountains and Iron Hills (Tolkien, index 44), Trollshaws, Ettenmoors and Angmar, and stone trolls turning to stone in sunlight (Tolkien, index 59). Should each get an original name or trait, and does the owner want to supply them?
4. **Coniferons and Ents (index 40).** Are Coniferons the Ents of Kilinti Forest under an original name, or a separate tree people? Where is [?Rush?] Forest (shared with the Vinocorne, part 1)?
5. **Dwarf goblins (index 42).** Are they a kind of dwarf, a kind of goblin, or a people of their own, and is the reading "Lerendor" (a kingdom of men in the North Lands) right for their main home?
6. **Dwarven homelands (index 44).** The vault leaves them unassigned; the card names six mountains and hills, three of them Tolkien names. Which places, under which names, are the dwarven homelands?
7. **Devils (index 46), Q-07.** The card calls devils immortal yet describes reviving dead devils by throwing the skull into lava. Is that the rule (ageless, killable, revivable by rite)? Where are [?Pornyior?], Ergos, the Death Islands and the Goldoroth Mountains?
8. **Demons (index 48).** Is "hell" (põrgu) the Nether or another realm? Are the "South Islands" the Islands of the South? Should the real-world inverted cross and pentagram drawn on the card appear in game art?
9. **Gektor (index 57).** What is Gektor's rank or kind? The card says he "often summons Great Spirits and kills them with an army of men", which clashes with Great Spirits being the high immortal order whose deaths are rare, age-defining events. Where is [?Sempos?], and how does his age of 460,000 years fit the chronology?
10. **Edvoid (indices 55, 62, 63), Q-03 and Q-10.** The vault's naming table records Edvoid as Zol's former placeholder name, while the card makes him a separate spirit, "a former minion made by Zol". Confirm that he is a separate being; give or approve his new name; say whether he is Zol's most loyal servant in the War of the Last Crowns despite "former"; and say how "quietly destroys cities every day" fits the long Fourth Age peace. Are the Beasts of Zol's Mistake his main minions?
11. **Elzon's home (index 61).** Is "Nazerun" the established Nezer, Volgan's seat Nafeher, or a separate place?
12. **Card ages.** Gorbatik 197,000 years, Gektor 460,000, Elzon 7,370,000 and Edvoid 7,945,640 years against about 6,800 reckoned years of the four ages. Should these figures be kept as in-world legend, rescaled, or dropped?
13. **Unplaced names.** Olodor (gnomes and chiromaws), Steadtler, [?Orewell?], High Mountain, Gigantic Mountain and [?Dorap?] Swamp are not in the vault or the region table: where are they?
14. **Untitled drawing (index 51).** Which creature is the red winged figure eating a small creature: a demon, a devil, or something else?

# Stage 2 integration note: starting forces, AI templates and designs, generic AI strategies, designer pieces

Module `tools/content/modules/forces.mjs`, data `tools/content/data/forces_templates.json` (division templates, per-people profiles, culture character, the start table of the named realms, the Great Wall forts), `forces_ai.json` (role ratios, AI template entries, production, navy AI, ship designs, doctrine and research tables) and `forces_geography.json` (per country: its states, the province of each victory point, the port province, the centre of the state on the map; **generated** by `node tools/content/write_forces_geography.mjs` from `history/states`, `map/buildings.txt`, `map/provinces.bmp` and `map/definition.csv`, never by hand), test `tools/content/forces.test.mjs` (57 tests, appended to the `test` script of `tools/package.json`). Specs: `docs/specs/03_gameplay/01_warfare.md` sections 4, 13, 15 and AI; `09_naval_air_doctrines.md` sections 8 to 10; `docs/specs/09_validation/02_ai_defaults.md` sections 3 to 5.

## 1. The call to add to `build.mjs`

```js
import { buildForces } from './modules/forces.mjs';
// after buildWarfare (the name lists it stages are read here) and after buildTechnologies (their start_packages.json is read from the staged set);
// before buildVanillaRemoval (the C3 scan must see these files). A probe build with the call placed right after buildWarfare passed C3 and the text scan.
const forces = await buildForces(staged, { countries, assignment, table });
```

`ctx` is `{ countries, assignment?, table?, startPackages?, splitNaval?, raw? }`: the roster, the Map tag -> people (or `table` of `loadCountryTable`; both are derived from `docs/design/countries.csv` when absent), the parsed `start_packages.json` (otherwise the staged copy of the technologies module, otherwise computed with `technologyPackages`/`startFor`), and `splitNaval` (see 4.2). It returns the counts: 305 orders of battle, 1,035 divisions, 278 ships, 47 division templates, 41 AI role entries with 44 target templates, 38 ship designs in 4 groups, 6 task forces, 5 fleets, 8 goals, 65 strategy blocks, 18 plans. It also stages `docs/design/forces/forces_report.json` (per country: people, tier, divisions, garrisons, templates, ships, stock, notes; the AI coverage matrix of people by role).

## 4. How the forces are made, and the choices

### 4.1 Orders of battle (one file per tag, seeded by the tag)
- **Division count.** Named realms follow the table of warfare 13.1 exactly (25 realms). Every other realm: `clamp(1 + round(states x 0.6), 1, 18)`, tier B x 1.25, times the culture's `martial` factor (the war traditions of the world bible, `cultureCharacter` in the data), capped by the population (`demography.json` people x the people's manpower share, one division per 5,000 men). A **village** (one state, under 60,000 people) keeps one garrison and no field host; a realm of fewer than three states fields only garrisons. 178 realms are garrison-only, 125 field at least one host, 2 (Zol and Yeldenne) have no division.
- **Garrisons** are reserve templates (`priority = 0`, experience half) placed one to a state, in the states ranked by victory point (the capital first); the share is the people's (men 30%, elves 15%, ...) plus 5 (tier B) or 10 (tier C) points, as the AI spec's tier deltas. **Field hosts** are drawn from the people's mix by largest-remainder apportionment (`mix` weights are the AI role ratios of the spec, multiplied by the culture's multipliers: riders for steppe and Noris, rangers for Selen, hill companies for Gunwaith and Mirolon, sea soldiers for sea kings, siege for guild cities...), two to a state over the best states. A template the realm cannot field (a sub-unit not unlocked by its start technologies) falls back to the people's first usable template and the report says so.
- **Wall garrisons.** Aenor's 6 and Kingson's 4 stand in the states nearest the three Great Wall forts (`geography/erdae.json` sites, normalised on the 5120 x 2560 layout), within 420 px.
- **Experience** is the table's (named) or the people's (men 0.1, elves 0.15, orcs 0.2, demons 0.3) with a seeded jitter of 0.03.
- **Equipment.** `start_equipment_factor = 1`; the stock is 1.2 x the need of the divisions (Volgan 1.5) of the best unlocked type of each archetype, plus pack trains (`train_equipment`, 20 + 10 per division) and cogs (`convoy_0`, for realms with ships) when their technologies are known; one production line (1 workshop, 50% efficiency, progress 0.5) of the people's main arms (men and orcs `zin_arms_equipment`, elves and Sunhot `zin_elven_arms_equipment`, trolls, giants, ents, yeti, demons and Volgan their own gear). Zol and Yeldenne stock nothing. No granted equipment is stocked (a test).
- **Names.** Every template pins `division_names_group` to the culture's host group of the warfare module when that group lists the template's main battalion (a test); divisions use `name_order` 1, 2, 3....
- **Fleets.** Named realms as the table; every other realm with ports and the galley technology gets `ceil(port states / 2 x sea factor)` galleys (at most 10) in task forces at its ports (the best hull type it knows: galley `_1` after War Galleys). Ships are named from the culture's name groups of the warfare module; the first warship is the pride of the fleet. Zol has its three corsairs (`zin_hull_raider_0`, Black Ship 1 to 3) in the harbour of its capital; no other country has a raider. No air wing exists anywhere.
- **No variants.** Ships are bare hull types (`zin_hull_galley_1 = { amount = 1 owner = TAG }`, no `version_name`). The spec's named variants of 7.2 would be `create_equipment_variant` in the country histories (the politics step); the AI makes its own from `zin_ai_equipment.txt`.

### 4.2 The fleet sits in the land file (probe)
A land order of battle that contains `fleet` blocks is accepted by the engine as far as the installed files show (`PAK_1936.txt` keeps `air_wings` in its land file; no installed file mixes divisions and fleets). If the Linux run logs a missing fleet, call the module with `splitNaval: true`: the fleets move to `<TAG>_world_naval.txt` and each country history adds `set_naval_oob = "<TAG>_world_naval"` (the politics step). The module's test covers both modes.

### 4.3 AI (spec 02_ai_defaults, drop 2)
- **Templates** (`zin_ai_templates.txt`): 41 role-level entries, 7 roles (`infantry`, `garrison`, `mobile`, `armor`, `siege_role`, `marines`, `rangers_role`). Men use `blocked_for` (every other tag), so a dynamic tag falls to the men's entries; every other people uses `available_for`. A test proves that every roster tag has exactly one entry for each role it uses, that `infantry` and `garrison` apply to all, that every history template matches an entry that applies to its country, and that every target names sub-units the people recruits. Heavy, warded, knight, siege and elven siege targets are enabled for tiers A and B only (a role whose every target is gated has its ratio cancelled for tier C by `zin_default_land_<people>_tier_c`, so no role is wanted without a target); the heavy host waits for `zin_tech_mail`, the warded host for `zin_zol_rising`. Zol's entries have `upgrade_prio` 0. The AI spec's tier changes to `upgrade_prio` and the second chain step per role are not written (one chain, for men's infantry).
- **Ship designs** (`zin_ai_equipment.txt`): 4 groups (men with `blocked_for`, elves, orcs and Dominion, the Mistake) of four hull classes, 38 designs, roles `naval_screen`, `naval_capital`, `naval_raider`; a design stops when the technology of the next hull type is known. Every slot name and module comes from the hull and module files of the warfare module (a test).
- **Navy** (`zin_taskforce.txt`, `zin_fleet.txt`, `zin_goals.txt`): the six task forces and four fleets of spec 10, plus a fifth fleet, `zin_coastal_fleet` (two coast guards), for realms with only galleys; the eight engine objective types without mines. All goals are for every country.
- **Strategies** (65 blocks): role ratios by people (the spec's table), tier shifts, marines for coastal men, the peace-first brake (`zin_default_peace_first`, `ai_wanted_divisions_factor` -50 in the peace era; this stands in for the `zin_war.txt` of the warfare spec, which does not exist), factory mix by people, equipment lines (variant factor = weight - 100; the seven granted archetypes and griffins and scorpions never), surplus, minimum lines, ratios of galleys, warships, raiders and cogs for sea powers, coastal realms and Zol, no dragons, griffins under `beasts`, and 15 grand doctrine and 4 track blocks. Every block has `allowed`, `enable` and `abort_when_not_enabled`; no value is above 300; no diplomacy token is used. People tests read the flag `zin_people_<key>` that the peoples module sets at startup (a dynamic tag has none, so it gets only the engine's defaults); Volgan and Zol are matched by `original_tag`.
- **Plans** (18 of them, one per people and tier in the roster): research multipliers by `zin_cat_*`; no focus order.

## 5. Spec conflicts and choices

1. **Dragons.** The brief allowed "air and dragon units where the world bible gives them"; warfare and the naval spec forbid any dragon in a stockpile or wing at the start, and the bible calls them rare allies. No air unit exists at the start; a test checks it.
2. **Population.** The demography file (a draft) and the state manpower in `history/states` disagree by a factor of seven (86.6 million people against 624 million manpower), so the population only caps the generic count; the spec's state-based formula and the named table decide. Re-tune `popPerDivision` in `forces_templates.json` after the demography is applied to the states.
3. **Ports.** `map/buildings.txt` has `naval_base_spawn` points in 522 states only. Coldor, Gunwaith, Girdon, Noris and Stabilo have none, so their spec fleets (6 and 3, 4, 4, 4, 4) are not placed; the module places them as soon as the geography file is regenerated from a map with a port (a test expects the fleet exactly when a port exists). No naval base level exists in any state history: ships stand in a port without a base (see section 6).
4. **Start technologies.** Goldoroth and Volgan have no galley technology in their packages (spec: 2 and 4 galleys): they get no ships. See section 6.
5. **Tier of AI templates.** The spec asks for per-tier changes of `upgrade_prio` and `reinforce_prio`; only `reinforce_prio` (garrisons 0, rangers and knights 2) and the tier C exclusion of heavy targets are written.
6. **`template_prio`, `build_building`, `building_target`, the equipment market, `pp_spend_*`, areas, theaters, the diplomacy, court and MTTH files of the AI spec** are not written here: the first three need the AI probes, the market and court belong to the institutions and diplomacy modules, areas and theaters to the map and historical-path steps. `zin_default_diplomacy.txt` and `zin_default_court.txt` therefore do not exist.
7. **Role ratio scale** is the spec's weights taken as they stand (probe AI-P1 calibrates).
8. **graphic_db file name** follows the disposition row (`zin_equipment_icons.txt`), not the warfare spec (`zin_graphic_db.txt`).

## 6. Dependencies on other slices (found by the module's checks)

- **Technologies:** (a) `zin_mounts_equipment` is not unlocked for elves, Sunhot, orcs, trolls, giants, ents, yeti and demons although scouts (and the elven riders) need mounts: 21 realms (ents, giants, demons, orcs, yeti, elves, Sunhot) stock `zin_mounts_equipment_0` which no technology unlocks (`elven_steeds` should unlock `zin_mounts_equipment_0`; the kin packages should unlock mounts or scouts should drop the mounts need). (b) `zin_bows_equipment` is not unlocked for the 21 orc realms (the goblin skirmishers' bows) and for Volgan. (c) The ents' package lacks the men's levy, archers and rangers that warfare 4.4 gives Kilinti, so Kilinti fields no Forest Archers. (d) Goldoroth and Volgan lack `oared_galleys` (and `war_galleys`) for their spec fleets. Add them to `assign.extras` of `technologies_packages.json`; the next build uses them without a change here.
- **Map step:** ports for Coldor, Gunwaith, Girdon, Noris and Stabilo (or confirmation that they are landlocked); a naval base level 1 in each port state of a country that starts with ships (the module's `naval_base` field points at the largest port).
- **Politics step:** stop writing `history/units`; `set_technology`/`set_grand_doctrine`/`set_naval_oob` as above.
- **Historical path / peoples:** the variable `global.zin_path_cp05` and the global flags `zin_war_of_last_crowns`, `zin_ending_reached` (era triggers; an unset variable reads as 0, so the peace era lasts until they exist), the flags `zin_people_<key>` (peoples module, exists) and `zin_zol_rising` (technologies read it too).
- **Warfare:** nothing new; it must stage `common/units/names_divisions/zin_names_divisions.txt` and `names_ships/zin_names_ships.txt` before this module runs.

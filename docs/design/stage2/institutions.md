# Stage 2 integration note: institutions and commanders

Module `tools/content/modules/institutions.mjs` (entry) with writers in `tools/content/lib/inst_war.mjs`, `inst_trade.mjs`, `inst_intrigue.mjs`, `inst_craft.mjs` and helpers in `inst_util.mjs`; data `tools/content/data/institutions.json` (camp, customs, chiefs, abilities, Valour, market, licences), `institutions_traits.json` (commander traits), `institutions_intrigue.json` (upgrades, agencies, operations, phases, tokens, raids) and `institutions_craft.json` (workshops, policies, spells, rewards, scholars, scars); test `tools/content/institutions.test.mjs` (31 tests, several hundred assertions; appended to the `test` script of `tools/package.json`). Spec: `docs/specs/03_gameplay/08_institutions.md`.

## 1. The call to add to `build.mjs`

```js
import { buildInstitutions } from './modules/institutions.mjs';
// after buildNames (it needs `table` and `pools`), BEFORE buildVanillaRemoval (the reference scan must see these files),
// BEFORE the text sweep (the sweep skips keys another module already staged) and before the assets check:
const institutions = await buildInstitutions(staged, { countries, table, pools });
```

`ctx`: `countries` (required, the roster), `table` (Map tag to `{ people, stance }`, `loadCountryTable`) and `pools` (Map scan to name pool, `buildNames`); both are derived from `docs/design/countries.csv` and `data/labels.json` when absent. It returns counts: 20 camp sub-units (15 roles), 13 abilities, 27 customs, 214 chiefs, 43 chief traits, 102 commander traits, 3 decisions, 21 agency upgrades, 15 agency records, 20 operations, 60 phases, 3 night raids, 9 workshops (90 traits), 12 policies, 9 spells, 26 prototype rewards, 4 scars, 9 scholar traits, 51 AI strategies, 1,307 ZIN text keys, 320 engine text keys, 342 sprites. Also exported: `loadInstitutions()` (the data), `startValour(data, countries, table)`, `equipmentRights()` (the licence and lend-lease answer per archetype, for the warfare module), `requiredEnumEntries()` (archetypes the MIOs name), `defineRows()`.

## 6. Choices and spec conflicts (no spec was edited)

1. **Camp sub-units: 20 for 15 roles.** A sub-unit needs one equipment set, so "Household Foot" (arms 60) cannot also serve the dead and the demons, and "Great-Kin Guard" cannot serve four peoples with four gears. Household Foot has three variants (arms, grave arms, nether arms) and the Great-Kin Guard four (troll, giant, ent, yeti gear). Every one of the 305 countries can field at least one household battalion (a test checks each people). Acceptance 1 should read "fifteen camp roles".
2. **All camp units are `active = yes`** except the eyrie liaison, so the camp is usable without waiting for the technology slice (vanilla unlocked each with its company technology). The costs are in Orders.
3. **No AI camp pause.** The installed AI has no strategy type for army headquarters; `zin_inst_camp_pause` cannot be written. The engine deploys a camp only for armies of 10 divisions or more (`ARMY_LEADER_MIN_DIVISIONS_FOR_HQ`). `zin_inst_war_camp_deployed` is provided for later use.
4. **Chiefs:** tier A six (army, navy, air, two marshals, master of lore), tier B two (army, master), Zol none (S4): 214, not "about 207". Names from the country's pool, no portraits of their own (the asset uniqueness check forbids reusing the 2+2 generic portraits). The navy chair is visible only to coastal realms and the air chair only while the sky rule is on. The lore family `zin_chief_lore_*` is added for the Master of Lore. Chief traits add `max_command_power` 10/20/30 (Orders), not the vanilla `command_cap_increase`.
5. **Valour at the start** is paid by `on_startup` (`common/on_actions/zin_inst.txt`), not by the politics step: `startValour()` is exported if the politics step prefers it. Sky Valour is 0 everywhere (no dragon exists at the start); navy Valour only for coastal realms.
6. **Scholars:** `zin_sch_marked` is a character dynamic modifier, not a scientist trait: no effect removes a scientist trait, and the spec wants Lifting the Curse to remove it. The other nine are scientist traits. The backlash multipliers read `has_trait` on the scientist (installed special projects do the same; not verified in game).
7. **Reward tokens are per rite** (`steady_*`, `insight`, `scarred_ground`): the experience and bonus effects name the rite, and a generic reward cannot. 26 reward tokens in all; good weights sum to 5.5 and bad to 1.9, as the spec (a test checks).
8. **Spell outputs:** the Lantern Ward gives `army_org_factor` 0.03 instead of the spec's "wardens company max_organisation +10" (the warden sub-unit id is warfare's and a sub-unit modifier needs it); Dragon-Speech gives `air_accidents_factor` -0.20 and the dragon equipment bonus; the Grave-Binding Rite gives `army_org_factor` 0.05 and `land_reinforce_rate` 0.02 for the grave-host templates' casualty term (the template id is warfare's). Wellspring Rite: `country_resource_oil` 4 (Essence, key `oil`).
9. **Market and Sending Arms AI:** the strategy types take a country tag, so faction and charter trade desire is left to the engine; sending arms gets one `send_lend_lease_desire` (60) per subject, enabled for the overlord (29 strategies from the roster's `overlord` field). Market sell/buy thresholds follow the spec.
10. **Workshops' `allowed`** lists the countries of the allowed peoples by tag plus the people flags, because the check runs at game start before `on_startup` sets the flags. A realm that joins a people later (a dynamic tag) gets no workshop until the diplomacy of the spec asks.
11. **Royal Orders** is renamed Royal Warrants (spec open question 2, in use). The guild kind (lodge, stud, order) is a name and a capstone only.
12. **Raids:** three types in category `zin_night_raids` (ransom a captain, burn a depot, fire a granary). The vanilla `rescue_captured_general` id is not kept (no script names it); the raid unit model is `infantry_entity`, which resolves through the people's graphical culture (`<culture>_infantry_entity`, written by `units3d.mjs`). No `unit_requirements` are written (sub-unit ids are warfare's): any land host may be chosen.
13. **Vanilla sprite names are not redefined** (a second definition logs a duplicate). The 112 names the engine keeps asking for (`GFX_trait_<id>` of the engine-keyed traits, the five token pictures and text icons) are listed in `docs/design/institutions_sprites.json` under `vanillaNames`, each with a suggested catalog picture; the art or interface pass replaces the texture files under the same names. Every other picture this module names (342 sprites: abilities, operations, phases, upgrades, agencies, workshops, policies, spells, scars, raids, decision pictures) is defined in `interface/zz_zin_institutions.gfx` and shows a picture of the icon catalog until the art pass draws its own.
14. **Deliberately not done:** the Officer Corps preferred-tactic list (warfare's tactics file), the chair strip of the politics screen, the model of the camp on the map (art), market windows text, operative events (none needed), an agency at the start (none, spec).

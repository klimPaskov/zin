# Map overhaul plan

**Status:** Draft (19 October 2026). **Priority:** 1. **Wave:** 1 and 2 of [00_master_overhaul_plan.md](../00_master_overhaul_plan.md). Criteria M1 to M9 and W3, W5 of [PROGRAM.md](../PROGRAM.md).

## Purpose

Rebuild the playable map of Erdae so that it reads as a designed continent: real lakes, no globe, borders along rivers, ranges and forests, terrain with character, named water, and a history behind every border. The map is generated, never hand-edited, by an improved HOI4 Agent Tools and a ZIN-side pipeline.

## Why the old map failed

The tool audit (`docs/plans/zin-map-pipeline.md` in the HOI4 Agent Tools repository, with measured numbers) found five root causes:

1. Terrain came from pixel colours, each class a constant height and a flat tint: mountains are plateaus (peak 142 against vanilla's 239) and nothing could say "fertile".
2. The colour map is too bright (mean value 129 against 74) and nearly flat (14 distinct colours against 1,084), so the engine's 0.75 overlay washes the land out.
3. Lakes were written at height 93 with a texture index that points at nothing, so they render as faint patches.
4. Provinces, states and countries ignore rivers and ridges (border enrichment over chance 0.94, 0.69 and 0.52, against vanilla's 1.37, 1.42 and 1.53); rivers are 0.33% of land against 2.28%; adjacency files are empty; names are "District N" and "Region N".
5. Nothing handles the world edge: land touches the seam on 1,037 rows.

## Decisions (from the master plan)

P1 the size stays 5,120 x 2,560 with 9,000 to 11,000 provinces; P2 an open-sea band plus frame borders at the edges, with a rim wall as fallback; P3 the Great Lake is an inland sea with a fresh-water look, every other lake a true lake province; P4 the Great Wall is an impassable range with four to six named gates and level-5 forts. The geography model's answers to its 18 questions are adopted as the working proposals ([OPEN_QUESTIONS.md](../OPEN_QUESTIONS.md), questions 29 to 46).

The map track starts with steps 1 and 2 in wave 1 (they need only tool phase 0 and C5) and finishes steps 3 to 7 in wave 2, when relief, hydrology, partition and territory services are merged.

## What changes in ZIN when the map changes

- State ids and province ids change: everything that names them must resolve through names. The politics step and the sites module are the only writers of such references; the content builder, trees and events use `zin_is_site_*` triggers and country names.
- The 1,332 states shrink or grow with the new density; `countries.csv` and the roster are unchanged (305 countries; tag total stays below 439).
- Every manifest (`.hoi4-agent-world.json`) is regenerated; `.zin-content.json` content survives, except files generated from state data.
- Specs that name states or regions are checked by the key-sites regeneration; any that do not resolve fail the build.

## Acceptance

1. **M1:** every map layer is written by the tool; `hoi4_map_inspect` and every validator pass; the layer manifest is current.
2. **M2:** the 12 lakes exist as lake provinces with the right height, texture and names; the Great Lake is an inland sea at least 150 x 150 pixels with ports; screenshots show water.
3. **M3:** zero land in the edge bands; no seam adjacency; screenshots at both edges show open sea, not a cut coast.
4. **M4:** the border-quality metric (default radii) reports enrichment of at least 1.3 for provinces, states and countries and a natural share of at least 0.70 for states and 0.73 for countries (vanilla parity: 1.35 / 1.40 / 1.46 and 0.69 / 0.70 / 0.73; the old ZIN map 0.94 / 0.70 / 0.50 and 0.08 / 0.07 / 0.09).
5. **M5:** the Great Wall is a continuous elevation-5 barrier with its gates; Kingson sits inside a closed ring of elevation 4 or 5; the East Lands carry the Hellfire Range with its volcanoes; Aenor's provinces are fertile plains (terrain share check per region against the model's targets within 10 points).
6. **M6:** 25 named rivers from source to mouth feed lakes and seas; every strait of the model is a named crossing in `adjacencies.csv`; every sea and strait is named.
7. **M7:** no state, region, lake, river or victory-point name matches `District`, `Region \d`, `Settlement` or a generated placeholder.
8. **M8, W3, W5:** the demography check passes (population against fertility, rivers and coasts); each country's holdings match the model's natural-border table and the world bible's founding story.
9. **M9:** the tag total stays below 439.
10. **Linux:** five great powers start and run with the clock unpaused; the error budget holds.

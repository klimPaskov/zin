# Realm roots: the Ember Lands

**Status:** Proposed under decision K67 (owner, 25 October 2026). Every root and sign is labelled `proposed` in [ember_lands.json](../../../../tools/content/data/world/history/ember_lands.json), which holds the full texts. The play hooks are proposals for the focus, decision and event owners.
**Companions:** [docs/lore/regions/ember_lands.md](../../../lore/regions/ember_lands.md), [nether.md](../../../lore/regions/nether.md), the Southern Dominion mechanic, and the vault note [Roots of the Ember Lands](../../../lore/vault/07%20-%20History/Roots%20of%20the%20Ember%20Lands.md).

## Ground rules for builders

- No new tags; identities are cosmetic (`set_cosmetic_tag`).
- **Kept open:** which New God Volgan served and overcame, and when; how he came to hold the plateau; whether his essence survives the breaking of his body; the King of Demons' own name (never shown); which Old God the devils served; Roka and the Abyss.
- **Canon guards.** Puvulan alone shows the Gate of Nafeher, and its ring of names is drawn plain (no letters). The Nether alone shows the King's seal, the one five-pointed figure of Aoaz inside a demon sigil, and never a trident. Nezer shows its galley and nothing of Elzon. No living realm carries a device of Elzon's order.
- Demons are corrupted angels, immortal Great Folk; devils are corrupted Lesser Spirits bound to their bodies, returning only by the lava rite; the King of Demons' pacts bind the living and the dead remain Zol's. No hook lets a pact claim a soul.
- Mirolon's beacon lamps are open flames in the lore, but no banner shows a lamp with an open flame (a god's device); the heron carries the watch instead.
- Audiences follow K61: the Dominion's rebuilding stays unheard beyond the region until the Veil breaks.

## The realms

### Puvulan (tree `volgan`)

**Roots.** A power's own realm: Volgan, once a New God's servant who overcame that god, holds the plateau behind the Gate of Nafeher, which the orders teach was the first thing raised there; whoever serves is cut into the ring of names round the gateway, a traitor is cut out of it, and the dead are raised under the names of those who served before.
**Sign.** The Gate of Nafeher within the plain ring of names, Volgan's own sign.

**Focus branches** (the 114-focus tree holds the forges, the grave-fields, the Veil and the court):
1. *The Ring of Names.* A loyalty system: lieutenants and sworn realms are cut into the ring (stability and compliance), and a traitor cut out of it starts a hunt; the ring's fullness is a counter the Dominion's strength reads.
2. *Names Under the Dead.* Grave musters raised under the names of former servants: elite grave units named for the Kalioth host and other First Age servants (cosmetic names; no new mechanic beyond the existing musters).
3. *The Black Tribute Remembered.* Diplomacy toward the eastern heirs of the Kalioth: if Erco wears the diadem, Puvulan may offer the old alliance, which every Kalioth-memory realm learns by minor report.
4. *The Gate Opened.* The march north: the gate's banner leads the host; its fall in the field is a morale blow to the Dominion.

**Identity.** None; the Dominion's name is the orders' and needs no other.

**Events and decisions.**
- Event "A Name Cut Out": a lieutenant betrays the Dominion and is cut from the ring.
- Decision "Cut a New Name": swear a new sworn realm or lieutenant into the ring (compliance, political power).
- Event "The Old Tribute Offered": when the diadem is worn in Erco, Nafeher sends envoys east.

### Mirolon (tree `mirolon`)

**Roots.** The Ainic lake-kingdom lost its kings to Volgan in 1A 1150; the survivors hid in the Great Swamp for five centuries, read the patrols by the herons and became the scouts; Hadric refused the crown and hung it in the beacon tower in 2A 3, and the wardens' laws come from the swamp years.
**Sign.** A grey heron standing watchful with its neck drawn back.

**Focus branches** (the 83-focus tree holds the watch, the proof and the exile court):
1. *The Swamp Years.* A memory branch: the Great Swamp as a refuge (defence, hidden supply caches) that the exile route can use before leaving the peninsula.
2. *Stand Like the Heron.* Scout doctrine: recon and intelligence bonuses, and the scouts' still-watch as a detection modifier on the Sound.
3. *The Crown in the Tower.* The lake-kings' crown: a branch that keeps it unworn (the elective crown's legitimacy) and an exile-court option to carry it out of Miherta so that it is never Volgan's again.
4. *The Lamp Never Out.* The beacon law: a warden whose lamp goes out is struck from the rolls; a beacon chain that warns the north, if anyone listens.

**Identity.** *The Free Watch of Mirolon.* Entitled by the swamp years and Hadric's refusal. Mirolon. Requires the Dominion's body broken (the established outcome) with Mirolon unconquered or reconquered, and the crown still unworn. Changes: name and the heron banner; the watch becomes the south's guardian with claims (never cores) on the Nan and Halia coasts only if their people invite it by event.

**Events and decisions.**
- Event "The Herons Rise": the scouts see the herons rise on the Sound, a warning of a crossing.
- Decision "Carry the Crown Out": in the exile route, take the lake-kings' crown north.
- Event "A Lamp Gone Out": a warden lets his lamp die; the rolls must strike him.

### Khorvrath (tree `regional_demon`)

**Roots.** A house of the Halls' beast-breeders, refused a portal of its own, rode up out of the Nether Maw in 2A 977 and would not go back down; its first lord broke a horn of his beast on the rim, and the horn-hold of Khorvazel is crowned with the horns of its fallen.
**Sign.** A single great curved horn broken at its tip.

**Focus branches:**
1. *The Horned Beasts.* Breeding the house's beasts again: shock units (cosmetic for existing heavy units) and a fear modifier on enemy organisation.
2. *No Way Back Down.* Defiance of the Nether's houses: a branch that refuses the king's demands and disputes the Maw.
3. *The Thralls at Olen.* The existing divergence `khorvrath_takes_olen`: raid Olen to take back the three hundred, or let the claim lapse.
4. *An Own Road.* The existing divergence `khorvrath_own_road`: break the oath to Nafeher when the Dominion falters.

**Identity.** None.

**Events and decisions.**
- Event "A Horn on the Hall": a great servant falls, and a horn is set on Khorvazel.
- Decision "Demand the Thralls": the yearly demand to Olen (political power; opinion).
- Event "The Halls Call Us Back": the Nether demands the house's return.

### Dolon (tree `regional_dolon`)

**Roots.** Queen Khirara's house of pact-dealers came up onto the salt coast in 2A 1120, drowned the headman in his brine-pan and crowned herself with its salt crystals; oaths of Dolon are written in salt so the sea may wash them away, and the forges of Unzeky make the Dominion's powder.
**Sign.** A crown of jagged white salt crystals.

**Focus branches:**
1. *Laws in Salt.* Pact-law: treaties that the queen may wash away (a decision to annul one oath per reign at an opinion cost).
2. *Freedom by Pact.* Thralls may buy freedom by pact: a manpower and stability system for the thrall population.
3. *Dolon's Forge.* Powder for the orc companies and the secret sale (existing divergence `dolon_sells_powder`).
4. *The Forge Destroyed.* The existing divergence `dolon_forge_destroyed`: rebuild or flee the coast.

**Identity.** None.

**Events and decisions.**
- Event "The Sea Takes an Oath": the queen washes away an oath.
- Decision "Wash an Oath Away": annul one treaty (once per reign).
- Event "A Thrall's Pact": a thrall offers a pact for his freedom.

### Nezer (tree `regional_nezer`)

**Roots.** A family of the Nether's ferrymen, steersmen of the galleys that cross the Sea of Lava, which hold their course only when both oars pull together; a queen and a lord of the house took the Necromancer's empty hills in 4A 3 and rule them as two crowns that must agree on war.
**Sign.** A black galley with two steering oars at its stern.

**Focus branches:**
1. *Both Oars Pull.* Two-crown government: decisions need both crowns (a joint-approval mechanic), with strong effects when they agree and paralysis when they do not (existing divergence `nezer_crowns_split`).
2. *Galleys of the Lava Sea.* The trade with the Nether: income and the power to stop carrying it.
3. *The Broken Ring.* Guarding the Burning Gate (existing divergence `nezer_gate_reopens`).
4. *Nothing of Him.* The Necromancer's hills: a branch that clears his relics from Gelroze, gains stability and refuses any necromantic order a home.

**Identity.** None.

**Events and decisions.**
- Event "One Oar Shipped": the crowns disagree, and the galley is flown with one oar shipped.
- Decision "Stop the Galleys": halt the Nether trade (pressure on the Nether, income lost).
- Event "Something in the Gate-Ring": the broken ring stirs.

### Nether (tree `regional_nether`)

**Roots.** The houses tell that the first demons came down corrupted into the heat beneath the island and cut the Halls under Mount Nether; their king's seal is burned into every pact, which binds the living and gives real power in life while the dead remain Zol's.
**Sign.** The King's seal, the one five-pointed figure of Aoaz, drawn in one unbroken line within a ring of fire.

**Focus branches** (the 46-focus tree holds the Halls, the pacts, the rings and the bargain):
1. *The Seal Burned In.* Pact-bearers: mortals under the seal become agents (intelligence) whom no other house may touch.
2. *The Houses Below.* Competition of the houses for the king's favour, with the house of Agon as the restless one; a favour mechanic.
3. *Beyond the Lava Sea.* Mortal pacts in the war years (existing divergence `nether_mortal_pact`): a northern ruler seeks the seal.
4. *A Bargain, Never an Oath.* The relation with Nafeher: renew, raise the price, or refuse (existing divergence `nether_refuses_volgan`).

**Identity.** None.

**Events and decisions.**
- Event "A Seal on a Mortal Hand": a pact-bearer is found in a northern court.
- Decision "Grant a Pact": sell power to a mortal ruler (in life only).
- Event "Agon's House Moves": the house acts without the king's leave.

### Halia (tree `shared_ember_lands`)

**Roots.** The Ainic salt-boilers buried their dead in salt, and when Volgan's necromancers raised the dead of the lake-kingdom, the freeholders believed the salted dead were spared; in 1A 1180 they agreed to pay salt for silence, and in 4A 1655 the five freeholds swore to pay one toll as one league.
**Sign.** An oar standing upright in a white cairn of salt, the freeholds' grave.

**Focus branches** (Halia's own module of the shared tree):
1. *Salt for the Dead.* The burial custom as a defence against the grave hosts: in a war with Puvulan, Halia's states give the Dominion no grave musters (a modifier on the Dominion's grave-manpower in Halia's land).
2. *One Toll, One League.* The league's unity: a tribute decision with one payment, and a stability test when one freehold wants to pay alone.
3. *Silence Broken.* The day the freeholds speak of Volgan at a grave: a branch that ends the silence and shelters Mirolon's scouts (existing divergence `halia_shelters_mirolon`).
4. *A Client Crown.* The existing divergence `halia_client_crown`.

**Identity.** None.

**Events and decisions.**
- Event "A Grave Unsalted": a freeholder is buried without salt, and the village fears what will come.
- Decision "Pay the Salt": the yearly toll to Nafeher (salt and fish).
- Event "The Thief in the Tavern": the old tale of the thief who did not grow older is told again when a stranger asks the way south.

### Nan (tree `regional_nan`)

**Roots.** The men of the island's old coast who would not serve the Dominion fled across the storm belt to the Nan Mountains; their wayfinders learned the storm-pilots' route by following the storm-petrels, Feron the Grey took Larorun in 4A 1188, and a Nan pilot is sold to whoever pays most.
**Sign.** A dark storm-petrel skimming low with its wings bent.

**Focus branches:**
1. *Follow the Petrels.* Storm navigation: only Nan's pilots cross the Tempest Reach without the Veil's attrition; a hire decision for any navy.
2. *The Men Who Ran.* Memory of the flight from the gate: a branch that turns Nan against Nafeher (refuses to lend pilots, offers them to Mirolon).
3. *Sell the Route.* The existing divergence `nan_sells_route` to Mirolon or Aenor.
4. *Sworn to the Gate.* The existing divergence `nan_sworn_dominion`.

**Identity.** None.

**Events and decisions.**
- Event "Petrels Before a Gale": a pilot reads the gap in a storm.
- Decision "Hire Out the Pilots": lend pilots to a navy (gold; that navy ignores the Veil's attrition for a time).
- Event "A Warlord Fails": the chieftains withdraw their acclamation after a defeat.

### Olen (tree `regional_olen`)

**Roots.** A breeze out of the storm belt kept Olen's cliff lamps lit through the winter of 2A 412 while sea-kestrels hung still in it above the cliffs; the shrine of Gelbus stands where the kestrels hung, and the lamp-keepers shelter whoever reaches the cliffs, including the thralls Khorvrath still demands.
**Sign.** A sea-kestrel hanging still with its wings spread and tail fanned.

**Focus branches:**
1. *The Holy Breeze.* Shrine and pilgrimage: stability and a refugee-shelter spirit.
2. *The Thralls Kept.* The shelter of 4A 1712 defended: defence on the cliffs, or give them up (existing divergence `olen_gives_up_thralls`).
3. *Lamps in Mirolon's Code.* The existing divergence `olen_beacon_to_mirolon`: the lamp-cliffs join the beacon line.
4. *The Breeze Healers.* Healers of the shrine (manpower recovery), shared with refugees.

**Identity.** None.

**Events and decisions.**
- Event "Kestrels on the Cliff": the breeze returns, an omen of shelter.
- Decision "Give the Cloth Kestrel": shelter a refugee band (manpower, Khorvrath opinion down).
- Event "Khorvrath's Demand": the yearly demand for the thralls.

## A shared identity of the peninsula

**The Coast That Kept Its Dead** (proposed). Entitled by the freeholds' salted graves and the shelter laws of Olen and Halia. Formed by Halia with Olen as a partner, after the Veil breaks. Requires Silence Broken and the Thralls Kept, the Dominion's body broken, and Mirolon's consent by event. Changes: Halia takes a league name with the oar-in-salt banner, Olen keeps its own identity as a partner in a defensive league, and the league gains a spirit against the grave hosts. Gives no claims.

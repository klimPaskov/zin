# Realm roots: the Islands of the South

**Status:** Proposed under decision K67 (owner, 25 October 2026). Every root and sign is labelled `proposed` in [islands_of_the_south.json](../../../../tools/content/data/world/history/islands_of_the_south.json), which holds the full texts. The play hooks are proposals for the focus, decision and event owners.
**Companions:** [docs/lore/regions/islands_of_the_south.md](../../../lore/regions/islands_of_the_south.md) and the vault note [Roots of the Islands of the South](../../../lore/vault/07%20-%20History/Roots%20of%20the%20Islands%20of%20the%20South.md).

## Ground rules for builders

- No new tags; identities are cosmetic (`set_cosmetic_tag`). The islands are small, poor in metal and ruled by elders and moots: no branch makes a conquering crown of them.
- **Kept open:** who carved Aen's name in the shrine beneath the tide, and when; what calls from the Night Reef; where the first Yonic canoes came from. The roots say the islanders read the name as Aen and do not know why; nothing says who carved it.
- The islanders look like the peoples of the real-world Indonesian islands (K54), and their material culture in these signs (stilt houses with upswept roofs, slit-drums, cane fish-traps, hand stencils in caves, water dividers on terraces) follows that look without copying any real people's sacred emblems.
- The stranger's coming (Eindil, 4A 1751) is established; the hooks below only shape how each island can meet him, and nothing names him before his own gate.
- The guest-right binds every island: a branch may test it, never abolish it without consequence.

## The realms

### Enhis Is Rond (tree `regional_enhis_is_rond`)

**Roots.** The first Yonic canoes counted their dead as waves on the reef rock; the lagoon villages took the name carved over the southern shrine's door into their tally as Aen, and when the Rising Tide of 2A 40 drowned the shrine, Nuoyo nau Enhis dived to it with a holed stone and came up alive, founding the Priest-Elders and the Calling of Names beneath the water.
**Sign.** The round holed diving stone on its looped cord.

**Focus branches:**
1. *The Dive With the Stone.* Priest-Elder succession by the dive: legitimacy, and a rare failure event when a candidate does not come up.
2. *The Reef Road.* Pilgrimage: the Reef Road's peace across the Great Isle, stability for all three realms of the island, and a decision to call a pilgrimage year.
3. *The Calling of Names.* The Wave-Tally as a state rite: names of the war dead become a stability and war-support modifier; refusing a name is a grave offence.
4. *The Name Read.* The existing divergence `the_name_explained`: when a stranger reads the inscription, the island learns what the name means, and the news reaches the north by minor report only.
5. *A Protector in a Storm Year.* The existing divergence `a_protector_for_the_isle`.

**Identity.** *The Isle of the Shrine Beneath the Tide.* Entitled by the drowned shrine. Enhis Is Rond. Requires the Reef Road kept for ten years, the Calling of Names held through a war, and Howan and Verde in a pact of the Great Isle. Changes: name and the diving-stone banner; the three realms of the Great Isle gain a joint defence and the Priest-Elders a voice in the other two's wars (they may proclaim a Reef Road peace).

**Events and decisions.**
- Event "The Stone Does Not Come Up": a candidate drowns on the dive.
- Decision "Call the Pilgrimage Year": the Reef Road peace across the Great Isle (no war among its realms for a year; stability).
- Event "A Name for the Tally": a foreign captain's dead are brought to the shrine; accepting them binds that crown's sailors to the island.

### Howan (tree `regional_howan`)

**Roots.** The terrace families built houses with roofs swept up like canoe prows so the ancestors would know them from the sea; Howan kept the guest-right first, broke it in the Fever Years, and swore it again through Maiyan's house for the shipwrecked, where every stranger is kept apart for a month before he is welcomed.
**Sign.** The stilt house with its saddle roof swept up at both ends, the house for the shipwrecked.

**Focus branches:**
1. *The Month Apart.* Quarantine law: disease resistance, and a refugee intake that does not bring plague events.
2. *Pearls for Iron.* Trade with the north for iron (the islands want it more than anything): resource imports and divers' guilds.
3. *The House for the Shipwrecked.* The existing divergences `the_house_shelters_him` and `the_house_turns_him_away`, weighed by the Fever Years' memory.
4. *The Terraces of the Tich.* Rice terraces and the moot's water law (farm output, stability).

**Identity.** None; Howan's honour is the house, not a crown.

**Events and decisions.**
- Event "A Wreck at Lamowan": strangers out of the surf; the house takes them in.
- Event "The Black Rain Again": a fever on a wreck tests the month apart.
- Decision "Open the House": formally take in a ship's survivors (manpower, opinion with their homeland, a small disease risk).

### Verde (tree `regional_verde`)

**Roots.** The timber clans hold that every hull carries the spirit of its tree, which is asked before it is felled, and that the men who knew the trees learned the stars; the mangroves on their stilt-roots taught them to stand in two worlds, and no Verdean cuts a mangrove without the chief's leave.
**Sign.** A mangrove on its arched stilt-roots.

**Focus branches:**
1. *Ask the Tree.* Shipbuilding with the clans' rites: ship quality and a timber export modifier, with a fire risk if the mangrove law is broken.
2. *The Edge of the Charts.* Navigators: the chief must have sailed to the edge of the charts; exploration and naval movement bonuses, and pilots hired abroad (existing divergence `navigators_for_a_voyage`).
3. *Timber for Aenor Isle.* The Orohaf trade, kept or withheld in war (existing divergence `the_timber_withheld`).
4. *The Mangrove Law.* Coastal defence from the mangroves (defence in coastal states) and the replanting rite.

**Identity.** None.

**Events and decisions.**
- Event "The Tree Refuses": a hull cracks at launch; the clans say the tree was not asked.
- Decision "Sell a Navigator": hire a Verdean pilot to a foreign ship (gold, opinion; the pilot may not return).
- Event "Fire in the Mangroves": a dry year.

### Nara (tree `regional_nara`)

**Roots.** The reef took the first canoes that fished it by night; after the storm year of 1A 800 the survivors swore the Day Rule, the elders call the boats in at dusk with a conch and mourn any that has not answered, and since 2A 1420 nobody crosses the reef after dark because of the drowned voices.
**Sign.** A fishing torch turned head downward and unlit: the survivors of 1A 800 drove their night torches into the sand of Riwang and swore the Day Rule. (A conch was considered and set aside, because a white conch is a real-world sacred emblem and a princely flag's charge.)

**Focus branches:**
1. *Home Before Dark.* The Day Rule as law: fishing output by day, no night operations near the reef.
2. *The Elders Count the Boats.* Moot government; the eldest speaks for the island.
3. *Children on the Reef.* The existing divergence `children_on_the_reef`: a storm strands the island's children at dusk, and the Day Rule must be kept or broken.
4. *The Aenoran Words.* The descendants of the refugee ship of 3A 1040: a link to Aenor and a stranger's welcome (existing divergence `nara_hosts_the_stranger`).

**Identity.** None.

**Events and decisions.**
- Event "A Boat Does Not Answer the Conch": a boat is lost after dusk.
- Event "Voices on the Night Reef": the voices call; the island's answer is always no unless a divergence opens it.
- Decision "Give the First Catch": the yearly gift to the families of the drowned (stability).

### Yongyong (tree `shared_islands_of_the_south`)

**Roots.** Every Yong village descends from Yong the weaver of the first cane fish-trap; villages of one name quarrelled until the Year of the Red Wind burned three of them, and the ring-council sworn in 4A 902 speaks only when every elder agrees and settles a disputed reach by whose trap catches more.
**Sign.** Yong's woven cane fish-trap, mouth upward.

**Focus branches** (a Yongyong module):
1. *Settle It by Weaving.* Dispute resolution without war: internal stability events resolved by contests.
2. *The Ring Speaks as One.* Unanimity: the council acts only when every elder agrees (slow decisions, strong legitimacy).
3. *Rope, Nets and Shell.* Crafts and trade: the islands' rope and nets as a trade good.
4. *The Ring Breaks.* The existing divergences `the_ring_breaks` and `the_ring_takes_a_protector`.

**Identity.** None.

**Events and decisions.**
- Event "Two Villages, One Reach": a quarrel settled by the season's catch.
- Event "A Red Wind": the feud season; the ring must hold.
- Decision "Weave the Ring's Trap": a renewal rite at Yongrost (stability).

### Kamu Lamo (tree `shared_islands_of_the_south`)

**Roots.** The first canoe-folk blew red ochre over their hands on the deepest cave wall before the first wave of the tally was carved; tally-keepers are made by adding a hand beside them, the caves are entered only at evening, and since 4A 640 the island has been the school of the Wave-Tally.
**Sign.** A left hand with its fingers spread, left dark inside a ragged cloud of blown red ochre on cave black (a stencil, never a red hand on a white field, which is a real-world arms).

**Focus branches** (a Kamu Lamo module):
1. *The School of the Wave-Tally.* Education: research and an advisor slot, and every island's tally-keepers trained here (opinion with every island).
2. *The Hands of the First Comers.* The oldest hand-marks: a cultural relic modifier; the caves never give up where the first comers came from.
3. *Guano for the Terraces.* The bird isle's guano feeds the southern terraces (a trade good and farm bonus for partners).
4. *The Caves Read or Closed.* The existing divergences `the_caves_read` and `the_caves_closed`.

**Identity.** None.

**Events and decisions.**
- Event "A Stranger Asks to Read the Oldest Tally": the elders decide.
- Decision "Lay a Hand": make a tally-keeper of a foreign island (opinion).
- Event "The Cold Cave": the plague names' cave grows colder, an omen of sickness.

### Kea (tree `shared_islands_of_the_south`)

**Roots.** The first terrace families brought a wooden divider that splits one stream into two equal streams; a field that takes more than its share steals from the dead who cut the terraces, the Water Oath of 1A 960 set a Water-Keeper over the Tuwang cisterns, and no island of the south lets another go hungry.
**Sign.** A forked water-channel splitting one stream into two equal streams.

**Focus branches** (a Kea module):
1. *The Divider's Law.* Water management: farm output and stability; a cheat event if a field takes more than its share.
2. *Rice for Every Island.* Famine relief: send rice to any island in a bad year (opinion and trade).
3. *The Tally-Caves Judge.* Disputes sent to Kamu Lamo's elders; a binding judgement mechanic between islands.
4. *Rice for a Protector.* The existing divergences `rice_for_a_protector` and `the_dry_year`.

**Identity.** None.

**Events and decisions.**
- Event "A Field Takes More": a terrace family cheats the divider.
- Decision "Send the Rice Ships": relieve a hungry island (food out, opinion in).
- Event "The Cisterns Run Low": a dry storm year.

### Sidea (tree `shared_islands_of_the_south`)

**Roots.** The islet families hollowed slit-drums to call each other across the channels; the drummers of Yumbo beat the first elder's summons in 2A 300 and the first warning of the drum road in 3A 700, and the elder holds office only as long as the drums will beat for him.
**Sign.** An upright slit-drum hollowed from a log.

**Focus branches** (a Sidea module):
1. *The Drum Road.* Early warning: storm and fleet warnings beaten to the Great Isle (intelligence and naval detection).
2. *The Drummers Choose.* Government by the drummers' consent; an elder who loses it is replaced.
3. *First Landfall.* The existing divergence `the_first_landfall`: a ship blown south from the West makes its first landfall here.
4. *The Drums Silent.* The existing divergence `the_drums_silent`: the drummers refuse a foreign fleet's summons.

**Identity.** None.

**Events and decisions.**
- Event "A Storm Seen First": the drums warn the Great Isle.
- Decision "Beat the Summons": the elder calls the islets together (manpower in a crisis).
- Event "The Drums Will Not Beat": the drummers withdraw consent from the elder.

## A shared island identity

**The Moot of the Two Arcs** (proposed). Entitled by the guest-right, sworn again at the drowned shrine after the Fever Years and kept by every island since. Formed by Enhis Is Rond, Howan or Kea (the shrine, the house and the rice ships, the three pillars of the guest-right). Requires five island realms in a pact of the moot formed by decisions, the Reef Road peace held, and no island having turned a stranger back into the sea for ten years. Changes: a faction of the island realms under one moot banner, a single carved wave of the Wave-Tally in white on lagoon blue (one wave only, never the three wavy bars of a god's device), a joint navy and a guest-right spirit for every member. It is not a crown and gives no claims.

A capital named after a geographic region is a provisional settlement. The source review confirms only eight in-bounds city/locality labels; the nineteen explicit anchors combine those with provisional capitals. Remaining victory-point settlements use generated names.

East Earth is the corrected name of the drawing headed WEST EARTH. No continent was deliberately relocated. The cleaned raster removes ink and paper joins; small outlines are reconstructed and should be compared with the original when exact geography matters. The pale Sea of Gods is treated as water.

The source table contains 711 anchors. Only 464 confirmed in-bounds labels enter generation. Uncertain readings and 32 labels outside the mosaic remain in the review data, without clamping them onto the map. The assignment file records matching native state/province names; an unmatched geographic label remains localisation/provenance and is not invented as a city.

Population, resources, industry, weather, ownership growth and drainage are deterministic assumptions using seed 73. Generated rivers may differ from pencil channels. This is a playable map sandbox with starting units, production, recruitment and supply, not a finished narrative or balanced campaign. No new events, decisions or national focus trees were authored. Old-world events/decisions and startup/AI hooks are suppressed by descriptor replacement folders. Shared base-game systems remain dependencies and can report unused-definition warnings.

## Source identity and geography

- The source folder `East-Earth` contains the drawing headed **WEST EARTH**, with Lerenwaith, Londolo and Ekedor. The author confirms its correct name is **East Earth**. Only its name changes.
- The source folder `West-Earth` contains the drawing headed **NORTH EARTH**, with Gunwaith, Girdon and surrounding lands.
- The Triton's Islands source is headed TORNADO ISLANDS; source-folder names and drawing headings are retained in the evidence table.
- Dark Lands, Dondor, the Dark Forests, Sea of Darkness, its islands, Sea of Death and surrounding seas stay exactly where drawn. No landmass relocation feature is used or included.

## Interpretation and uncertainty

Coordinates are normalized to the full original 17520 by 12720 image. Regional scans were registered to the mosaic using matched pencil features and visually checked footprints; median registration error is below 0.25 pixels in the 3200-pixel overview. Label anchor uncertainty is much larger (approximately 2% of its regional scan). Thirty-two anchors fall outside the full mosaic because their original sheets were cropped; they are documented and never clamped into a false position.

Uncertain spellings remain flagged. Ellipses and uncertain readings must not silently become invented in-game names. Sea, lake and mountain names are geographical labels; native HOI4 has no individual lake/range text layer, so such names may be retained in localisation and provenance rather than displayed as a separate map label. Final per-label generated entity links are pending map acceptance.

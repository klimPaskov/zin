# ZIN dynamic effects

This file documents the reusable scripted effects of ZIN that take arguments, as `CLAUDE.md` asks. Arguments follow decision E1: a temporary variable `zin_arg_<name>` set just before the call, which the effect reads and clears, or an event target the caller saves. Every effect here is generated by the content builder; the source file is named under each heading, and changes go there, never into the staged script.

The older slices document their own helpers in their specs (`docs/specs/04_mechanics/*.md`); this file starts with the realm life and start variety effects of 25 October 2026 and is the place for every new dynamic effect from now on.

## Realm life (`tools/content/modules/realm_life.mjs`, `common/scripted_effects/zin_realm_life.txt`)

Spec: [realm_life.md](specs/04_mechanics/realm_life.md). Every realm life effect uses THIS and PREV and never ROOT, because the monthly pulse runs in the world runner's chain where ROOT is the runner.

### `zin_realm_settle_crisis`

- **Purpose:** ends a succession crisis (G3) in one of five ways and tells the realm (`zin_realm.15`).
- **Scope:** the realm in crisis (country).
- **Input:** `zin_arg_way`: 1 the council elects (calm -5%), 2 the claimants fight for the crown (a civil war of the same allegiance, counted in `global.zin_realm_contested_crowns`), 3 a prince of the house in `zin_realm_match` takes it (a sworn realm of that house on the free course, its guarantee otherwise), 4 the ablest captain-general takes it, 5 the nearest kinsman is proclaimed.
- **Output:** `zin_realm_crisis_way` (the way, read by the event text); removes `zin_realm_crisis` and the flag `zin_realm_in_crisis`.
- **Default:** none; a way outside 1 to 5 settles the crisis with no other change.
- **Example:** `set_temp_variable = { zin_arg_way = 1 }` then `zin_realm_settle_crisis = yes`.

### `zin_realm_offer_terms`

- **Purpose:** offers terms of peace (G6) and fires `zin_realm.40` on the enemy.
- **Scope:** the realm that offers (country).
- **Input:** event target `zin_realm_terms_to`, the enemy.
- **Output:** event targets `zin_realm_terms_from` (THIS) and, for a land, `zin_realm_terms_land`; the enemy's variable `zin_realm_terms_kind`: 1 peace on the old borders, 2 the land THIS holds of the enemy's and claims (when the enemy's surrender is above 0.25 and THIS's below 0.1), 3 silver for peace (when THIS's surrender is above 0.25 and it can pay).
- **Side effects:** none until the enemy answers (`zin_realm_accept_terms`, `zin_realm_refuse_terms`).
- **Example:** `FROM = { save_event_target_as = zin_realm_terms_to }` then `zin_realm_offer_terms = yes`.

### `zin_realm_answer_raid`

- **Purpose:** the raided realm's answer to a raid (G8); tells the raiders (`zin_realm.71`).
- **Scope:** the raided realm (country).
- **Inputs:** `zin_arg_way` (1 ride out: the cause `zin_realm_cb_raiders` against the raider for a year; 2 pay `constant:zin_realm.raid_tribute` authority to the raider; 3 bar the gates: `zin_realm_raided` for half a year) and event target `zin_realm_raider`.
- **Output:** the raider's variable `zin_realm_raid_answer`; event target `zin_realm_raid_victim`.
- **Example:** `set_temp_variable = { zin_arg_way = 1 }` then `zin_realm_answer_raid = yes`.

### `zin_realm_found_exile_court`

- **Purpose:** a fallen realm's court goes into exile (G9) and `zin_realm.50` asks the shelter.
- **Scope:** the fallen realm (country; it may already be annexed).
- **Input:** event target `zin_realm_conqueror`, the realm it fell to (never chosen as the shelter).
- **Output:** the flag `zin_realm_court_in_exile`; on the shelter, `zin_realm_exile_court` (the fallen realm); on the fallen realm, `zin_realm_sheltered_by`; event target `zin_realm_fallen`. With no shelter, the court disperses (`zin_realm_court_dispersed`).
- **Default:** does nothing when `zin_realm_court_may_flee` is false (the historical path, the Aenor court, the Deliverer's seat, the dark powers, a court already in exile or dispersed).
- **Example:** in `on_capitulation`, `FROM = { save_event_target_as = zin_realm_conqueror }` then `zin_realm_found_exile_court = yes`.

### `zin_realm_restore_exiles`

- **Purpose:** the end of a court in exile (G9).
- **Scope:** the shelter (country).
- **Inputs:** `zin_arg_way` (1 release the fallen realm free, 2 release it as a sworn realm, 3 keep its land and let the court disperse, 4 the court goes home to a realm that still stands) and event target `zin_realm_fallen`.
- **Side effects:** `release` through a `meta_effect` on the fallen realm's tag; the Golden King's restoration sets `zin_golden_king_restored`; removes `zin_realm_exiled_court` and the court records on both sides.
- **Example:** `set_temp_variable = { zin_arg_way = 1 }` then `zin_realm_restore_exiles = yes`.

### `zin_realm_seat_heir`

- **Purpose:** the waiting named heir of THIS realm takes the crown (owner answer Q-87, K59). A child (younger than `constant:zin_realm.adult_age` by `zin_realm_heir_born` against `global.year`) reigns through the timed idea `zin_realm_regency` for the years until grown, clamped to two to five years; an heir whose birth and span are known (`zin_realm_heir_born`, `zin_realm_heir_life`) takes his vigour from his own years and marks it with `zin_realm_vigour_given`.
- **Scope:** the realm (country); the old ruler is already gone (`kill_country_leader`).
- **Inputs:** the country variables `zin_realm_heir_born`, `zin_realm_heir_life`, `zin_ruler_rate`. **Outputs:** `zin_realm_succession_way` (1 grown, 2 a regency), `zin_ruler_vigour`; clears `zin_realm_heir_born` and the flag `zin_realm_heir_disputed`.
- **Side effects:** fires no event; `zin_realm_announce_heir` tells the realm (`zin_realm.10` or `.11`).
- **Example:** `kill_country_leader = yes` then `zin_realm_seat_heir = yes` and `zin_realm_announce_heir = yes`.

### `zin_realm_remove_heir`

- **Purpose:** the named heir of THIS realm leaves the succession (passed over by `zin_realm.16`, or dead before the ruler by `zin_realm_check_heir`): he is retired, so no heir can be determined until the decision Name an Heir names a kinsman.
- **Scope:** the realm (country). **Inputs:** none. **Outputs:** clears `zin_realm_heir_born` and `zin_realm_heir_disputed`.
- **Example:** `zin_realm_remove_heir = yes`.

### `zin_hears_in_<land>` and `zin_realm_hears_of` (scripted triggers, `tools/content/modules/news_reach.mjs`, `common/scripted_triggers/zin_news_reach.txt`)

- **Purpose:** the realms that would hear of a moment (owner decision K61: most things remain unheard). `zin_hears_in_<land>` is true for a realm of that land (the realm-life land flag `zin_realm_land_<land>`) or a realm that borders one; there is one for each of the thirteen lands of the roster. `zin_realm_hears_of` is true for ROOT itself, its neighbours, the realms of its land, its enemies, its leaguemates, its overlord and its sworn realms.
- **Scope:** the realm that would hear (THIS). `zin_realm_hears_of` reads ROOT as the realm where the moment happened, which is what `show_major` gives (the original recipient; Kaiserreich precedent `show_major = { is_in_faction_with = ROOT }`).
- **Inputs:** none (the land flags are set on startup by realm life). **Outputs:** none.
- **Use:** a news event of the story that is not world news lists its lands and involved realms in `tools/content/data/news_reach.json`; the builder gives it `show_major = { OR = { zin_hears_in_<land> = yes ... } }`. A new major news event that the data does not classify stops the build.
- **Example:** `show_major = { zin_realm_hears_of = yes }` on a news event fired in the scope of the realm where it happened, or `limit = { zin_hears_in_west_lands = yes }` for a minor report event sent with `every_country`.

## Start variety (`tools/content/modules/start_variety.mjs`, `common/scripted_effects/zin_start_variety.txt`)

### `zin_variety_add_temper`

- **Purpose:** gives the ruler of a realm one of the twenty tempers (`zin_trait_temper_<key>`), drawn by weight (owner answer Q-91, K59): the common tempers weigh 10, the uncommon 4, the rare 2 and the rarest 1 (`tools/content/data/start_variety.json`, `weight` and `V4.weightDefault`).
- **Scope:** the realm (country); the trait goes to its sitting ruler.
- **Inputs:** none.
- **Callers:** the start variety's V4 and `zin_variety_temper_new_ruler`, which realm life calls for every new ruler of an AI realm (of any realm under FULL, owner answer Q-92) while the start variety is on (`zin_start_variety_drawn`), with a chance of `constant:zin_variety.temper_chance` in a hundred.
- **Example:** `zin_variety_add_temper = yes`.

## World magic (`tools/content/modules/world_magic.mjs`, `common/scripted_effects/zin_world_magic.txt`)

Spec: [04_magic_and_world_systems.md](specs/03_gameplay/04_magic_and_world_systems.md).

### `zin_world_give_dragon`

- **Purpose:** takes one dragon from the pool of Dragon Mountain (`global.zin_dragons_free`, which never rises) and hands it to warfare's `zin_war_grant_dragons` in the receiving realm.
- **Scope:** any.
- **Input:** `zin_arg_to`, the receiving country (a country scope stored in the temporary variable).
- **Output:** the pool loses one; the receiver gets one dragon flight through `zin_war_grant_dragons` (argument `zin_arg_amount = 1`).
- **Default:** does nothing when the pool is empty; the caller still counts its own Pact or binding (`zin_world_dragon_pacts`, `zin_world_corrupted_dragons`).
- **Callers:** the decisions The Dragon Pact and Bound by Corruption, and the elves' `zin_elves_focus_dragon_pact` (Wings Over the Woods, Dragons of the March).
- **Example:** `set_temp_variable = { zin_arg_to = ROOT }` then `zin_world_give_dragon = yes`.

### `zin_world_raise_varners_wards` and `zin_world_grant_new_gods_gift`

- **Purpose:** the two one-time interventions: Varner's wards (90 days, potent magic, the shelter and `zin_world.40` for the host's allies at war with Zol's Mistake) and the New Gods' gift (365 days, only while Eindil himself bears Glo).
- **Scope:** any; both act on `global.zin_eindil_host`, else on the calling country.
- **Inputs:** none.
- **Once:** `zin_varner_wards_raised` and `zin_world_gods_gift_given`; a second call changes nothing.
- **Example:** `zin_world_raise_varners_wards = yes` (the journey's death of Varner).

## Story AI (`tools/content/modules/story_ai.mjs`)

### `zin_ui_refresh` (`common/scripted_effects/zin_ui.txt`)

- **Purpose:** step 10 of the world tick: raises the panels' dirty tick (`zin_ui_tick`, country and global) and recomputes the panel of every mechanic the country plays (the Golden Court, the Hidden Council, the Presence, the Narrow Strait, the Southern Dominion and Mirolon's watch), each behind its owner's trigger. Pure formatting: it changes no mechanic variable.
- **Scope:** a human player's country (the world tick calls it in `every_country = { limit = { is_ai = no } }`); safe from any panel.
- **Inputs:** none.
- **Example:** `zin_ui_refresh = yes`.

## Development of a realm (`tools/content/lib/tech_development.mjs`, `common/scripted_effects/zin_development.txt`)

Spec: [07_industry_and_electronics_techs.md](specs/03_gameplay/07_industry_and_electronics_techs.md) section 3.6 (owner decision K58). A realm's development tier is the country variable `zin_dev_tier` (1 learned, 2 settled, 3 of custom; the triggers `zin_development_learned`, `zin_development_settled` and `zin_development_customary` read it), and its learning toward the next tier is `zin_dev_progress`. The tuning values are the script constants `zin_development.<tier>_cost`, `zin_development.step` and `zin_development.learn_points` in `common/script_constants/zin_development.txt`, written from the `development` block of `tools/content/data/technologies.json`.

### `zin_development_init`

- **Purpose:** gives a country its start tier once: of custom or settled when its tag is in the builder's list for that tier (from its start package), learned otherwise; then applies the tier. A country without a tier (Zol's Mistake, whose package has the tier "none") is skipped.
- **Scope:** country. Called by `on_startup` for every country (`common/on_actions/zin_technologies.txt`).
- **Inputs:** none. **Outputs:** `zin_dev_tier`, `zin_dev_progress` (0) and the dynamic modifier `zin_development`.
- **Side effects:** none when the country already has `zin_dev_tier`.
- **Example:** `zin_development_init = yes`.

### `zin_development_apply`

- **Purpose:** sets `zin_dev_advanced_cost` to the script constant of the country's tier, adds the dynamic modifier `zin_development` (which reads it as `cat_zin_advanced_cost_factor`) if missing, and forces the modifier to update.
- **Scope:** country with `zin_dev_tier`.
- **Inputs:** `zin_dev_tier`. **Outputs:** `zin_dev_advanced_cost`.
- **Example:** after changing `zin_dev_tier` by script, `zin_development_apply = yes`.

### `zin_development_learn`

- **Purpose:** one point of learning for a country below the learned tier; every `zin_development.step` points lift the tier by one and reapply it. Shows the tooltips `zin_development_learn_tt` and, on a rise, `zin_development_rise_tt`.
- **Scope:** country. Called by the research of Libraries, Universities, Scholars' Conclaves and Royal Academies (`on_research_complete`, with `on_research_complete_limit = { check_variable = { zin_dev_tier > 1 } }`) and by the decisions `zin_dev_send_scholars` and `zin_dev_endow_learning`.
- **Inputs:** none (the temporary variable `zin_dev_step` is internal). **Outputs:** `zin_dev_progress`, and `zin_dev_tier` on a rise.
- **Side effects:** nothing for a learned country or a country without a tier.
- **Example:** in a focus reward or an event option: `zin_development_learn = yes`.

## The deep past (`tools/content/modules/deep_past.mjs`, `common/scripted_effects/zin_deep_past.txt`, `common/scripted_triggers/zin_deep_past.txt`)

The six fallen realms of owner decision K64 ([docs/specs/04_mechanics/deep_past.md](specs/04_mechanics/deep_past.md)). The single-purpose effects the focus trees call (`zin_deep_cords_counted`, `zin_deep_read_first_stair`, `zin_deep_read_door` and the others of the spec's section 3) open a loop or record a choice and take no argument; the reusable ones are below.

### `zin_deep_share`

- **Purpose:** a keeper shares one of the Low Country's keepings with the realm that asked: the shared flag, `zin_opinion_keeping_given` both ways, the category opened for the asker, and the Two Counts checked.
- **Scope:** the keeper (ROOT of the answer event), with FROM the asker. **Inputs:** temporary `zin_arg_keeping` 1 the Cord, 2 the Stone, 3 the Tide. **Outputs:** `zin_deep_shared_cord`, `_stone` or `_tide` on FROM.
- **Example:** `set_temp_variable = { zin_arg_keeping = 2 } zin_deep_share = yes clear_variable = zin_arg_keeping`.

### `zin_deep_check_two_counts`

- **Purpose:** fires the Two Counts (`zin_deep.30`) for a realm that holds the Cord and the Stone, has not charted the low fields and lives in a game where the giants did not keep the silence.
- **Scope:** the realm. **Inputs:** none. **Side effects:** the event charts the low fields (`zin_deep_chart_low_country`).

### `zin_deep_diadem_fate`

- **Purpose:** the fate of the Kalioth diadem, called by Erco's three focuses and by `zin_deep.101` for any other owner of the Kalioth state.
- **Scope:** the realm deciding. **Inputs:** temporary `zin_arg_way` 1 walled up again, 2 melted, 3 worn (cleared after reading). **Outputs:** `zin_deep_diadem_decided`; way 1 `zin_deep_diadem_walled` and Calm; way 2 `zin_deep_diadem_melted`, the Mine-Lords' Oath, The Kalioth Forge recovered, Alerhia's goodwill; way 3 `zin_deep_diadem_worn`, `global.zin_deep_diadem_wearer`, the relic, claims (never cores) on the old Kalioth lands, `zin_opinion_old_tribute`, the coalition's weight.
- **Example:** `set_temp_variable = { zin_arg_way = 2 } zin_deep_diadem_fate = yes` (the focus compiler writes it from `{ "call": "zin_deep_diadem_fate", "args": { "way": 2 } }`).

### `zin_deep_moot_answer`, `zin_deep_cast_vote`, `zin_deep_crown_answer`

- **Purpose:** tally the answers of a council of realms: the Road Moot (three holds come or stay away), the Deep King's vote (four votes, the caller or the greatest hold), the crown's route of the Sea-Roads (Raerby and Seite agree or refuse). The last answer settles the outcome and tells the realms involved.
- **Scope:** the answering realm. **Inputs:** temporary `zin_arg_came`, `zin_arg_caller` or `zin_arg_aye` (1 yes, 0 no). **Outputs:** the global counters `zin_deep_moot_answers`/`_came`, `zin_deep_votes_cast`/`_caller`, `zin_deep_crown_votes`/`_ayes`, and the outcome flags.

### `zin_deep_coalition_weight`

- **Purpose:** the deep past's part of the eastern coalition weight (eastern spec section 5): the treaty of passage, the First Stair read under Dominion or the rift whisper, the Kalioth diadem worn.
- **Scope:** an East Lands court inside `zin_east_compute_weight`. **Inputs:** none. **Outputs:** adds to the caller's temporary `zin_e_w` (the constants `coalition_treaty`, `coalition_stair`, `coalition_diadem`).

### `zin_deep_crossing_banks`

- **Purpose:** the low fields under the Crown Sea ground a share of the Western Crossing's first sailing for Aenor Isle when the defenders have charted them and no island pilots sail with the host.
- **Scope:** the crossing's leader inside `zin_west_sail`, which calls it only when the landing is the isle. **Inputs:** the caller's temporary `west_lift`. **Outputs:** lowers `west_lift` by `banks_share` and the crossing fleet by `banks_fleet` (`zin_west_add_fleet`); `zin_deep.45` to the leader.

### `zin_deep_tide_window`, `zin_deep_long_light` (scripted triggers)

- **Purpose:** `zin_deep_tide_window` is true in the months of the great tides (the constants `dive_month_a` and `dive_month_b`), read through `meta_trigger` with the year of the date (the calendar module's pattern); `zin_deep_long_light` is true after a mild Cold Lands winter until the next winter season opens.
- **Scope:** any. **Example:** `custom_trigger_tooltip = { tooltip = zin_deep_tt_tide_window zin_deep_tide_window = yes }`.

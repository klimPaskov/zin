# Sex rebalance handoff (K54, Q-104)

Owner decision K54 and the answer to Q-104 (25 October 2026): about one woman in a hundred characters, so about ten women of roughly 1,030 characters in total. Only canon women of the lore vault and owner decisions, plus women whose reign the histories make a defining fact, stay women. Every other woman became a man: a male name from the realm language, a rewritten title, bio and pronouns, and updated references.

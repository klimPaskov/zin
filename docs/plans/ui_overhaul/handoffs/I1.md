# Lane I1: technology icons (handoff)

## Made

- 279 technology medallions, one per researchable technology in the K51 tree (`GFX_<key>_medium`, `gfx/interface/technologies/zin/<key>.dds`, 64 x 64, round alpha). Each has its own picture; no technology shares a texture.
- 23 sheets generated with gpt-6-luna through `tools/content/imagegen.mjs` (22 sheets of 4 x 3 and one of 5 x 3). Raw sheets in the art store `raw/technologies/technology-01..23.png`; accepted copies in `art/source/technologies/`. Every generation and review is in `art/source/provenance.jsonl` and the art store's `jobs/provenance.jsonl`.
- Every sheet was cut with the builder's own cut code and reviewed at 3x and 1x: each medallion matches its stated subject and era, with no lettering and no modern objects.
- A transparent 64 x 64 `gfx/interface/technologies/zin/blank.dds` backs `GFX_zin_technology_blank`. It is an empty slot, not an icon.

- `art/source/technologies/*.png` and `art/source/provenance.jsonl` (review and generation lines).

Subjects were written from `docs/plans/zin-art/tech_icon_list.json`; the subject file lives in the scratchpad and is not committed.

## Size decision

Every technology icon is 64 x 64, following the accepted design system (§4.2, `iconFamilies.technology`) and check 12. The large equipment tiles (K51) keep the 64 x 64 picture centred in the 183 x 84 item. Vanilla equipment technology pictures are 131 to 146 px wide, so a wide family would need a separate family in `design_system.json` and `icon_registry.mjs`. That is a lead decision; not done here.

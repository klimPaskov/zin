## Sound hooks (not this lane: the super-event audio lane)

Every moment keeps its song entry: `music/zin_super_events.asset` names `music/zin_se_<key>.ogg` for each of the 31 keys (`name = zin_se_<key>`, volume 0.8), played by `zin_super_event_play` with `scoped_play_song` and silenced by the player's `zin_se_music_off`; the briefs are in `docs/plans/super_events/audio_briefs.json` and the pending rows SA02 to SA32 in `docs/plans/zin-art/pending_from_integration.md`. The window also keeps `show_sound = event_popup` (vanilla's event sound) when it opens. Nothing ships under these names yet, so a moment plays no music until the audio lane delivers licensed recordings.

## Quotes

All 31 moments carry an in-world quote and attribution. By owner decision K52 (spec §7) the quotes are original lines of Aoaz, never real-world quotes, so none is missing and none goes to the quote researcher.

## Left vanilla, and why

Nothing of vanilla is drawn by this window. It uses the engine's `GFX_zin_button` (C3's neutral button) for the music toggle and vanilla's `event_popup` sound.

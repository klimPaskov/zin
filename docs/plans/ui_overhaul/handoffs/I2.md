# Lane I2: equipment pictures and the equipment designer (handoff)

## Made

- 151 equipment pictures at 146 x 54, one per concept: 35 archetypes (31 equipment archetypes and the four naval hulls) and 116 equipment types (106 land, air and logistics variants and 10 hull variants). Textures: archetypes `gfx/interface/archetypes/zin/<archetype>.dds`, types `gfx/interface/equipment/zin/<variant>.dds`, one frame each.
- 12 equipment designer pictures: four hull sides (`gfx/interface/equipmentdesigner/zin/zin_hull_<class>_designer.dds`, 512 x 260) and four category emblems with their selected states (`slot_zin_<category>.dds` and `slot_zin_<category>_highlight.dds`, 47 x 76).
- 14 generated sheets with gpt-6-luna through `tools/content/imagegen.mjs`: 3 archetype sheets, 10 type sheets (the last with 8 pictures) and one designer sheet. Raw sheets in the art store `raw/equipment/`; accepted copies in `art/source/equipment/`.
- Every sheet was reviewed at full size against its listed subjects, in grid order. The cut was checked on a 2x contact sheet of all 151 pictures (every picture whole). The designer sheet was checked at full size and its cut sizes printed.

## Files changed

- `tools/content/modules/equipment_art.mjs` (new): finds one picture per grid cell (a cell's components are unioned, so a gun and its match stay together and a picture that crosses a cell line stays whole), keys the black ground to alpha, contains each picture in its canvas (146 x 54 for equipment, the per-key size for designer pictures), writes DDS, the staged accepted sheets and `art/source/equipment_art_manifest.json`. Exports `shippedEquipment`, the keys shipped by the last run. Asserts completeness when the cell map says `complete`.
- `tools/content/data/equipment_art_cells.json` (new): 14 sheets with layouts, kinds, per-key sizes for the designer sheet; `complete: true`.
- `tools/content/modules/warfare.mjs`: imports the module, runs it in `buildWarfare` (before forces), and `writeSprites` writes `interface/zin_equipment.gfx` for all 151 concepts.
- `tools/content/modules/forces.mjs`: imports `shippedEquipment`; the designer sprites in `designerGfx` point at their DDS when shipped, else the ZIN unknown frame. The old check against the live mod folder is gone.
- `art/source/equipment/*.png`, `art/source/provenance.jsonl`: generation and review lines per accepted sheet; accepted:false lines for the five rejected magenta sheets (reason written in each line). The art store `jobs/provenance.jsonl` has the matching lines (appended only).
- `docs/plans/zin-lead/TRACKER.md`: production row updated for I2.
- Not touched: `technologies.mjs`, `technology_art.mjs`, `ui.mjs`, other lanes' files.

## Duplicate-sprite question (convoy, train, motorized)

- The seven names (`GFX_motorized_equipment_0..3`, `GFX_train_equipment_0..1`, `GFX_convoy_1`) also exist in vanilla `interface/Technologies.gfx`. ZIN now defines them.
- Same-name redefinition is already the mod's pattern: `frontendmainviewbg.gfx` (GFX_frontend_bg), `frontendmainviewbg_basic.gfx` (GFX_frontend_bg_basic, GFX_frontend_bg_basic_small) and `small_background.gfx` (GFX_load_1_small to GFX_load_4_small) redefine vanilla sprite names on purpose.
- Renaming is not an option: the engine finds equipment pictures by the equipment type name (`GFX_<type>_medium`), and the type names are fixed by warfare.
- Linux check needed: whether the duplicate definition logs a line in `error.log`. If it does and the owner rejects it, the route is a shared override file, which needs the lead.

## Rejected sheets (logged, not used)

## The content

**Bookmark.** "The Fourth Age", 1 Thaw 4A 1748 (`START_DATE`), default Aenor, `filters = { label }` with `label_order`, `scrollable_country_list = yes`, `include_majors_in_minor_list = no`. Description: the spec section 3 text with the north's hint changed to the black ships among the isles (the rumour Zol's Mistake's own history text records), plus a second paragraph for player start variety (K59): every realm is playable and "The Shape of the Age" may change a ruler's temper, an old feud or a disputed valley. The spec's Chronicle sentence was left out because this lane did not verify that entry point.

**Great row (8):** Aenor, Lerendor, the Magical Forest, the Worshipdom, Mirolon, Goldoroth, Puvulan (Volgan), Zol's Mistake. They span the four ideology groups, men, elves, orcs and the dead, sea, court, temple, watch and horde starts. Yeldenne, in the spec's row, is retired; Goldoroth replaces it. Three focuses each, chosen from the tree roots (for example Aenor: The Golden Court, The Middle Kingdom Concord, The Temples of the Deliverer; Zol's Mistake: The Silence of the North, The Black Islands, The Dead of Zol).

**List (77):** the other five majors (Coldor, Noris, Stabilo, Gala, Gunwyn, with chosen focuses) and every realm with a regional tree (72; first three focuses by row and column whose names are plain text), ordered by land then name, each with `minor = yes` and its land label.

**Descriptions (85):** each realm's `today` text from `data/world/history/<region>.json` (its situation in 4A 1748), trimmed by whole sentences, then "Founded <date>. Banner: <sign.device>" (the K68 banner). At most 600 characters. Two overrides: Puvulan drops the clause naming the King of Demons, and the Nether gets its own text for the same reason. Spirits: up to three of the start spirits in the order culture, faith, second culture, world, land, people (Zol's Mistake starts with two).

**Land filters (10):** Middle Lands, North Lands, Cold Lands, West Lands, East Lands, Dark Lands, Ember Lands (with the one South Lands realm), Lands of the Sun, Magical Lands, The Isles (Triton's Islands and the Islands of the South). Narrow 104 px tabs with 3 px spacing so the ten fit the 1181 px row.

**Difficulty (spec section 5):** Burgher, Pilgrim, Man-at-arms, Veteran, Champion, with the five ZIN tooltip lines; `FE_DIFFICULTY_HARD` is not replaced because the vanilla label already reads Veteran and check C5 forbids copying a vanilla value (`localisation/replace/zin_frontend_replace_l_english.yml`; the text sweep leaves `FE_DIFFICULTY_*` to `frontend.mjs`).

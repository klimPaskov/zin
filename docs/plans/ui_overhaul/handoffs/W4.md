- `tools/content/art/w4-icons-jobs.mjs` (new): the image jobs (art store `jobs/ui-w4-icons-jobs.json`).

### 4. Materials: the intelligence windows (Chancery)

The Banner Hall windows show one material since C1 (woad wool and gold cord), so they get no override. The intelligence agency drew Chancery walnut at the top over a Banner Hall woad body with a vanilla paper column (three materials). `tools/content/modules/ui_military.mjs` (new, called from `build.mjs` right after `buildChancery`) writes `interface/countryintelligenceagencyview.gui` and `interface/countryintelledger.gui` as the vanilla files with sprite names swapped to lane W2's Chancery sprites (W2's swap table, imported, plus four bodies in `tools/content/data/ui/military_ui.json`: the paper bodies to `GFX_zin_cha_reading`, the insignia window to `GFX_zin_cha_section`). The two section titles that sit on the dark Chancery band ("Branches of the Court", "Agents") were the typewriter reading face, dark ink, on that band (check 19 contrast); they are set in the label face `hoi_16mbs` with `text_color_code = "H"`. Every vanilla element name is kept in order (tested). Both files are on the closed list (`OVERRIDE_FILES`).

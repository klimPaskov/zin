## Second session (1 November): what was finished

1. **Header provenance.** The 30 header lines the first session restored were no longer in either log (both had been rewritten again since). The ten bands were checked to be exact recuts of the raw pictures (`sharp extract` with the `band.top` of `data/ui/zin_headers.json` reproduces every tracked band byte for byte) and the job prompts equal the current spec prompts. Appended to both `art/source/provenance.jsonl` and `<ZIN_ART>/jobs/provenance.jsonl` (append only, 30 lines each): per header the imagegen generation line of the raw picture (`ui-header-<id>`, `restored: true`, sha256 of the stored picture, its file time), a band line (`ui-header-<id>-band`: the generation's prompt, the band's own sha256, `kind: band`, `from` and `from_sha256` of the raw picture, the crop operation), and an accepted review line on the band's sha256. The asset check finds a generation for a tracked file by sha256 or by path, and the raw path (`ui/headers/ui-header-<id>.png`) differs from the band's (`ui/headers/<id>.png`), so the band line is what links each band; the asset gate now reports no finding on `art/source/ui/headers/*` or `gfx/interface/zin/headers/*`. The review looked at every band again: no figure, face, lettering, crown, sun disc or interface element (the grey drape in the council band is a curtain in a doorway). The council band keeps a faint horizontal seam in its floor about two thirds down (the edge of the model's own padding under its painted strip); every band that drops it shows the setting sun, so it stays, recorded in its review line.
2. **Launchers under the Aoaz bar.** The Realm panel passes `noLauncher: AOAZ_BUILT` (the realm part runs after the bar, so the Realm button window is no longer written). The shared `Panel` takes `bar: '<key of AOAZ_PANELS>'`: such a panel writes no launcher window, launcher script or launcher texts and is recorded in `BAR_PANELS`; after the bar is built, the realm part of `buildUi` runs `missingBarOpenings` on the staged `common/scripted_guis/zin_aoaz_bar.txt` and stops the build when the bar lacks a `zin_aoaz_panel_<key>_click` that sets the panel's flag (so no panel can lose its only way in). Court, Council, Presence, Dominion, Watch, Strait and Accord pass `bar`; each one's launcher visibility equals the trigger of the bar row that opens it (`aoaz_bar.json` rows in `modules/ui.mjs`), and no country matches an earlier row as well.
3. **The Horde keeps its launcher.** The guard found that the bar never builds its Goldoroth row: `buildAoazBar` reads Goldoroth's tag from `common/scripted_guis/zin_horde.txt` (`modules/ui.mjs`, Aoaz-bar part, line 352), but the Horde's scripted GUI is in `common/scripted_guis/zin_dark_panels.txt`, so `gol` is always null and the bar has neither the Goldoroth meters nor the Horde button. Not this lane's code, so the Horde launcher stays, moved to x 8, y 124, just below the bar (it was at y 116, under the bar's last 4 px). Fix for the bar's owner: read `zin_dark_panels.txt` (its only `original_tag = <tag>` is the Horde's), then pass `bar: 'horde'` in `modules/mech_dark_panels.mjs` and drop the y 124 launcher (the test in `mech_dark.test.mjs` then expects 2 scripted GUIs and no launcher).
4. **The accent strip.** The renders showed each mechanic's accent strip only 128 px wide at the header's left: the accent textures are 128 × 4 and a plain sprite as a container background is drawn at its own size. The strip is now five 128 px icon tiles in the 600 × 4 container (clipping on, the transparent clip background of the meter wells), so it spans the header.
5. **Realm height 504.** Rendered at 1280, 1920, 2560 and long text: header, emblem, three-line people description, alignment, rule and the centred footer fit with room above the frame; no overflow, no clipping.
6. `review_windows.json`: `z_realm_btn` replaced by `z_aoaz_bar` (the bar in the Aenor court row with its Golden Court button); `z_court_btn` removed (that window is gone).

The text key error the lead saw on 31 October (`zin_horde_sway_row`) came from a moment when `data/mech_regions_dark.json` was briefly reset to HEAD during this lane's edit; the current files read only `zin_horde_sway_row_tt`, which exists.

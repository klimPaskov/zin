## Simplifications, omissions, and blockers

1. **Allowlist format (blocker for the lead).** hoi4-imagegen 0.2.1 has three allowlist keys: `aliases` (sprite or texture with families, for reuse findings), `no_shine` and `missing_textures`. `GFX-CARD-CORNERS` and `GFX-SPRITE-UNDEFINED` cannot be allowlisted. I did not add entries that do nothing. The 120 + 6 findings stay in the audit. Options: a fixed hoi4-imagegen release, or a ZIN-side note in the handoff.
2. **Idea seals reused, not regenerated (4).** `chain_broken`, `handshake_oath`, `banner_league` and `scroll_sealed` reuse their I11 seals, so the 56 pictures produce 52 new seals.
3. **One seal per picture, not per decision.** Several decisions share one icon (for example `envoy` is used by 8 decisions), so each shared picture has one seal, briefed from the picture's concept.
4. **Only the staged build shows the idea seals.** The repository gfx and common/decisions still hold the idea icons until the lead applies the staged output.
5. **Build summary.** `build.mjs` still prints the I11 decision line. The idea seal counts are verified in the staged tree (52 declared, 171 DDS, 0 idea icons left) but not printed. A later lane can add one console line.
6. **Achievement review is at 64 px.** No in-game check; the lead owns in-game verification.
7. **Review reviewer.** The approvals are recorded under `claude-i12-review` (hoi4-imagegen approval ids, copied to the provenance lines as `approval`).

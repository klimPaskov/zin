## Open

1. **Exact duplicates of controls.** Fifteen groups of controls that vanilla draws as different files come out byte-identical from one control cell at one size (add one, five and ten; the colour variants of checkboxes; two close buttons). They were already accepted in `docs/design/validation/asset_baseline.json` as needing their own art (rows of `docs/plans/zin-art/pending_from_integration.md`, P0033 to P0287); C1 updated only their keys and messages to the new bytes, with the same reasons and rows.
2. **Theme conflicts (phase 2).** 110 C1 textures are drawn by windows of more than one theme (`renders/C1/theme_conflicts.json`, votes per theme). A replaced texture has one path, so it can take one theme; after weighting the main windows, the remaining main-window mismatches are the peace conference (8 shared textures, Banner Hall), the event window (`tiled_window_transparent.dds`), the Gathering Dark history (`tiled_plain_bg.dds`) and `header_bg.dds` in five Banner Hall views (Chancery). They need a themed sprite per window through the .gui overrides K69 allows (lanes W2 to W5).
3. **Luminance.** 66 textures stay pending because no cell lands in their class: mostly the vanilla glows (light or mid class under a soft alpha; the glow cells are measured dark), shape-masked markers and a few strips. They keep vanilla art, as before.
4. **Seams.** 50 tiled bodies still step above 0.02 at the check's join: the woven and grained bodies differ column to column by about that much. The check's body box caps the border at a third of the side (63 for a 190-px tile with border 64), one pixel off the engine's join; the fit matches both joins as well as it can.
5. **Tiny controls without states.** Nine strips still have near-identical frames (two-frame neutral plates and bezels with one cell of their class; iron buttons whose pressed face is 2.9% darker).
6. **Selected tab in the Chancery** is a raised walnut tab with a bright rim, not the design system's vellum tab (§1.3): vanilla draws light text on tabs, so a light tab would fail the luminance class. The same holds for the Banner Hall meter groove (dark woad, not linen).
7. **Asset baseline.** The asset check now reports 101 baseline findings fixed (mostly old chrome duplicates and copies); the baseline can be regenerated by the lead (`ZIN_ASSETS_UPDATE_BASELINE=1`), which C1 did not do because other lanes' rows are in the same file.

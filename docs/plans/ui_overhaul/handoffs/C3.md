## Simplifications, omissions and blockers

- One fallback per module (the one-line rule): the equipment sprites in `warfare.mjs` fall back to the unit unknown icon, and the doctrine sprites written by `technologies.mjs` fall back to the technology unknown icon (`doctrine_art.mjs`'s own constant points at the doctrine one). Both are reported by check 11; lanes I1 and I2 may split them.
- The dividers are resized along their length (up to 1.58) to keep the 520 px layouts; a 520 px cell would need a new generation.
- The old sheets `art/source/ui/ui-frame.png`, `ui-button.png`, `ui-rule.png` are no longer read; they stay tracked (deleting tracked art is the lead's call).
- No mechanic header art (600 × 96) was made: it is per mechanic and belongs to W7 and the art lanes; this lane made the accent strips only.
- The build of the shared tree failed for a while on lane C1's in-progress asset errors (101 new `UNIQ_EXACT_DUP` and `PROV_REF_FOREIGN` findings on C1 files); my preview builds accepted those in a scratch copy of the asset baseline (never in the tracked one). The final verify ran on the plain tree and passed the build (section Verify).

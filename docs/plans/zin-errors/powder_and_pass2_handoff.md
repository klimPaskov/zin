## 1. Black powder (owner decision K19)

Implemented; design proposed. Spec: `docs/specs/03_gameplay/01_warfare.md` section 11.8 (new) and open decision 4 (now "implemented").

- **Access.** The roster's 16 `gunpowder` realms carry the country flag `zin_gunpowder` in their history (`modules/countries.mjs`; `lib/roster.mjs` `GUNPOWDER_FLAG`). It is the only gate: `can_be_produced` of both archetypes (`flag:` producers in `modules/warfare.mjs`; an engine trigger, so it loads), the allow_branch of the two technology roots (a new who clause `flag:<country flag>` in `modules/technologies.mjs` and `lib/tech_layout.mjs`, group `powder`, the flag listed in `technologies_ids.json` countryFlags), and the AI's powder production.
- **Spread (proposed).** Only through the story and trade: the delving holds' event 2 `powder_offered`, *Pay their price*, sets the flag on the buyer, gives 20 Cast Bronze Bombards (`zin_bombard_equipment_1`, a named variant because a buyer without the technologies has no latest one), a siege research bonus, and `mark_technology_tree_layout_dirty`. Of the buyers only Gunwyn is not already a powder realm. Neither archetype can be licensed or lent (`institutions.json` licences.no).
- **Equipment** (`data/warfare_equipment.json`): `zin_bombard_equipment` (artillery; Iron 2, Stone 1; 7 IC; five variants 1600 to 1764) and `zin_handgun_equipment` (infantry; Iron 1, Timber 1; 0.6 IC; four variants 1650 to 1760). Groups: hand-guns in Smith-Work, bombards in Siege Engines; the institutions workshops (smiths, engineers) list them too.
- **Sub-units** (`data/warfare_units.json`): `zin_men_handgunners` (Hand-Gunners; men, and so the dwarves), `zin_siege_bombard` (Bombard Battery; fort attack +100%) and `zin_support_bombards` (Bombard Company; shares `same_support_type = zin_catapults`), the last two for men, orcs and demons. Models borrow the men's foot model (`warfare_models.json`, own = false).
- **Technologies** (`data/technologies_warfare.json`): the line Black Powder (Engines and Walls, column 12: Black Powder 1600, Cast Bombards 1700, Corned Powder 1749, Culverins 1756, Great Bombards 1764) and the line Hand-Guns (Arms and Armour, column 16: Hand-Guns 1650 needing Black Powder, Matchlocks 1720, Long Matchlocks 1752, Arquebuses 1760). Powder realms start with the first two of each (`technologies_packages.json` assign.gunpowder, read by `startFor`).
- **Balance** (tested in `warfare.test.mjs`): a bombard costs more than a trebuchet and throws a little less soft attack per piece at the start, but pierces more, breaks forts harder and comes a century earlier to the realms that have it; a hand-gun matches a crossbow's soft attack with more piercing, less defence and a higher price.
- **AI.** Start templates: Line Host with two hand-gunners, Siege Park (men) and Siege Band (orcs) with bombards, first in their option lists, so only realms with the technologies use them; the stockpile follows the templates. AI templates: `powder_host` (men infantry), `bombard_park` (men siege), `bombard_band` (orcs siege), each enabled by its technology. Production: `zin_default_production_powder` (flag `zin_gunpowder`) from `forces_ai.json` production.powder. Dolon (demons) has no AI siege role, so it fields bombards only by hand.
- **Text.** Powder text names bombards, hand-guns and black powder (voice spec 2.5). The banned-word rule `bw_modern_industry_030` (guns, gunners) is lifted for the powder keys only by the allowance `al_black_powder`; `lib/banned.mjs` allowanceCovers now takes a key prefix ending in `*`, and the warfare and technology text checks apply the allowances as the text scan does.
- **Stand-ins swapped.** *The Bombard Foundries* casts 20 bombards (was 20 catapults and 10 trebuchets); event 2 sells bombards (`docs/design/focus/shared_delving_holds.json`, spec `shared_delving_holds.md`).
- **Art debt** (`docs/plans/zin-art/pending_from_integration.md`, "Art the integration also needs"): equipment pictures, counters, technology icons, the two 3D models and the catalog focus icons `bombard_casting`, `powder_casks`. All sprites are registered on the ZIN frame; no asset-check error, so no baseline entry is needed.

Linux run must confirm: the powder realms can produce bombards and hand-guns and field the three sub-units; a non-powder realm sees neither line; Gunwyn sees both lines after buying powder (the tree redraw); the start divisions of Kunhrim, Hrakbrund and Aenor carry hand-gunners and bombards.

## 3. Kusven

**Canon (owner, 24 October 2026):** Kusven, formerly Edvoid, is Zol's most loyal servant, a Great Spirit made by Zol, a being of his own. He, not Zol, comes forth in the War of the Last Crowns, breaks Horos's blue sword, is wounded by Eindil with a shard of it and withdraws, not destroyed, when Lorien and ancient spirits die opposing him. Q-29: his Beasts of the black north fight as rare story-granted units with Zol's hosts.

**Implementation (`lib/mech_zol_script.mjs`, `modules/mech_zol.mjs`, `data/mech_zol.json` block `kusven`):**

- Flags `zin_kusven_forth`, `zin_kusven_withdrawn` (only after he came forth), `zin_kusven_wounded`. The contract flags `zin_zol_manifested` and `zin_zol_withdrawn` are still set beside them: about thirty readers in other slices' files (focus designs and registries, the Eindil journey, the crown events, the world events, the elves and Afrit slices, `presentation-cells.json`) still read the old names. `zin_zol_withdrawn` keeps the meaning "Zol's war has ended", set on every route; `zin_zol_manifested` means the same moment as `zin_kusven_forth`. Neither is retired yet.
- Effects `zin_zol_send_kusven` (once in the age): power -25, Convergence 0, idea `zin_zol_kusven_in_field` *Kusven Takes the Field* (attack +30%, defence +20%, morale +30%, breakthrough +20%), `recruit_character = zin_kusven` and `add_field_marshal_role` (skill 6, attack 7, defence 4, planning 3, logistics 3; traits the Volgan slice's `zin_trait_great_spirit_in_arms` and his own `zin_trait_kusven_grave_wind` *The Grave-Wind*: attack +2, army attack and morale +15%), one division of **Kusven's Host** (4 deathless battalions and the warfare slice's great-being company `zin_great_spirit`, "A Great Spirit"), the 63-day mission, Gathering Dark +10, the news `zin_mistake.20`, the sword or the wound for the Host and Gala's question. `zin_kusven_withdraw`: removes the idea, takes away his field marshal role (he is never killed or retired), deletes Kusven's Host, ends the mission, *The Servant Wounded* (730 days), the deathfield, power -20 (floor 10), deletes Zol's divisions outside the Mistake, white peace, news `.24` (Lorien's stand) or `.25` (every other end). `zin_kusven_break_the_sword` (above).
- Triggers `zin_kusven_can_come_forth` (rising, at war, Convergence 100, power 60, not yet come forth) and `zin_kusven_is_forth`; `zin_is_zol` by original tag.
- His time on the field ends when the Presence falls below 20, when the mission times out, or when `num_equipment_in_armies@zin_great_spirit_equipment` is below 1 (his host is gone); the catch-up for a historical AI sends him after Volgan's body breaks or on 1 Glernaen 4A 1758, never after Zol's war has ended.
- Decision `zin_zol_send_kusven_decision` *Send the Servant* (replaces *Come Forth*); the mission keeps its id and reads *The Servant Holds the Field*.
- The character `zin_kusven` (`common/characters/zin_kusven.txt`) has no role at the start and is never a country leader (S4); the Will of Zol stays Zol's emblem leader.
- The Zol tree's focus and spirit texts that had Zol taking a shape (`docs/design/focus/zol.json`: *The Servant Called*, *The Price of the Field*, *The Servant Takes the Field*, *Into the Dark Again*, *Wounded, Not Slain* and its event) now speak of his servant without naming him, because they can be read before he comes forth.
- **Named only after he appears:** Kusven's name is in his character, the in-field and wounded ideas, the mission text and the events gated on `zin_kusven_forth`. The decision and the tree do not name him. Tests hold both rules.

**Not done: the Beasts of the black north (Q-29).** The warfare data has no beast sub-unit, and a new custom unit needs a sub-unit, equipment, counters, a 3D model and sourced sound (CLAUDE.md custom-unit rules), which belong to the warfare owner and the asset pipeline. Raising them as a template of an existing sub-unit would be an unapproved stand-in, so nothing is spawned. Needed: a warfare sub-unit (for example `zin_mistake_beasts`, "Beasts of the Black North", granted equipment), then a `zin_kusven_loose_beasts` effect in `mech_zol` called by `zin_zol_send_kusven` (a few divisions at Kusven's Host's muster state) and removed by `zin_kusven_withdraw`.

**Portrait:** the generic portrait of the dead (`GFX_zin_portrait_the_dead_male_1`) until his own portrait exists; the owner's pick is redesign A (K41; `art/source/design_refs/kusven_v2_a.png`). Request for the portrait worker: `gfx/leaders/zin/kusven.dds` with sprite `GFX_zin_portrait_kusven`, army large slot of `common/characters/zin_kusven.txt` (set `data/mech_zol.json` `kusven.portrait`).

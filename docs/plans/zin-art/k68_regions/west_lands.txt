GNW|S|a black rook carrying a gold barley-ear in its beak on downs green|The rook and the barley-ear are the sign and the clan song's own birds and grain, the rook that led the clans over the neck to the first sown land.
GIR|S|five grey stepping-stones laid across a single blue river band on green|The wardens' stones are the sign and the commonwealth's own line, the stone each warden carried from his ford; the river band is the Grey River that the ford-share is kept on.
WER|S|half of a broken gold torc with its jagged end on black|The half-torc is the sign and the Erg claim's own relic, the half Dyfan pressed into his younger son's hand; a torc is ornament and is drawn as the relic.
LAB|S|a stone granary raised on four mushroom staddle-stones in gold on green|The raised granary on the staddle-stones is the sign and the lords' peace of the common field; the granary is a structure drawn as identity. Flagged: a structure drawn as identity.
THN|S|an olive tree with one grafted bough of a lighter green on warm gold|The Mother Olive is the sign and the lowland's own tree of grafting, the eldest grafted tree the kings are acclaimed under.
DOB|S|a round brown loaf with a single curved score in cream on harvest green|The Famine Bread is the sign and the basin's own loaf; the thumb-print is dropped because a thumb on a loaf reads as a hand, which is barred. Flagged: a round loaf may read as a disc.
BRU|S|a black iron fire-pot hung on a chain with a red fire rising from it, on dusty ochre|The fire-pot is the sign and the Road-Fire, the hold's soul carried out of the Stone Road; a flat flame is allowed where it is the identity. Flagged: a vessel drawn as identity.
GRO|D|a black iron gate-bolt drawn back on rust red|The dwarf's clenched fist is barred, so the Outer Gate's bolt is drawn from the roots: the gate the hold keeps shut for its grudges and opens to a knock. Flagged: a bolt is a tool piece.
SAO|S|a closed ivory book weighted with a black iron ingot on fen green|The weighted book is the sign and the league's own oath, sworn with dwarf iron laid on its cover; the book is a contract object and the ingot is the realm's own iron.
BLD|S|a white swallow-tailed pennon on its staff, seamed by a black stitch, in white and black on grey|The shroud-banner is the sign and the company's own soul, the black seam closing the shroud; the staff is drawn only as the banner's pole. Flagged: a banner drawn as identity.
KRA|S|a hazel tally-stick broken across with its two halves set apart in white on red|The broken tally is the sign and the Unpaid Year's own record of a debt refused; the two halves are the object's identity.
CRG|D|an iron chest with two locks side by side in black on blue over white|The sealed letter is a document and letters are barred, so the chest the unopened letter is kept in, its two locks held by the two consuls, is drawn from the roots. Flagged: a vessel drawn as identity, for the lead to review.
VRN|S|a black iron lantern with a white light on cape red|The iron lantern is the sign and the sea watch's own; the light is the lantern's flame and is allowed where it is the identity. Flagged: a vessel drawn as identity.
WEN|D|an uncut white rope drawn in a single arc across a water-blue band on pale green|The empty barge would be a vessel counted among the ships, so the uncut ferry-rope the wardens swore on is drawn from the roots instead. Flagged: a rope.

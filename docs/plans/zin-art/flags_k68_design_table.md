# Realm flags, K68 design table (31 October 2026)

Status: the generated design table of the 155 realms, written from the K68 heraldry data (`tools/content/heraldry/k68_designs.mjs`, merged by `build_heraldry.mjs --write` into `tools/content/data/world/heraldry.json`). Yeldenne (YEL) is retired and is not generated. Each device follows the realm's adopted sign and roots (K67, K68); the banner shows the device, the culture's field rule and hem (K65), and the colours.

Basis: K kept the K65 device; S drew the sign's own device (its object parts dropped); D derived a new device from the roots. Each row's Why is the realm's identity in one sentence. Objects are drawn only as the realm's direct identity (K68).

## Rulings applied (lead, 31 October 2026)

- Objects: kept as direct identity (Heodwa, Bordon, Woqood, Hrakbrund, Alerhia, Xison, Mina, Buldor, Halia, Luv-zhise, Nolorim, Dondor, Blackton, Skragdurg, Molot's stake as the story's own mark). - Ships, five in all: Nezer's galley (NEZ), the Mother-Keel (SOY), the Returning Ship (NAA), the four-oared boat of the Mid-Lake Moot (DOL), the Keel-Hall hull (POT). Every other vessel sign is a non-ship emblem.
- Small-size readings: Howan's roof with straight-cut ends; Kea's channel divides into two parallel channels; Zol's broken arcs (broken_rings_sigil, open and broken); Drak's broken collar; Heodwa's bands on the horn; Dobbum's oblong loaf; Mostok's scabbard without crossguard; Rhachia's seated clay mother kept.
- Split fields: 15 of 156 realms are split in two (per fess, per pale, per bend, per chevron, quarterly), below the one-third cap; the rest are plain fields or chief, base, pile and bordure divisions. The culture rows were not edited; the reduction is per realm in the K68 rows, so each culture keeps its hem and palette.
- Forbidden devices: no crosses, crescents, stars, real arms, fists or open hands (except the Worshipdom's loaf-hand), no bundles of spears (Fearno keeps one spear), no knotwork, no letters. Fire only as flat flames where it is the identity (Kingson's vigil flame, Burnikit's door flame, Dulundur's four flames, Lens's lamp, Brundahrim's flame removed with the fire-pot).
- Canon guards: the King's seal with one pentagram (NET), the Gate of Nafeher (PUV), the noon-door with the sun (LIG), the broken-ring sigil of Zol (ZOL), the Worshipdom's loaf-hand (WOR). The Lens's lamp was dropped because a canon god's device is the lamp; the gannet stands for the Keepers.

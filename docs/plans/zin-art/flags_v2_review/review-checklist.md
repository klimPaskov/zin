# Review checklist (flags v3, 25 October 2026)

A flag fails if any item holds:
- a literal everyday object (logs, tools, scales, keys, lanterns, coins, cups, chalices, torches, braziers, cairns, bows, spears, harpoons, rolled charts, buoys, pots, sails on spars);
- burning objects or fire of any kind as a device;
- a real flag or arms (three crowns read as Sweden, a band crossing a bar reads as a cross);
- crosses, fleurs-de-lis, stars, crescents, forbidden register;
- crenellated bands, battlements or stepped edges in a hem or field;
- a red ring with a bar on white or blue (roundel);
- a plain disc on a plain or banded field;
- a bundle of rods or spears bound together;
- a raised clenched fist, clasped or shaking hands;
- an interlaced knot or Celtic knotwork;
- equal horizontal bands carrying the device;
- literal botanical drawings (leaves with veins, wreaths, sprigs).
Accepted as the standard: stylised beasts, trees and ships that are the realm's own (Konori, Kilinti, the narwhal, the fir), bold crowns, suns and simple rings that are not roundels.

K65 (owner, 25 October 2026): culture, not region, and handmade.
- Culture, not region: a device that is the region's landscape, animal, plant, or goods (narwhal, fir, waves, sheaf, shell, salt, amber, fur, wool, stone pile) fails unless the record's justification ties it to the realm's own culture or founding (the fifteen justified exceptions).
- Handmade: more than four flat colours plus gold or silver thread fails; painted shading, gradients, fine illustration, scenes, tiny details, photographic texture fail; a device a weaver or embroiderer could not sew, or that does not read from a distance, fails.

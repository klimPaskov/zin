## 1. The project and where it stands

### 1.1 What ZIN is

ZIN is a Hearts of Iron IV 1.19.3 total overhaul. The game starts on **1 Thaw 4A 1748** of the Fourth Age (the month name will change with the twelve-god calendar, section 2.3) in a world at peace under the Golden King of Aenor. The established storyline runs to 4A 1760 (Afrit's rise, Eindil's journey, the War of the Last Crowns, three canonical endings); the sandbox continues to 4A 1800. The roster has **305 countries**; with the 75 dynamic tags the tag total is 380 and must stay below 439 (section 5.2, flag-sheet crash).

The mod is generated, not hand-edited: the content builder `tools/content/build.mjs` stages every game file from modules in `tools/content/modules/` and data in `tools/content/data/`, and `node --import tsx content/apply.mts plan|apply` writes them through the shared atomic rewrite engine. Country histories, states and the map come from the map generator (HOI4 Agent Tools) and the politics step (`C:/Users/klimp/Documents/Projects/hoi4-agent-tools/.tmp/zin-politics-v4.mts`, planned to move to `tools/politics/`). Commands and folders are described in `tools/README.md`.

## 2. Canonical corrections of 24 October 2026

These are owner decisions. They override the vault and every older spec. Each subsection lists the current conflicting material found on 24 October so the implementation can propagate the change.

### 2.1 Exchange Erdae and Aoaz

| Previous name | New canonical name | Identity that stays with that land |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Erdae (labelled **Arda** in some sources: the vault and the bestiary cards) | **Aoaz** | the main setting of the mod: its geography, inhabitants, kingdoms, gods and history |
| Aoaz | **Erdae** | the neighbouring small continent, with its own magic, history and identity |

This is a **name change only**. Nothing physical, historical or cultural moves between the two lands.

Footprint found on 24 October: "Erdae" occurs **1,075 times in 205 tracked files** of the mod outside the vault (localisation, specs, data such as `tools/content/data/geography/erdae.json`, art provenance, docs), plus throughout the vault (`Erdae and Its Regions.md`, `Fourth Age Map of Erdae.md`, `Magic and Sustenance in Erdae.md` ...) and in folder names of the owner's private files (`C:/Users/klimp/Documents/ZIN/World/Erdae/`). "Aoaz" occurs nowhere in text today (only as random bytes in binaries). On the owner's world map (`IMG_20261023_121133.jpg`) two small neighbouring landmasses are labelled **ARDA** (left) and **AOAZ** (right); the bestiary cards use "Arda" for the main setting (the Edvoid card: "tahab endale saada terve Arda", wants to own all of Arda).

Migration requirements are in 02, R-01. In short: a two-phase token swap with an audit so no "Erdae" that already means the small continent is re-renamed; the private originals keep their file names; a traceability table records every renamed file and term with its old name.

### 2.2 The Great Wall of Aenor is a natural mountain range

The Great Wall of Aenor is the enormous **natural** mountain range that separates the Middle Lands from the Magical Lands. Any reading of it as an artificial wall or megastructure is removed.

Conflicting material found:
- Vault, `07 - History/Fourth Age Chronology.md` row 4A 96, "The Wall Accord": Elzon's Order raised a fortified line, the Pale March, kept by treaty and "rebuilt in stone" (proposed).
- Vault, `07 - History/The Campaign of 4A 1748.md`: "proposed origin in Elzon's Pale March".
- Master plan `docs/specs/00_master_overhaul_plan.md`, decision P4 (an impassable range with four to six named gates carrying level-5 forts) and P13 (the one affordable Meshy model is "the Great Wall segment").
- Geography model and map plan acceptance M5 ("a continuous elevation-5 barrier with its gates"); focus trees, mechanics and events that mention gates (check the elves, Aenor and Kingson material).

Fortified passes and watchtowers may exist where history explains them, but the barrier itself is mountains: passes, valleys, snowlines and the routes they allow.

### 2.3 Twelve New Gods name the twelve months

Aoaz has **twelve New Gods**; the number is deliberate. The twelve months are named after them, one god per month. **Glernaen**, god of power and wisdom, is the most powerful New God; keep his place in the hierarchy (He Who Was, Is and Will Be; the Holy Seven; Old Gods; New Gods; Great Spirits; Lesser Spirits: see `01 - Cosmology/Hierarchy of Being.md`).

Current sources: the vault's `01 - Cosmology/New Gods.md` says "their number, identities, and domains are still unwritten" and asks not to invent canon names without a decision; at least some New Gods serve Zol; Volgan once served a New God he later overpowered; **Afrit is publicly recognised as a New God at the end of the Third Age**. `03 - Figures/Glernaen.md` establishes Glernaen and Glo. **The purpose behind the number twelve is not in any source**: the owner must supply it before the pantheon's foundation is written (question Q-01). The current calendar (`tools/content/lib/calendar.mjs`) names the months Deepwinter, Ironfrost, Thaw, Rainwake, Sowing, Highsun, Goldfield, Harvest, Fallowing, Emberleaf, Mistfall, Longnight in ten languages; it must be replaced.

Chronology: a god who became recognised later (Afrit's public deification at the end of the Third Age; any New God created after the calendar began) must be reconciled with the calendar without editing history to make implementation easier (Q-02).

### 2.4 Zol does not appear in the War of the Last Crowns

The war in question is the **War of the Last Crowns** (vault, `07 - History/War of the Last Crowns.md`; flag `zin_war_of_last_crowns`). Zol does not appear personally in it. The being who appears is **Zol's most loyal servant, a Great Spirit**.

Conflicting material found:
- Vault, `War of the Last Crowns.md`, step 5: "Zol appears bodily for a rare moment, overpowers the renewed champion, shatters the blue sword ... Eindil wounds Zol's body with a shard. Lorien and ancient spirits die forcing the Old God to withdraw"; and "Zol's manifestation is temporary and costly".
- Vault, `03 - Figures/Glernaen.md`: "Zol later breaks the sword".
- Shared contract `docs/specs/04_mechanics/00_shared_contract.md`: flags `zin_zol_manifested` and `zin_zol_withdrawn` (4A 1757); the name `zin_zol_manifested` appears in **20 tracked files**, plus the uncommitted Zol mechanic, the world events and the endings.
- Event, focus tree and art material about Zol's bodily appearance (events `03_war_of_the_last_crowns.md`, `04_endings.md`, the Zol tree spec, event pictures).

The servant's identity is open (Q-03). The only candidate in the sources is **Edvoid**: his bestiary card (`IMG_20261023_120131.jpg`, read in full) says, in Estonian, that he lives in "Šodõlõrõõl", is 7,945,640 years old, is an evil spirit who wants to own all of Arda, quietly destroys a city every day to gain power, is feared by all, feeds on souls, and "Ta on Zoli tehtud endine käsilane": **he is a former minion made by Zol**. "Former" conflicts with "most loyal servant", and the card does not call him a Great Spirit. The drawn map also has a region "Ocean of Edvoid". Do not assign the role to Edvoid or anyone else without the owner's answer.

### 2.5 Bastian and the other wizards

**Bastian** is another well-known wizard in the realm of men. He appears in no source checked (vault, specs, card titles). **Varner** is established as a wise wizard and **Lesser Spirit** of the Magical Lands who fought Elzon's necromancy through the Third Age, accompanied by his small purple servant **Golbran**, and dies in the War of the Last Crowns (`03 - Figures/Varner.md`, `Golbran.md`). **Elzon** is a necromancer (vault and the Elzon card). The vault's Peoples page lists "arcanists of the Zhen Hills". Requirements for the wider world of practitioners are in 02, R-34.

### 2.6 Devils

**Devils are corrupted Lesser Spirits who formerly served an Old God and escaped to Aoaz.** Not every devil served Zol.

Related material: vault `04 - Peoples/Peoples of Erdae.md`: demons are corrupted angels (Shachihata, Netherborn serving Volgan, Afrit's demons); Volgan's army contained "demons, devils, monsters, Great Spirits, dragons"; Zol is the only Old God in Aoaz (`Old Gods.md`), so devils who served another Old God came from elsewhere. The Devil card (`IMG_20261023_115319.jpg`, read in full) says devils are huge (20 to 30 m, drawn 25 m), immortal and few; they live in "Pornyioris(?)", Ergos, the Death Islands, the Nether and the Goldoroth Mountains; they fight with strength, claws, teeth, horns, wings and fire; **"many devils serve Vez'nanit"**; and a dead devil can be revived by a ritual that throws its skull into lava. **Vez'nan is an old name of Volgan** (owner, 24 October 2026; section 2.7), so the card means that many devils serve Volgan, which agrees with the vault's account of devils in Volgan's army. Whether "immortal" and "revived" fit the spirit metaphysics is open (Q-07).

## 3. The wider world and its mysteries

The owner's world map (`IMG_20261023_121133.jpg`) shows a much larger world. Aoaz is a small continent; its neighbour, now called Erdae, is also small and has its own magic. The other continents give geographical and cosmological context; they do **not** expand the playable scope.

| Place | Authorial truth (never common knowledge) | What people of Aoaz may know or believe |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Erdae (the neighbouring small continent, formerly called Aoaz) | small, its own magic, history and identity | travellers occasionally arrive from it |
| Haven | much larger land; population and conditions largely unknown | travellers occasionally arrive from it |
| Cerio | much larger land; largely unknown | some may have heard of it; knowledge uncertain |
| Lao | legendary land | traditions place the gods' dwelling and the destination of souls there; interpretations differ |
| Erdu | the largest continent; gods are born and trained there; divine magic works more freely there | **unknown** to the people of Aoaz |
| Dunda | much larger, mostly empty; retains the presence of Zol's creator, one of the Seven, destroyed long ago; darkness and suffering associated with that being remain; Zol serves that being | unknown or at most dark rumour |

Rules: the name and deeper nature of Zol's creator stay mysterious; do not explain the destroyed god's continuing influence by a resurrection, a surviving body or any definitive answer. Other continents may have their own gods, dark lords, peoples and magic; do not copy Aoaz's societies and conflicts onto them. Hidden cosmology must not leak through tooltips, casual dialogue, public histories or convenient exposition. The vault already separates Holy Seven knowledge (most people do not believe they existed) and keeps Afrit's and Eindil's secrets out of public text; follow the same discipline.

## 5. Decisions, lessons and failed approaches

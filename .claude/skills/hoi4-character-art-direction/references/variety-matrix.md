# Anatomy sheet and variety matrix templates

Generic templates. Fill them in the project spec or work manifest, not here.

## Anatomy sheet (one per non-human people)

| Field | Entry |
| --- | --- |
| People or species name | |
| Skull and face structure | brow ridge, cheekbones, muzzle or flat face, forehead slope |
| Jaw and teeth | width, underbite or overbite, tusks, tooth shape and condition |
| Nose | flat, absent, snout, hooked, slits |
| Ears | shape, size, placement, notches |
| Eyes | size, spacing, pupil shape, colour, lid structure |
| Skin or surface | bark, hide, scales, stone, fur, rot; texture scale and weathering |
| Proportions | head to body, limb length, neck, shoulders |
| Posture | stooped, upright, hunched, coiled |
| Hands | finger count, claws, size relative to the face |
| Scale versus an adult man | height or mass multiple, and the in-frame scale cue to use |
| Must NOT look human | the list of human features to forbid in the prompt |
| Owner reference | file path of the owner's drawing, if any |
| Licensed anatomy studies | URLs and licence status, if any |

## Variety matrix (one per pool)

One row per pool member. Columns are the axes; mark each cell with a short value, and compare rows.

| Member | Age | Build | Face shape | Nose | Brow | Scars or blemishes | Teeth | Hair and beard | Expression | Gaze | Wear | Jewellery | Lighting | Dress |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

Rules:

- Cover the whole age range of the pool, young to elderly, unless the spec limits it.
- No two rows share more than the allowed number of identical axes (default three).
- Include at least some members with a visible flaw: asymmetry, broken nose, missing teeth, pockmarks, sunburn, a scar.
- Dress and jewellery follow the people's culture in the project spec, not a shared costume.
- Record the demographic split (sex, skin tone, region) next to the matrix and check it against the spec's ratios.

## Contact sheet review

Build one contact sheet per pool at the real game size (156x210 for leader and commander portraits, 65x67 for small advisor cards). Review all members side by side before any is accepted; replace members that read as the same person, the same generator face, or the same expression.
